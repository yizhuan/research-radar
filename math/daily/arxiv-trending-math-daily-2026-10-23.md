### 1. [Lenses make many points: Pure point spectrum disproves Simon's $L^2$ conjecture](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31637)
- **Abstract:** For every dimension $d\ge2$ and $0\le\sigma<1/3$, the authors construct a smooth bounded nonnegative potential with arbitrarily small weighted $L^2$ size for which $-\Delta+V$ has only pure point spectrum at positive energies.
- **Authors:** Simon Becker, Mengxuan Yang
- **arXiv:** `2610.31637v1` (published 2026-10-22 17:52:23 UTC; categories: `math.SP`, `math-ph`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** An independent math.SP feed posted the paper title alongside another spectral-theory preprint; the result directly challenges a named conjecture and is cross-listed in spectral theory and mathematical physics.
- **Claimed result:** The authors say their lens-type potential disproves Barry Simon's conjecture that the stated weighted-square-integrability condition forces an absolutely continuous component of infinite multiplicity at almost every positive energy.
- **Assumptions and setting:** Schrödinger operators on $\mathbb R^d$, $d\ge2$, with smooth, bounded, nonnegative potentials; the construction covers $0\le\sigma<1/3$ and can make both the supremum norm and the specified weighted integral arbitrarily small.
- **Caveat:** The result does not cover the endpoint $\sigma=1/3$ or sign-changing potentials; the authors disclose LLM assistance in developing the construction.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, 2026-10-23 announcement batch
- **Themes:** Analysis & PDE; Mathematical physics

### 2. [The Critical 2D KPZ Process](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31614)
- **Abstract:** The authors construct a non-Gaussian, distribution-valued critical solution of the two-dimensional KPZ equation as a limit of centered log partition functions for Gaussian directed polymers and through a renormalized Cole–Hopf transform of the critical stochastic heat flow.
- **Authors:** Quentin Berger, Francesco Caravenna, Jeremy Clark, Alexander Dunlap, Yu Gu, et al.
- **arXiv:** `2610.31614v1` (published 2026-10-22 17:40:36 UTC; categories: `math.PR`, `math-ph`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** A claimed critical scaling-limit construction with linked polymer and stochastic-heat-flow formulations, plus reported covariance and small-scale fluctuation results, spans probability and mathematical physics.
- **Claimed result:** The authors identify a critical process that is non-Gaussian, has a log-log covariance blow-up near the spatial diagonal, and exhibits Gaussian fluctuations after averaging on shrinking spatial scales.
- **Assumptions and setting:** The setting is the critical two-dimensional KPZ equation, interpreted through Gaussian directed polymers and a renormalized Cole–Hopf transform of the critical stochastic heat flow.
- **Caveat:** This is an 84-page preprint whose authors describe extensive generative-AI input; the limiting construction and technical arguments require specialist scrutiny.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, 2026-10-23 announcement batch
- **Themes:** Probability & statistics; Mathematical physics

### 3. [Conway's thrackle conjecture: a new upper bound of $\frac43n$](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31589)
- **Abstract:** For every thrackle on $n\ge4$ vertices, the author proves an upper bound of $\lfloor4(n-1)/3\rfloor$ edges, using a finite computer-assisted verification and a discharging argument.
- **Authors:** Dániel Gábor Simon
- **arXiv:** `2610.31589v1` (published 2026-10-22 17:23:50 UTC; categories: `math.CO`, `math.GN`, `math.GT`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** A concrete improvement to the best-known linear bound on a prominent extremal-graph conjecture, with combinatorics and two topology cross-lists; the paper specifies the computational and discharging ingredients.
- **Claimed result:** For finite simple thrackleable graphs on $n\ge4$ vertices, the author proves $m\le\lfloor4(n-1)/3\rfloor$, improving the coefficient in previous general upper bounds.
- **Assumptions and setting:** A thrackle is a plane drawing in which every pair of edges meets exactly once, either at a common endpoint or at a proper crossing; the theorem is for finite simple graphs.
- **Caveat:** The result improves an upper bound but does not prove Conway's conjectured $m\le n$; its argument incorporates a computer-assisted check for small graphs.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, 2026-10-23 announcement batch
- **Themes:** Combinatorics & discrete mathematics; Geometry & topology

### 4. [Aleshin graphs are expanders](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30662)
- **Abstract:** The authors prove that Aleshin level graphs are two-sided expanders, establish linear diameter bounds for Aleshin and Bellaterra graph families, and give explicit spectral-gap and edge-expansion lower bounds.
- **Authors:** Olga Kharlampovich, Alina Vdovina
- **arXiv:** `2610.30662v1` (published 2026-10-22 03:23:21 UTC; categories: `math.CO`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** A claimed resolution of the Malyshev–Pak conjecture with explicit constants, alongside improved diameter growth, gives a concrete combinatorial result rather than a topic-only signal.
- **Claimed result:** The authors obtain normalized spectral gap at least $1-2\sqrt2/3$ and edge-expansion constant at least $3-2\sqrt2$ for Aleshin graphs, and show that undirected Aleshin graphs are not Ramanujan for $n\ge10$.
- **Assumptions and setting:** The claims concern the specified levels of the Aleshin and Bellaterra graph families, whose level-$n$ vertex count is $2^n$.
- **Caveat:** These estimates concern particular automaton-generated graph families; they do not establish comparable bounds for arbitrary finite graphs.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, 2026-10-23 announcement batch
- **Themes:** Combinatorics & discrete mathematics

### 5. [Deep holes of full-length Reed--Solomon codes: uniform bounds and exact classifications](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31460)
- **Abstract:** The author classifies deep holes for interpolation-degree offsets $m=3,4,5,6,7$ over every finite field and proves uniform bounds for arbitrary $m\ge3$, with explicit exceptional families in two characteristic-two cases.
- **Authors:** Xiang Fan
- **arXiv:** `2610.31460v1` (published 2026-10-22 15:53:11 UTC; categories: `cs.IT`, `math.CO`, `math.NT`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** The abstract states exact classifications without a field-size hypothesis for five offsets, general uniform bounds, and cross-listing across coding theory, combinatorics, and number theory.
- **Claimed result:** For $m\in\{3,4,5,6,7\}$ it gives all deep-hole cases over finite fields; it also supplies field-size-dependent exclusions/classifications for arbitrary $m\ge3$.
- **Assumptions and setting:** Full-length Reed–Solomon code $\operatorname{RS}_q(k)$ and received words whose interpolation polynomial has degree $k+m$; the broad uniform bounds require the explicit inequalities on $q$ stated in the abstract.
- **Caveat:** The exact unconditional classification is limited to $m\le7$; the general-$m$ results have field-size and parity conditions, and some finite cases use supplied computation certificates.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, 2026-10-23 announcement batch
- **Themes:** Combinatorics & discrete mathematics; Algebra & number theory

### 6. [A semiampleness result for hyperkähler fourfolds and smoothings of Lagrangian subvarieties](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31497)
- **Abstract:** For a smooth hyperkähler fourfold with Picard group generated by a nontrivial nef non-big line bundle of nonnegative Kodaira dimension, the authors prove that some positive multiple defines a Lagrangian fibration.
- **Authors:** Andreas Höring, Vladimir Lazić, Christian Lehn
- **arXiv:** `2610.31497v1` (published 2026-10-22 16:14:52 UTC; categories: `math.AG`, `math.CV`, `math.DG`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** A stated semiampleness theorem with a geometric proof and three algebraic-geometric/differential-geometric categories; the authors also identify unobstructedness and smoothing results as tools of independent interest.
- **Claimed result:** Under the stated hypotheses, a positive tensor power of the line bundle induces a Lagrangian fibration; the proof uses an analogue of Voisin's unobstructedness result and a smoothing theorem for singular Lagrangian subvarieties.
- **Assumptions and setting:** $X$ is a smooth hyperkähler fourfold; $\operatorname{Pic}(X)$ is generated by a nef, nontrivial, non-big line bundle $L$ and $\kappa(L)\ge0$.
- **Caveat:** The theorem retains the Picard-rank-one and nonnegative-Kodaira-dimension hypotheses; it does not establish semiampleness for every nef non-big class.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, 2026-10-23 announcement batch
- **Themes:** Geometry & topology

- **Caveat:** The conclusions are conditional on the stated arithmetic hypotheses and use low-corank 2-converse and 2-primary BSD inputs.

## Open Problems and Research Directions

- **Author-stated open problem:** Conway's conjecture still asks whether every thrackle has at most as many edges as vertices. Simon's $4(n-1)/3$ bound narrows the gap but does not close it. A follow-up could test whether stronger exclusions of small non-thrackleable configurations improve the discharging bound. ([Simon](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31589))
- **Boundary cases (synthesis):** Becker and Yang cover $0\le\sigma<1/3$; determine whether an analogous construction exists at $\sigma=1/3$, or under weaker smoothness/sign conditions, to locate the sharp threshold. ([Becker–Yang](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31637))
- **Classification beyond the proved range (synthesis):** Fan's exact classification stops at $m=7$, while the arbitrary-$m$ results require field-size conditions. Investigate whether those thresholds are sharp and whether exact classifications extend to $m\ge8$. ([Fan](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31460))
- **Relaxing geometric hypotheses (synthesis):** For the hyperkähler semiampleness result, test whether the conclusion survives when the Picard group is not generated by one class or when $\kappa(L)\ge0$ is removed. ([Höring–Lazić–Lehn](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31497))
- **Arithmetic hypotheses (synthesis):** The Kolyvagin-class nonvanishing theorem uses surjectivity and a level-4 exclusion condition; identify which parts can be weakened or replaced while preserving the twist construction. ([Sun](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31631))
