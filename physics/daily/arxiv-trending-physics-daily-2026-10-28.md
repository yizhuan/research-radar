### 1. [Search for Neutrino-Produced Long-Lived Particles Using the SBND Cosmic Ray Tagger Beam Telescope](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37555)
- **Abstract:** The SBND CRT Beam Telescope dataset enables a search for neutrino-produced long-lived particles decaying to electron–positron pairs; no significant excess is found and 90% confidence limits are set.
- **Authors:** R. Acciarri, L. Aliaga-Soplin, D. Andrade Aldana, C. Andreopoulos, A. Antonakis et al.
- **arXiv:** `2610.37555v1` (published 2026-10-27 17:04:41 UTC; categories: `hep-ex`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** First beyond-Standard-Model search using this standalone SBND CRT data set, with a concrete decay-length-dependent limit and Fermilab report number FERMILAB-PUB-26-0632-PPD.
- **Claimed result:** The authors report no significant excess and 90% CL upper limits on effective production cross sections of roughly 10⁻³⁷–10⁻³⁹ cm² across 0.3–100 m laboratory decay lengths.
- **Evidence type:** experimental; CRT Beam Telescope data from the Booster Neutrino Beam, corresponding to 2.95×10²⁰ protons on target.
- **Caveat:** The authors identify limited geometric acceptance and reduced event information in the CRT-only configuration as sensitivity limits.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, 28 Oct 2026 batch
- **Themes:** Particle, nuclear & high-energy physics; Instrumentation & detectors

### 2. [Measurement of antideuteron and net-Λ number fluctuations at the Large Hadron Collider](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.36307)
- **Abstract:** ALICE measures antideuteron–net-Λ number correlations in Pb–Pb collisions to separate correlations associated with hadronization from those associated with antinucleus formation.
- **Authors:** ALICE Collaboration
- **arXiv:** `2610.36307v1` (published 2026-10-26 21:30:06 UTC; categories: `nucl-ex`, `hep-ex`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** A new ALICE collaboration measurement with CERN report number CERN-EP-2026-283, cross-listed between nuclear and experimental high-energy physics, and a targeted observable for a stated model discrepancy.
- **Claimed result:** The authors find that the antideuteron–net-Λ correlation and net-Λ second-order cumulant are described by Thermal-FIST statistical hadronization; they argue this supports a nucleosynthesis origin for the antideuteron–antiproton correlation.
- **Evidence type:** experimental; ALICE Pb–Pb collision data at √sNN = 5.02 TeV.
- **Caveat:** The mechanistic interpretation depends on comparisons with statistical-hadronization and coalescence models and is specific to the measured collision system and observables.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, 28 Oct 2026 batch
- **Themes:** Particle, nuclear & high-energy physics

### 3. [Improving cosmological distance measurements at high redshift using Lyman-Alpha forest BAO reconstruction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37664)
- **Abstract:** The authors adapt Zel’dovich reconstruction to Lyα-forest BAO and find reduced peak damping and smaller distance-parameter uncertainties in AbacusSummit mocks, including DESI-like masked-survey tests.
- **Authors:** Boryana Hadzhiyska, Roger de Belsunce
- **arXiv:** `2610.37664v1` (published 2026-10-27 17:59:31 UTC; categories: `astro-ph.CO`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** Quantified gains on a high-redshift DESI observable, demonstrated across idealized and DESI-like mock configurations; arXiv lists report number MIT-CTP/6133.
- **Claimed result:** In a DESI-like masked mock, reconstruction reduces uncertainties in radial and transverse BAO scaling parameters by about 27% and 10%, respectively; this is a simulation result, not a new DESI measurement.
- **Evidence type:** computational; Lyα mocks built on AbacusSummit N-body simulations, with linear control variates and modeled survey masks.
- **Caveat:** The authors note that mocks omit important observational effects, including instrument response, pixel noise, continuum fitting, finite forest lengths, gaps, and several contaminants; the masked test also assumes perfect continuum knowledge and no radial mask.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, 28 Oct 2026 batch
- **Themes:** Cosmology & gravitation; Computational & data methods

### 4. [Weighing dark matter particles with gravitational waves](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37638)
- **Abstract:** The authors forecast that LISA extreme-mass-ratio inspirals in sufficiently dense dark-matter spikes could constrain ultralight dark-matter particle masses through wave-dependent environmental effects on the inspiral.
- **Authors:** Rodrigo Vicente, Francisco Duque, Theophanes K. Karydas, Gianfranco Bertone
- **arXiv:** `2610.37638v1` (published 2026-10-27 17:54:29 UTC; categories: `gr-qc`, `astro-ph.CO`, `astro-ph.GA`, `astro-ph.HE`, `hep-ph`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** Unusually broad five-category physics/astrophysics cross-listing and a concrete bridge between ultralight-particle physics, dark-matter spike structure, and future millihertz gravitational-wave inference.
- **Claimed result:** Using simulated LISA signals and joint inference, the authors forecast order-of-magnitude mass constraints across 10⁻¹⁴–10⁻¹¹ eV for low-mass massive black holes, around 10⁵ solar masses.
- **Evidence type:** forecast/proposal; relativistic inspiral waveforms with modeled accretion and dynamical friction, tested on simulated LISA signals.
- **Caveat:** The forecast requires sufficiently dense dark-matter spikes and depends on the assumed environmental and binary-waveform models; it is not a detection.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, 28 Oct 2026 batch
- **Themes:** Cosmology & gravitation; Astrophysics; Particle, nuclear & high-energy physics

### 5. [Improved nonflow control for multiparticle correlators and system-size-dependent evolution from OO to Pb--Pb collisions at the LHC](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.36922)
- **Abstract:** The authors validate a nonflow-subtraction method for multiparticle heavy-ion correlators and use it to predict oxygen–oxygen results for comparison with published Pb–Pb measurements.
- **Authors:** Neelkamal Mallick, Suraj Prasad
- **arXiv:** `2610.36922v1` (published 2026-10-27 09:49:55 UTC; categories: `nucl-ex`, `hep-ex`, `hep-ph`, `nucl-th`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** Four-way cross-listing and a direct methodological response to nonflow biases that can mimic collective signals in small collision systems; the work connects OO predictions to existing Pb–Pb results.
- **Claimed result:** In tests without collective flow, the authors find their multiplicity-scaled estimator more reliable for nonflow control, then apply it to AMPT predictions for OO collisions at √sNN = 5.36 TeV.
- **Evidence type:** computational; controlled toy model, PYTHIA 8 Angantyr, and AMPT simulations, compared with published ALICE Pb–Pb measurements.
- **Caveat:** The OO outcomes are model predictions, not reported OO measurements; inference depends on the nonflow subtraction and AMPT modeling.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, 28 Oct 2026 batch
- **Themes:** Particle, nuclear & high-energy physics; Computational & data methods

### 6. [Mediated Interactions between Fermi Polarons in the Strongly Interacting Regime](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37654)
- **Abstract:** Zero-temperature quantum Monte Carlo calculations find repulsive mediated interactions between Fermi polarons throughout the polaronic branch and recover RKKY-like behavior for static impurities at weak coupling.
- **Authors:** Renato Pessoa, S. A. Vitiello, Luis A. Peña Ardila
- **arXiv:** `2610.37654v1` (published 2026-10-27 17:58:38 UTC; categories: `cond-mat.quant-gas`, `physics.atom-ph`, `quant-ph`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** Cross-listed across quantum gases, atomic physics, and quantum physics; it gives a microscopic benchmark and explicitly addresses a trend seen in prior experiments near resonance.
- **Claimed result:** The authors report a repulsive Landau interaction at all couplings in the polaronic branch, with short-range universal results insensitive to microscopic interaction details.
- **Evidence type:** computational; zero-temperature quantum Monte Carlo calculations of Fermi polarons and static impurities.
- **Caveat:** The result is a ground-state calculation within the polaronic branch; comparison to near-resonance experimental trends does not by itself resolve finite-temperature or experimental-system effects.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, 28 Oct 2026 batch
- **Themes:** Condensed matter & materials; Atomic, molecular & optical physics

## Open Problems and Research Directions

- **SBND acceptance and event information — paper-stated limitation:** The CRT-only geometry and reduced event information limit the search (2610.37555). **Follow-up (synthesis):** test whether full SBND detector running and additional decay topologies can extend the meter-scale LLP reach while controlling backgrounds.
- **Lyα survey realism — paper-stated limitation:** The BAO reconstruction tests omit instrument response, pixel noise, continuum fitting, finite forest lengths and gaps, and other contaminants; the mask result assumes perfect continuum knowledge (2610.37664). **Follow-up (synthesis):** validate the method on realistic DESI-like mocks and data, including continuum and instrument systematics, and compare forest-derived versus external-tracer displacement fields.
- **Disentangling antinucleus formation from hadronization — paper-grounded open question:** The ALICE result interprets different correlations through coalescence and statistical-hadronization descriptions (2610.36307). **Follow-up (synthesis):** measure complementary species correlations, collision energies, and centralities to test whether the interpretation persists beyond the reported Pb–Pb observable.
- **Dark-matter spike dependence — paper-stated model condition:** The LISA mass forecast requires sufficiently dense spikes around massive black holes (2610.37638). **Follow-up (synthesis):** vary spike profiles and environmental systematics in waveform injections to quantify when particle-mass inference remains identifiable.
