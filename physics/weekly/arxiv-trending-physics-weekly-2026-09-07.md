# This week's notable physics papers on arXiv

## Headline

The strongest visible pattern in the 1–7 September 2026 sample is the push to extract more physics from difficult, noisy regimes: joint 21-cm/CO cosmological reconstruction, Bayesian stress-testing of dark-energy extensions, noisy entanglement, and open-system transport. Astrophysics also shows a recurring emphasis on formation histories and survival constraints rather than isolated discoveries.

This is an inferred “hot” list, not an official arXiv ranking: arXiv publishes no trending, download, or readership chart. Because the papers are only days old, citation-based popularity signals are necessarily sparse.

## Top papers (ranked)

### 1. [Tracing the Cosmic Origins: Machine Learning Reconstruction of the Primordial Density Field from EoR Observations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.05412)
- **Abstract:** A 3-D U-Net reconstructs the primordial density field at z≈300 from simulated 21-cm and CO intensity maps at z≈8, with joint tracers improving recovery and tightening σ8 and ns constraints by about a factor of two.
- **Authors:** Anchal Saxena, P. Daniel Meerburg, Guochao Sun, Tzu-Ching Chang, Lluís Mas-Ribas
- **arXiv:** `2609.05412v1` (published 2026-09-04 17:58 UTC; categories: `astro-ph.CO`, `astro-ph.IM`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** Cross-listed between cosmology and instrumentation/methods; directly connects future SKA1-Low- and COMAP-ERA-like observations to primordial-information recovery; Semantic Scholar record was present but reported no citation totals yet.
- **Claimed result:** In simulations, combined 21-cm and CO tracers retain useful initial-field information under instrumental noise and improve parameter constraints; claimed by the authors, not yet demonstrated on real observations.
- **Evidence type:** Computational; LIMFAST simulations, realistic survey-resolution/noise models, U-Net reconstruction, and simulation-based inference.
- **Caveat:** The result is simulation-based and depends on the assumed tracer and astrophysical/instrumental models; real foregrounds and calibration effects remain to be established.
- **Announcement type:** New submission, 2026-09-04 batch
- **Themes:** Cosmology & gravitation; Computational & data methods; Instrumentation & detectors

### 2. [Early against Late: A contrast on dark energy in the light of DESI DR2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.05410)
- **Abstract:** A Bayesian model comparison of early-dark-energy and late-time CPL extensions using DESI BAO, Type-Ia supernova, and CMB information finds stronger evidence against EDE than against CPL, weakening apparent multi-sigma parameter shifts.
- **Authors:** Miguel A. Zapata, Karim Carrion, Gabriela Garcia-Arroyo
- **arXiv:** `2609.05410v1` (published 2026-09-04 17:58 UTC; category: `astro-ph.CO`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** Timely use of DESI DR2 and a concrete methodological point about prior-volume penalties; Semantic Scholar reported no citation totals yet.
- **Claimed result:** The authors report decisive Bayesian rejection of the tested EDE model after CMB inclusion, while CPL is only moderately disfavoured despite posterior shifts.
- **Evidence type:** Observational/statistical; DESI BAO, supernova, and CMB likelihood analysis with nested-sampling validation.
- **Caveat:** Conclusions are model- and prior-dependent; the comparison does not establish that ΛCDM is the final cosmological model.
- **Announcement type:** New submission, 2026-09-04 batch
- **Themes:** Cosmology & gravitation; Computational & data methods

### 3. [Two extremely irradiated volatile-rich sub-Neptunes with companions in the TOI-426 and TOI-1839 systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.05413)
- **Abstract:** TESS and 147 HARPS radial velocities identify two highly irradiated, volatile-rich sub-Neptunes plus additional companions, with atmospheric-escape modelling favouring steam-world interpretations and a population-level excess of outer giant companions near the Neptunian desert edge.
- **Authors:** A. Castro-González, O. Barragán, D. J. Armstrong, A. Aguichine, V. Bourrier, et al.
- **arXiv:** `2609.05413v1` (published 2026-09-04 17:58 UTC; category: `astro-ph.EP`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** Accepted-for-publication status in A&A; combines new TESS/HARPS system characterization with a population statistic (9/25 versus 9/148, Fisher p=1.4×10⁻⁴); Semantic Scholar reported no citation totals yet.
- **Claimed result:** The authors argue that the survival of volatile-rich planets and the outer-companion excess support a role for high-eccentricity tidal migration in shaping the desert edge.
- **Evidence type:** Observational; TESS photometry, HARPS radial velocities, Gaussian-process activity modelling, and interior/escape calculations.
- **Caveat:** The population inference is based on a modest detected sample and remains sensitive to selection effects and atmospheric-composition assumptions.
- **Announcement type:** New submission, 2026-09-04 batch
- **Themes:** Astrophysics; Computational & data methods

### 4. [Non-local games and communication complexity with noisy entanglement](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.05122)
- **Abstract:** The paper bounds noisy-CHSH performance, proves several parallel-repetition results, and derives communication lower bounds for distilling entanglement and computing Equality with noisy EPR pairs.
- **Authors:** Srijita Kundu, Olivier Lalonde
- **arXiv:** `2609.05122v1` (published 2026-09-04 13:20 UTC; category: `quant-ph`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** Multiple theorem-level consequences in quantum information and a claimed answer to an open question on noisy shared bits; Semantic Scholar reported no citation totals yet.
- **Claimed result:** Under several noise models, noisy entanglement is provably weaker than noiseless entanglement for selected games and communication tasks.
- **Evidence type:** Theoretical; non-local games, parallel repetition, and communication-complexity proofs.
- **Caveat:** Bounds are model-specific, and the results do not imply that all practical noisy quantum networks incur the same communication overhead.
- **Announcement type:** New submission, 2026-09-04 batch
- **Themes:** Quantum information

### 5. [Decompactification and the Flat-Space Limit of AdS5×S5 Master Correlators](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.05409)
- **Abstract:** Master propagators and correlators are used to take a simultaneous decompactification and flat-space limit of AdS5×S5 holographic correlators, connecting boundary and bulk constructions to higher-dimensional flat-space amplitudes.
- **Authors:** Burkhard Eden, Paul Heslop, Harshal Kulkarni, Arthur Lipstein, Romain Ruzziconi, Akshay Yelleshpur Srikant
- **arXiv:** `2609.05409v1` (published 2026-09-04 17:57 UTC; category: `hep-th`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** Addresses a central bridge between holography and flat-space scattering, with a cross-regime construction rather than a narrow calculation; Semantic Scholar reported no citation totals yet.
- **Claimed result:** The authors argue that master correlators recover restricted 5-D or unrestricted 10-D flat-space amplitudes in appropriate limits and discuss implications for flat-space holography.
- **Evidence type:** Theoretical; analytic correlator and amplitude construction.
- **Caveat:** Several interpretations are argued through analytic continuation and limiting procedures; their physical status depends on the validity of those constructions.
- **Announcement type:** New submission, 2026-09-04 batch
- **Themes:** Particle, nuclear & high-energy physics; Cosmology & gravitation

### 6. [Environment-assisted transport in a strongly correlated boundary-driven Fermi-Hubbard chain](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.05137)
- **Abstract:** TEBD simulations of an open one-dimensional Fermi-Hubbard chain reveal two distinct dephasing-induced transport enhancements associated with separate charge and spin/Hubbard-band redistribution processes.
- **Authors:** Hakan Yeler, Özgür Çakır
- **arXiv:** `2609.05137v1` (published 2026-09-04 13:38 UTC; categories: `cond-mat.str-el`, `quant-ph`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** Cross-listed between condensed matter and quantum physics and isolates a correlation-specific mechanism behind environment-assisted transport; Semantic Scholar reported no citation totals yet.
- **Claimed result:** Dephasing can enhance current in two regimes in the strongly correlated chain, unlike the single enhancement known for spinless fermions.
- **Evidence type:** Computational; Lindblad master-equation dynamics solved with time-evolving block decimation.
- **Caveat:** The study is one-dimensional and model-based; finite-size, bath, and boundary-driving choices limit direct experimental generalization.
- **Announcement type:** New submission, 2026-09-04 batch
- **Themes:** Condensed matter & materials; Quantum information; Computational & data methods

### 7. [Nonlinear evolution in Galileon EFTs: Regularization and screening](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.05407)
- **Abstract:** Spherically symmetric nonlinear evolution in two related Galileon effective field theories is used to compare ghost-free screening dynamics with a higher-derivative regularization that can introduce tachyonic or ghost-related behaviour.
- **Authors:** Farid Thaalba, Aaron Held, Ramiro Cayuso, Thomas P. Sotiriou
- **arXiv:** `2609.05407v1` (published 2026-09-04 17:55 UTC; categories: `gr-qc`, `hep-th`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** Bridges modified gravity and EFT well-posedness, and is cross-listed in gravitation and high-energy theory; Semantic Scholar reported no citation totals yet.
- **Claimed result:** The authors find that the unregularized ghost-free model becomes ill-posed only after the derivative expansion fails in the tested regime, while the regularized model can track it before breakdown but develops problematic behaviour in other regimes.
- **Evidence type:** Theoretical/computational; nonlinear spherical evolution of EFT field equations.
- **Caveat:** The conclusions are restricted to the studied symmetry, initial data, and EFT regime; a regulator that improves well-posedness is not automatically a consistent UV completion.
- **Announcement type:** New submission, 2026-09-04 batch
- **Themes:** Cosmology & gravitation; Particle, nuclear & high-energy physics; Computational & data methods

## Trending physics themes

- **Inference under realistic noise:** Papers 1, 2, and 6 all treat noise, priors, dissipation, or model uncertainty as part of the physics rather than as a post-processing detail.
- **Cross-subfield methods:** Papers 1 and 6 are explicitly cross-listed; machine learning, tensor-network simulation, and Bayesian evidence are being used as physics instruments, not merely generic tools.
- **Formation history as a discriminator:** Papers 3 and 4 ask whether observed systems can be explained by competing histories—planet migration versus evaporation, and isolated binary evolution versus hierarchical black-hole mergers.
- **Limits and consistency:** Papers 5 and 7 focus on whether formal limits or regularizations preserve the intended physical theory.

## Research opportunities

### Potential research areas

- Apply foreground-aware, end-to-end reconstruction to joint 21-cm/CO data rather than idealized simulated maps (paper 1).
- Develop evidence calculations that compare broader families of time-dependent dark-energy models while testing prior robustness (paper 2).
- Search for additional outer companions around highly irradiated sub-Neptunes to test the proposed desert-edge population excess (paper 3).
- Transfer noisy-entanglement bounds to experimentally realistic network protocols and finite-copy distillation (paper 4).

### Unsolved problems

- Whether primordial-field recovery survives realistic foreground subtraction and uncertain reionization/line-emission modelling (paper 1).
- Whether the DESI-era dark-energy preference changes under alternative supernova compilations, priors, or nonparametric histories (paper 2).
- Whether the planet-companion excess survives completeness corrections and a larger, less targeted sample (paper 3).
- Whether the Galileon regularization can be both numerically well-posed and physically acceptable in the screened regime (paper 7).

### Potential research directions

- Build joint simulation challenges with instrument systematics, foregrounds, and blind cosmological inference for the 21-cm/CO reconstruction problem (paper 1).
- Pair exoplanet radial-velocity surveys with population-level completeness modelling and migration simulations (paper 3).
- Test the two transport-enhancement regimes in cold-atom or engineered Hubbard systems with tunable dephasing (paper 6).
- Seek observables that distinguish isolated-binary from hierarchical black-hole formation channels, especially spin-orbit misalignment and repeated-merger signatures (paper 4's related astrophysical context is outside the ranked set; this direction is therefore presented as a synthesis, not a result of the seven papers above).
