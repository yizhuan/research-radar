### 2. [Completely monotone negative powers of hyperbolic polynomials](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.31592)
- **Abstract:** The paper proves complete-monotonicity and positivity conjectures for negative powers of homogeneous hyperbolic polynomials and derives quantitative log-concavity and Gaussian-comparison bounds for the associated Riesz kernels.
- **Authors:** Dongsheng Wei
- **arXiv:** `2609.31592v1` (published 2026-09-25 17:48 UTC; categories: `math.CO`, `math.AG`, `math.CA`)

Ranked highly for a direct answer to named conjectures, explicit dimension-dependent bounds, and its bridge across combinatorics, algebraic geometry, and analysis.
- **Claimed result:** The author states that every homogeneous half-plane-property polynomial has a completely monotone negative power, and that p^-alpha is completely monotone for alpha >= 4096n^2 under the stated hyperbolicity assumptions.
- **Assumptions and setting:** p is homogeneous and hyperbolic in n >= 2 variables and positive on its hyperbolicity cone; the Riesz-kernel conclusions additionally assume pointed cone closure.
- **Caveat:** The numerical constants and the specialized V'amos-quartic example are author claims in a preprint and need expert verification.

- **Bridges between geometry and algebra:** papers 5 and 6 use categorical or Fourier--Mukai frameworks to extend classical arithmetic and geometric correspondences.

## Open Problems and Research Directions

- **Author-stated open problem:** Firester, Tsiamis, and Zhao conjecture that the lowest density among non-flat cones in dimension n >= 5 is achieved by an O(k) × O(n-k)-invariant cone (paper 4). A direct test is to compare the constructed density families against broader symmetry classes.
- **Boundary cases for stability:** paper 1 leaves a natural research direction in extending the sharp hierarchy across the remaining dimensional and parameter regimes and determining whether analogous constants hold beyond the stated weighted line.
- **Generalization of the acceleration obstruction:** paper 7 suggests testing whether the no-universal-gain phenomenon persists under additional regularity, strong convexity, or finite-dimensional structure assumptions.
- **Arithmetic period rings:** paper 5 opens the question of how the higher-dimensional R_{H,K} framework interacts with further formal-group towers and whether more classical comparison results survive when φ_E is not a residue-field Frobenius lift.
- **Scope of Prym duality:** the stack-based techniques in paper 6 suggest checking which parts of the duality, motivic decomposition, and perverse-filtration package extend beyond the elliptic locus and the specified compactified fibrations.
