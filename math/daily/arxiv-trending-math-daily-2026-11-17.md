### 1. [A proof of the Reinhardt conjecture](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21470)

- **arXiv:** `2611.21470v1` (published 2026-11-16 17:48:16 UTC; categories: `math.MG`, `math.OC`)

- **Claimed result:** The author claims the Reinhardt conjecture, including the equality characterization by nonsingular affine images of the smoothed regular octagon (arXiv abstract).
- **Assumptions and setting:** Centrally symmetric convex disks in the plane; compare each disk's optimal lattice-packing density.

### 2. [Generic splitting types and Terao's conjecture for seventeen and eighteen lines](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21506)
- **Abstract:** For complex projective line arrangements, the authors claim the generic splitting type of the logarithmic bundle is determined by the intersection lattice through 18 lines, thereby proving Terao's freeness conjecture for 17- and 18-line arrangements and extending a bounded-exponent case to any number of lines.
- **Authors:** Cristian Anghel, Rares Stan
- **arXiv:** `2611.21506v1` (published 2026-11-16 18:04:08 UTC; categories: `math.AG`, `math.CO`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** A concrete finite-range conjecture result spanning algebraic geometry and combinatorics; the paper supplies ancillary code, data, and certificates and devotes sections to computation reliability. Its introduction notes an independent recent proof for the 17-line case, while this paper also treats 18 lines and broader consequences.
- **Claimed result:** The authors claim lattice-determined generic splitting through 18 lines, Terao for 17 and 18 lines, and freeness invariance for free arrangements whose smaller exponent is at most 8 (paper abstract and Theorems 1.1–1.2, 1.6).
- **Assumptions and setting:** Arrangements of complex projective lines; the stated conjecture cases are 17 and 18 lines, and the all-size extension is limited to the stated smaller-exponent condition.
- **Caveat:** The proof is computer-assisted and the authors explicitly make no positive-characteristic claim; the computations and certificates warrant independent verification.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, 17 November 2026 announcement batch
- **Themes:** Algebra & number theory; Combinatorics & discrete mathematics

### 3. [A Proof of Stein's Equidissection Conjecture](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.20240)
- **Abstract:** The author claims that no balanced planar polygon can be equidissected into an odd number of equal-area triangles, completing Stein's conjecture beyond the previously treated rational-coordinate case.
- **Authors:** Dmitry Krachun
- **arXiv:** `2611.20240v1` (published 2026-11-15 20:43:22 UTC; categories: `math.CO`, `math.NT`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** A stated full resolution of a named conjecture with a clear bridge between polygon dissections, trivalent-graph flows, and number-field valuations; cross-listed in combinatorics and number theory.
- **Claimed result:** The author claims that every balanced polygon in the plane has no equidissection into an odd number of triangles (paper abstract and Theorem 2).
- **Assumptions and setting:** A balanced polygon is one whose oriented boundary vectors in each direction sum to zero; the claim concerns equal-area triangular dissections and odd triangle counts.
- **Caveat:** The result is specifically about balanced polygons and parity of equal-area triangular dissections; it does not settle analogous questions for arbitrary polygons.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, 17 November 2026 announcement batch
- **Themes:** Combinatorics & discrete mathematics; Algebra & number theory

- **Claimed result:** The author claims a rank-profile obstruction invariant under Hilbert–Schmidt perturbation and normal stabilization, distinguishing the constructed operator from zero (arXiv abstract).
- **Assumptions and setting:** A compact operator with trace-class self-commutator and zero Helton–Howe measure; the non-approximation assertion is in the Schatten class S₂ under the stated bounded perturbations.
- **Caveat:** This is a technically constrained operator counterexample; its hypotheses should not be read as a statement about arbitrary compact operators.

Testing whether the lattice-uniformity and certificate methods extend to larger line counts, larger exponents, or positive characteristic would probe these boundaries. [2611.21506]
- **Exceptional loci (Green; synthesis):** Since the authors state the conjectures hold generically, a natural direction is to characterize which special covers or related curve families produce the non-generic syzygy and secant failures, beyond the exhibited genus-10 W(E₆)-covers. [2611.21197]
- **Operator hypotheses (Voiculescu; synthesis):** The counterexample depends on trace-class self-commutator, vanishing Helton–Howe measure, and a Hilbert–Schmidt obstruction. Determining which hypotheses can be weakened while retaining a stable obstruction would test the reach of the construction. [2611.21495]
- **Potential-class boundary (Ten Martini; synthesis):** The abstract leaves the admissible monotone-potential class implicit. Making the hypotheses explicit and testing whether the polynomial gap mechanism extends to broader monotone or less regular potentials would clarify the theorem's boundary. [2611.21544]
- **Beyond the critical rational case (Thouless; synthesis):** The theorem is stated for the critical rational Harper operator. Investigating whether analogous bandwidth scaling persists away from criticality or under irrational-frequency limits would test how special the elliptic-period mechanism is. [2611.19723]
