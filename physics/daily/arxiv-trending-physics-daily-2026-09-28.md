## Open Problems and Research Directions

- **Supernova decomposition uncertainty (author-stated):** The JWST paper says the extinction-corrected luminosity depends on host subtraction and template assumptions.[1] Follow-up spectra or independent host/transient decompositions could test how robust the luminosity estimate is.[1]
- **TOI-210's interior signals and composition:** The two short-period radial-velocity signals remain plausible rather than confirmed, and bulk density permits distinct volatile-rich interiors.[2] Further NIRPS radial-velocity monitoring could test the signals; atmospheric characterization, which the authors identify as a JWST opportunity, could help distinguish composition scenarios.[2]
- **Generalizing the gauge sampler (scope of present validation):** The tests cover U(1) in 2D/4D and SU(2) in 2D.[5] A useful next test would be larger non-Abelian lattices and weaker-coupling regimes, with comparisons against conventional Monte Carlo; this is a proposed extension, not a result claimed by the authors.[5]
- **From a photonic interface to network operation:** The Si-on-SiC paper demonstrates single-photon emission and fiber operation, not multi-node entanglement.[3] Demonstrating coherent spin–photon entanglement and a reproducible link between two devices would test the platform's network-level promise.[3]
