### 1. [Construction of the 2D non-Abelian Yang-Mills-Higgs measure via stochastic quantisation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03640)
- **Abstract:** The authors construct the non-Abelian Yang–Mills–Higgs measure on a two-dimensional torus by stochastic quantisation, proving global well-posedness, uniform-in-time bounds, and a unique invariant measure for the associated flow on gauge orbits.
- **Authors:** Bjoern Bringmann, Sky Cao, Martin Hairer, Wenhao Zhao
- **arXiv:** `2611.03640v1` (published 2026-11-02 18:58:13 UTC; categories: `math.AP`, `math-ph`, `math.PR`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** Three-way cross-listing across PDE, mathematical physics, and probability; the abstract claims a first construction for this model. A Warwick stochastic-analysis seminar listing for Wenhao Zhao on 28 Oct 2026 is an independent, checkable discussion signal.
- **Claimed result:** The authors report that, for the stochastic Yang–Mills–Higgs flow, a gauge-covariant approximation converges after renormalisation to a globally well-posed flow that is topologically irreducible and strong Feller; hence it has a unique invariant probability measure defining the sought measure.
- **Assumptions and setting:** Two-dimensional torus; compact connected Lie group, finite-dimensional orthogonal Higgs representation, and fixed coupling parameters; the paper works in distributional gauge-orbit spaces with a sufficiently small regularity parameter.
- **Caveat:** This is a 350-page preprint, and the measure is constructed in the stated two-dimensional setting, not for arbitrary manifolds or dimensions; the extensive analytic argument has not been independently assessed here.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, 3 Nov 2026 announcement batch
- **Themes:** Analysis & PDE; Probability & statistics; Mathematical physics

### 2. [Local scaling limit of the four-dimensional uniform spanning tree](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03582)
- **Abstract:** The author proves that the wired uniform spanning tree of \(\mathbb Z^4\), viewed from the origin, converges locally to a spatial Brownian Kesten tree, jointly tracking intrinsic distance, volume, and Euclidean embedding, with logarithmic rescalings.
- **Authors:** Runsheng Liu
- **arXiv:** `2611.03582v1` (published 2026-11-02 18:32:13 UTC; categories: `math.PR`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** The paper addresses the critical four-dimensional case left open by earlier scaling-limit results and gives a concrete continuum object plus sharp first-moment asymptotics. The abstract’s claim is a substantial probability result, rather than evidence of measured readership.
- **Claimed result:** The author proves local rooted spatial Gromov-weak convergence to a Brownian spine with independent spatial Brownian trees grafted at constant rate, and convergence of the rescaled space-time occupation measure to infinite canonical super-Brownian motion.
- **Assumptions and setting:** The wired uniform spanning forest on \(\mathbb Z^4\), rooted at the origin; distance, volume, and spatial positions are rescaled jointly using dimension-four logarithmic corrections.
- **Caveat:** The result is specific to the critical four-dimensional lattice model and its stated local topology; the scaling constants \(\alpha\) and \(\kappa\) are not individually known explicitly (the paper gives their product).
- **Announcement type:** new submission, 3 Nov 2026 announcement batch
- **Themes:** Probability & statistics

### 3. [A proof of the spectral rigidity for complex projective space](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03536)
- **Abstract:** The authors claim that, among compact connected Kähler manifolds, the spectrum with multiplicities of the Hodge Laplacian in any one fixed degree determines complex projective space with its Fubini–Study metric up to holomorphic isometry.
- **Authors:** Kefeng Liu, Hao Xu
- **arXiv:** `2611.03536v1` (published 2026-11-02 18:01:00 UTC; categories: `math.DG`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** A precise rigidity theorem addressing a stated folklore conjecture, with a strong one-spectrum conclusion for every fixed pair \((p,n)\), is a notable geometric result; no attention or citation metric is used for its placement.
- **Claimed result:** The authors prove the one-fixed-degree Hodge-spectrum rigidity statement and outline a chain using periodic geodesic flow, integral cohomology, heat coefficients, wave traces, and Kähler geometry.
- **Assumptions and setting:** Compact connected Kähler manifold without boundary, with the Hodge-Laplacian spectrum including multiplicities equal to that of \(\mathbb{CP}^n\) in a single fixed degree \(0\le p\le n\); the conclusion is holomorphic isometry to the Fubini–Study model.
- **Caveat:** This is a technically long preprint; the universal claim depends on handling exceptional dimension pairs by additional wave-trace and topological arguments, which have not been independently checked here.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, 3 Nov 2026 announcement batch
- **Themes:** Geometry & topology

### 4. [Near-optimal bounds for the critical 2d SHF and the delta-Bose gas](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03191)
- **Abstract:** The authors improve the upper bound on the \(N\)-th moment of the critical two-dimensional stochastic heat flow to \(\exp(\exp(CN^{1+o(1)}))\), bringing it close to the known lower bound and identifying the exponential scale predicted for the delta-Bose gas.
- **Authors:** Xue-Mei Li, Te-Chun Wang
- **arXiv:** `2611.03191v1` (published 2026-11-02 14:21:03 UTC; categories: `math.PR`, `math-ph`, `math.FA`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** Three-way probability/physics/functional-analysis cross-listing and a quantified improvement from an \(N^2\) exponent to \(N^{1+o(1)}\), compared in the abstract with an existing lower bound.
- **Claimed result:** The authors establish near-optimal upper and lower exponential scales for the critical 2D stochastic heat-flow moments, and for each fixed \(N\) express the delta-Bose ground-state energy through the bottom of a principal-operator spectrum.
- **Assumptions and setting:** Critical two-dimensional stochastic heat flow and the two-dimensional \(N\)-body delta-Bose gas; the spectral formula is stated for each fixed \(N\).
- **Caveat:** The result reaches the conjectured exponential scale only up to a vanishing error in the exponent; the abstract does not claim a full resolution of Rajeev’s conjecture.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, 3 Nov 2026 announcement batch
- **Themes:** Probability & statistics; Mathematical physics; Analysis & PDE

### 5. [The proof of the Harary--Hill conjecture: a detailed exposition and an extension to rotation systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03515)
- **Abstract:** The author presents a combinatorial exposition of a reported proof of the Harary–Hill crossing-number conjecture and proves a more general weighted inequality for rotation systems of complete graphs.
- **Authors:** Gelasio Salazar
- **arXiv:** `2611.03515v1` (published 2026-11-02 17:51:35 UTC; categories: `math.CO`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** The conjecture proof has independent public discussion, including an earlier arXiv exposition and a mathematical commentary; this paper adds a stated rotation-system generalisation. The discussion signal is about the underlying proof, not evidence that this exposition has been peer reviewed.
- **Claimed result:** The author proves that every rotation system of \(K_n\) has at least \(Z(n)\) when one counts realizable four-vertex subsystems with weight one and non-realizable ones with weight three; the Harary–Hill formula follows.
- **Assumptions and setting:** Complete graph \(K_n\), \(n\ge3\), and an arbitrary rotation system; the paper’s main inequality is combinatorial and does not require a drawing of the graph.
- **Caveat:** The author explicitly frames this as an exposition of a proof uploaded elsewhere, rather than the original discovery; the underlying proof’s correctness is not independently established by the existence of this explanatory preprint.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, 3 Nov 2026 announcement batch
- **Themes:** Combinatorics & discrete mathematics

### 6. [Improved lower bounds for the kissing number in dimensions 49 to 61](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.01906)
- **Abstract:** The author gives explicit sphere-contact configurations improving the previously published kissing-number lower bound in every dimension from 49 through 61, with exact-integer certificates and code/data linked from the paper.
- **Authors:** Dmitry Kamenetsky
- **arXiv:** `2611.01906v1` (published 2026-11-01 13:49:22 UTC; categories: `math.MG`, `cs.IT`, `math.CO`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** Cross-listed in metric geometry, information theory, and combinatorics; the improvement covers 13 dimensions and the abstract links reproducible construction data and exact certificates.
- **Claimed result:** The author reports improvements over prior published lower bounds in all 13 dimensions, totaling 506,461 additional points across the stated configurations, verified in exact integer arithmetic.
- **Assumptions and setting:** Kissing configurations in Euclidean \(\mathbb R^d\), \(49\le d\le61\); constructions use an antipodal block lift of a large lattice shell and computer-assisted local/greedy search, with code and certificates linked in the arXiv record.
- **Caveat:** These are lower bounds, not proofs of the exact kissing numbers; the search certifies the listed configurations but not their optimality.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, 3 Nov 2026 announcement batch
- **Themes:** Geometry & topology; Combinatorics & discrete mathematics

### 7. [$C^1$-rigidity of $\mathbb{H}^{p,q}$-convex cocompact representations of Gromov hyperbolic groups](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03539)
- **Abstract:** The author proves that specified convex-cocompact representations with a continuously differentiable limit set must preserve a totally geodesic hyperbolic subspace, forcing the quotient representation to be virtually a cocompact hyperbolic lattice in the stated cases.
- **Authors:** Nikolay Bogachev
- **arXiv:** `2611.03539v1` (published 2026-11-02 18:02:36 UTC; categories: `math.DG`, `math.DS`, `math.GR`, `math.GT`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** Four-way cross-listing across geometry, dynamics, group theory, and geometric topology, together with a classification-style rigidity conclusion under a clear regularity hypothesis.
- **Claimed result:** For \(p>3\), a non-elementary hyperbolic group with boundary \(\mathbb S^{p-1}\) and the stated \(\mathbb H^{p,2}\)-convex cocompact representation with \(C^1\) proximal limit set preserves \(\mathbb H^p\); for odd \(p\), the paper extends the conclusion to signatures \(p>q\ge2\).
- **Assumptions and setting:** The group boundary must be a sphere of dimension \(p-1\); the representation is convex cocompact and its proximal limit set is a \(C^1\) submanifold. The broader arbitrary-signature conclusion is non-Zariski-density, not the full lattice conclusion.
- **Caveat:** The strongest conclusion has the listed dimension, signature, and boundary hypotheses; it should not be read as rigidity for arbitrary hyperbolic-group representations.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, 3 Nov 2026 announcement batch
- **Themes:** Geometry & topology

- **arXiv:** `2611.03487v1` (published 2026-11-02 17:29:16 UTC; categories: `math.NT`)

- **Claimed result:** If the polynomial Wronskian is nonzero modulo \(t\) and \(1\le s\le k(k+1)/2\), the counted integral solutions satisfy \(\Upsilon_{s,k}(X;\boldsymbol\varphi)\ll X^{2s+\varepsilon}\); the author outlines extensions and applications.
- **Assumptions and setting:** Polynomials \(\varphi_j(u)\in\mathbb Z[t,u]\), the stated Wronskian condition, and the stated range of \(s\); variables obey \(|u_i|,|v_i|\le X\), with constants implicit in the bound.
- **Caveat:** The estimate is conditional on the Wronskian and range restrictions; the abstract describes some broader applications as forthcoming rather than proved in this paper.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, 3 Nov 2026 announcement batch

## Open Problems and Research Directions

- **Author-stated / reported limitation — delta-Bose gas:** Li and Wang reach the predicted exponential scale only up to a vanishing error in the exponent, and give a principal-operator formula for each fixed \(N\). A follow-up could seek the sharper asymptotics needed to settle the remaining content of Rajeev’s conjecture, especially in a regime where \(N\) grows. [2611.03191]
- **Author-stated limitation — four-dimensional UST constants:** Liu notes that the scaling constants \(\alpha\) and \(\kappa\) are not individually explicit, although their product is identified. A concrete direction is to determine either constant or obtain quantitative rates for convergence to the Brownian Kesten tree. [2611.03582]
- **Synthesis — kissing numbers:** Kamenetsky supplies certified lower bounds, not matching upper bounds. One natural test is whether improved upper bounds can close any of the 49–61 dimensional gaps, using the released configurations as explicit candidates. [2611.01906]
