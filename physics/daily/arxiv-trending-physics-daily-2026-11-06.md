Brief agenda: collision geometry and nuclear forces; what changes when inference systematics are modeled; and two distinct ways JWST reaches from obscured galactic nuclei to neutrino cosmology. arXiv has no official trending chart; this is an inferred ranking, and citation/independent-attention evidence is limited because these papers are only a few days old.

## Top papers (ranked)

### 1. [Correlations and fluctuations of flow with event shape engineering in Pb-Pb and Xe-Xe collisions at the LHC](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08473)
- **Abstract:** The ALICE Collaboration measures correlations and fluctuations of flow harmonics in Pb–Pb and Xe–Xe collisions, using event-shape engineering to separate how initial geometry affects the resulting flow.
- **Authors:** ALICE Collaboration
- **arXiv:** `2611.08473v1` (published 2026-11-05 18:56:11 UTC; categories: `nucl-ex`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** ALICE provides a public figure set for this analysis and the paper is submitted to Physics Letters B; it reports direct flow-harmonic measurements in two collision systems and event-shape-resolved components of v4.
- **Claimed result:** The authors report a strong v2–v3 anticorrelation and positive v2–v4 correlation, and directly resolve linear and nonlinear v4 contributions versus q2 for the first time in Pb–Pb and Xe–Xe.
- **Evidence type:** experimental; ALICE Run-2 Pb–Pb (5.02 TeV) and Xe–Xe (5.44 TeV) data from 2015–2018, analyzed with event-shape engineering.
- **Caveat:** The paper reports relative systematic uncertainty for v4 of roughly 3–10%; the nonlinear-coupling coefficient also retains some q2 dependence in the most central collisions.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, Friday 6 November 2026 batch
- **Themes:** Particle, nuclear & high-energy physics

### 2. [The obscured nucleus of NGC3256 S. I. Anisotropic AGN near-IR radiation field traced by CO v=1-0 with JWST/NIRSpec](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07020)
- **Abstract:** JWST/NIRSpec CO emission from the outflow base in NGC 3256 S, interpreted with radiative-transfer models, indicates that its obscured active nucleus illuminates the outflow anisotropically.
- **Authors:** Miguel Pereira-Santaella, Eduardo González-Alfonso, Ismael García-Bernete, Andreas Efstathiou, Dimitra Rigopoulou et al.
- **arXiv:** `2611.07020v1` (published 2026-11-04 19:00:05 UTC; categories: `astro-ph.GA`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** Accepted for publication in Astronomy & Astrophysics with DOI 10.1051/0004-6361/202662221; it combines spatially resolved JWST spectroscopy with explicit radiative-transfer modeling to infer the obscured source geometry.
- **Claimed result:** The authors infer a 4.7-μm radiation field at the outflow base about 2–4 times stronger than along our line of sight and, under a collimated-beam interpretation, a 15–20° half-opening angle and a roughly 15 ± 5 pc collimating structure.
- **Evidence type:** observational; JWST/NIRSpec integral-field spectroscopy and non-LTE Monte Carlo radiative-transfer fits to CO v=1–0 emission in 13 apertures.
- **Caveat:** The opening angle and collimator size depend on the assumed geometry and radiative-transfer model; the authors note CO-ice absorption likely contributes to underpredicted P(1)/P(2) line fluxes.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, Friday 6 November 2026 batch
- **Themes:** Astrophysics

### 3. [Sudden disappearance of a bright exciton in solid helium](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07097)
- **Abstract:** The authors show that electron–hole exchange can make a prominent solid-helium exciton peak vanish for selected momentum-transfer directions near Bragg points.
- **Authors:** Fatema Mohamed, Lucia Reining, Matteo Gatti
- **arXiv:** `2611.07097v1` (published 2026-11-04 20:03:59 UTC; categories: `cond-mat.mtrl-sci`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** The work was accepted by Physical Review B on 2 November 2026 (DOI 10.1103/z9ks-9kyk) and connects a sharply testable optical-spectra prediction to electron–hole exchange and crystal local-field effects.
- **Claimed result:** Their response analysis predicts that near a Bragg point an arbitrarily small change in momentum-transfer direction can switch a strong excitonic peak off; they discuss pressure dependence and conditions for similar effects in other materials.
- **Evidence type:** theoretical; ab initio GW quasiparticle bands plus a Bethe–Salpeter electron–hole response calculation for solid-helium loss spectra, compared with previously reported experimental evidence.
- **Caveat:** This paper’s central result is an interpretation and prediction, not a new measurement; extension to other materials remains conditional on strong isolated excitonic peaks and significant local-field effects.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, Friday 6 November 2026 batch
- **Themes:** Condensed matter & materials

### 4. [Reanalysis of GW170817 and GW190425 under the Goldilocks Prior](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07147)
- **Abstract:** The authors reanalyze two binary-neutron-star gravitational-wave events with an equation-of-state-informed prior and find that source tidal-deformability posteriors can shift substantially relative to uniform-prior analyses.
- **Authors:** Sanika Khadkikar, B. S. Sathyaprakash
- **arXiv:** `2611.07147v1` (published 2026-11-04 21:18:35 UTC; categories: `gr-qc`, `astro-ph.HE`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** The paper is cross-listed across gravitation and high-energy astrophysics and points to a public LIGO Document Control Center companion (`P2600110-v3`); it quantifies how prior choice changes inferred neutron-star properties.
- **Claimed result:** For GW170817, the most probable tidal deformability of the lighter star rises from about 215 to 443 under the Goldilocks rather than uniform prior; for GW190425, allowed deformabilities contract, while its equation-of-state constraints remain more prior-sensitive.
- **Evidence type:** observational; Bayesian reanalysis of GW170817 and GW190425 gravitational-wave data with spectral and Gaussian-process equation-of-state ensembles.
- **Caveat:** The result is explicitly prior-dependent: the two prior treatments give differing GW190425 equation-of-state constraints, while the joint constraints are largely dominated by GW170817.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, Friday 6 November 2026 batch
- **Themes:** Cosmology & gravitation; Astrophysics

### 5. [Linking chiral nuclear forces to light-ion collisions: a global sensitivity analysis](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08122)
- **Abstract:** Coupled-cluster emulators are used to connect quadrupole geometry in ultrarelativistic oxygen and neon collisions to the low-energy constants of chiral nuclear interactions.
- **Authors:** Thomas Duguet, Andreas Ekström, Giuliano Giacalone, Gaute Hagen, Takayuki Miyagi, Alexander Tichai
- **arXiv:** `2611.08122v1` (published 2026-11-05 14:56:01 UTC; categories: `nucl-th`, `hep-ph`, `nucl-ex`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** It is cross-listed across nuclear theory, phenomenology, and experiment and makes a concrete bridge between LHC light-ion flow observables and parameters of low-energy nuclear forces.
- **Claimed result:** At next-to-next-to-leading order, the authors find linear, additive sensitivity of quadrupole eccentricity to all 16 chiral-interaction constants; 20Ne collisions are especially sensitive to the two-nucleon spin–orbit interaction.
- **Evidence type:** computational; subspace-projected coupled-cluster emulators and global sensitivity analysis, motivated by measured LHC 16O+16O and 20Ne+20Ne collective flow.
- **Caveat:** This is a sensitivity map at a specified order in the chiral expansion, not a new extraction of the force parameters from collision data; conclusions depend on the interaction and emulator framework.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, Friday 6 November 2026 batch
- **Themes:** Particle, nuclear & high-energy physics

### 6. [Analytic Marginalization Over Calibration Uncertainties in Gravitational Wave Inference and Application to Black Hole Ringdown](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07023)
- **Abstract:** The authors extend spline-based calibration-uncertainty marginalization to the time domain and apply it to black-hole ringdown analyses.
- **Authors:** David Dzingeleski, Aaron Zimmerman
- **arXiv:** `2611.07023v1` (published 2026-11-04 19:00:06 UTC; categories: `gr-qc`, `astro-ph.IM`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** The method is cross-listed in gravitation and astrophysical instrumentation and is demonstrated on two recent high-signal-to-noise ringdowns, making the calibration treatment directly relevant to strong-field inference.
- **Claimed result:** After calibration marginalization, the authors retain evidence for two modes in GW250114; for GW250207, the remnant mass and spin agree with full-signal estimates, but no second mode is supported.
- **Evidence type:** computational; analytic time-domain inference with basis splines, applied to LIGO ringdown data for GW250114 and GW250207.
- **Caveat:** The lack of evidence for a second GW250207 mode means the analysis does not tightly constrain deviations from general relativity during that ringdown.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, Friday 6 November 2026 batch
- **Themes:** Cosmology & gravitation; Instrumentation & detectors

### 7. [Latest $ν$s from the Earliest Galaxies: Probing neutrino self-interactions with JWST](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07013)
- **Abstract:** The authors combine JWST high-redshift-galaxy UV luminosity functions with Planck CMB data to constrain possible neutrino self-interactions.
- **Authors:** Ranjini Mondol, Priyank Parashari, Anirban Das, Vera Gluscevic, Ranjan Laha
- **arXiv:** `2611.07013v1` (published 2026-11-04 19:00:02 UTC; categories: `astro-ph.CO`, `hep-ph`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** The paper bridges cosmology and particle phenomenology and applies JWST’s high-redshift UV-luminosity data jointly with CMB constraints to a beyond-Standard-Model interaction parameter.
- **Claimed result:** The authors report 95% upper limits on log10(G_eff/MeV⁻²) of about −2.75 for one interacting neutrino flavor and −3.41 when all three flavors interact, tighter than their Planck-only bounds.
- **Evidence type:** observational; cosmological parameter-constraint analysis using JWST UV luminosity functions and Planck CMB data.
- **Caveat:** These are conditional upper limits, not evidence of a detection; the inferred bound depends on the interacting-flavor scenario and the modeling that maps early-galaxy abundances to small-scale matter power.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, Friday 6 November 2026 batch
- **Themes:** Cosmology & gravitation; Particle, nuclear & high-energy physics

### 8. [Threshold-Free Symmetry-Enriched Topological Quantum Memory](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07017)
- **Abstract:** The authors construct a decoder for a symmetry-enriched toric-code memory and prove exponentially decreasing logical error with system size under the paper’s noise model for a sufficient symmetry-size bound.
- **Authors:** Abhijat Sarma, Ruochen Ma, Cenke Xu
- **arXiv:** `2611.07017v1` (published 2026-11-04 19:00:04 UTC; categories: `quant-ph`, `cond-mat.str-el`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** The paper is cross-listed between quantum physics and strongly correlated matter and gives an explicit decoder construction with a quantified sufficient bound, rather than only proposing a qualitative protection mechanism.
- **Claimed result:** For a honeycomb toric code coupled to Sp(N) rotors, the authors show exponentially decreasing logical error for N ≥ 40 at any bond error probability in [0,1] under incoherent electric noise; they also extend the construction to other symmetries.
- **Evidence type:** theoretical; minimum-weight decoder and logical-error analysis for a symmetry-enriched toric-code model.
- **Caveat:** N ≥ 40 is a conservative sufficient condition for the specified model and noise channel, not an experimentally demonstrated hardware threshold or an optimized minimum.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, Friday 6 November 2026 batch
- **Themes:** Quantum information

## Trending Research Themes

- **Collision geometry to nuclear microphysics:** ALICE event-shape engineering separates flow-harmonic responses in Pb–Pb and Xe–Xe (#1), while the light-ion sensitivity study maps collective-flow geometry onto chiral-force parameters (#5). These are related LHC-facing analyses, not independent confirmations of one result.
- **Inference choices are part of the measurement:** the Goldilocks-prior reanalysis changes neutron-star tidal posteriors (#4), while time-domain calibration marginalization changes what can be inferred from black-hole ringdowns (#6).
- **JWST observables span very different physics scales:** spatially resolved CO around an obscured local AGN constrains its radiation geometry (#2); high-redshift galaxy counts combined with Planck constrain neutrino interactions (#7). The papers use distinct targets and analyses, not a shared dataset.
- **Isolated theoretical signals:** direction-sensitive exciton suppression in solid helium (#3) and symmetry-enriched quantum memory (#8) are notable individual results in this batch, not yet recurring multi-paper themes here.

## Open Problems and Research Directions

- **Author-stated — flow systematics:** ALICE reports v4 relative systematic uncertainty of roughly 3–10% and residual q2 dependence of the nonlinear-coupling coefficient in the most central events (#1). **Follow-up synthesis:** higher-statistics event-shape selections and comparisons with initial-state/hydrodynamic models could test whether the observed system-size dependence is geometric or fluctuation-driven.
- **Author-stated — AGN radiative-transfer fit:** the NGC 3256 S model underpredicts selected CO lines, plausibly because of CO-ice absorption, and the inferred collimation is model-dependent (#2). **Follow-up synthesis:** spatially resolved mid-IR/CO-ice constraints and independent geometry diagnostics could test the inferred opening angle and the proposed clearing of the obscuring envelope.
- **Author-stated — neutron-star inference:** GW190425’s equation-of-state constraints change with prior treatment, even after source-level priors are removed (#4); the GW250207 ringdown does not resolve a second mode (#6). **Follow-up synthesis:** compare broader waveform and equation-of-state prior families across additional binary-neutron-star events, and use future higher-SNR ringdowns to better separate calibration, mode content, and departures from relativity.
- **Model dependency — early-universe neutrino limits:** the JWST+Planck bounds vary with the assumed number of interacting flavors and depend on mapping high-redshift galaxy abundance to small-scale matter power (#7). **Follow-up synthesis:** improved high-redshift UV-luminosity functions and independent small-scale structure constraints could test how stable the bound is under alternative galaxy-formation prescriptions.
- **Author-stated bound — quantum memory:** N ≥ 40 is presented as a conservative sufficient bound for exponential error suppression in the specified model (#8). **Follow-up synthesis:** finite-size simulations and analysis under additional realistic noise channels could establish how close practical devices can come to this protection regime.

## Takeaway

The clearest cross-cutting pattern is that new analyses are extracting more information from existing high-value data by controlling geometry or treating inference systematics explicitly. The results are promising but remain author-reported preprints or accepted-paper findings, with important model and measurement dependencies still to be tested.
