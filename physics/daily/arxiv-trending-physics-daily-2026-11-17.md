- **arXiv:** `2611.20418v1` (published 2026-11-16T02:26:43Z; categories: `astro-ph.IM`, `astro-ph.GA`, `astro-ph.HE`)

- **Evidence type:** observational; independent reanalysis of Event Horizon Telescope very-long-baseline interferometry data.
- **Caveat:** This is a reanalysis of the same sparse EHT observations, not a new or independent telescope dataset; reconstruction remains sensitive to data coverage and analysis choices.

### 2. [Experimental Evidence of Elastic Instability in Viscoelastic Kolmogorov Flow](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21087)
- **Abstract:** Experiments and Oldroyd-B simulations show arrow-shaped flow structures and chaotic fluctuations consistent with a center-mode elastic instability in a confined, quasi-two-dimensional viscoelastic Kolmogorov flow.
- **Authors:** Christophe Brouzet, Mayank Chopra, Dario Vincenzi, Jason R. Picardo, Atul Varshney
- **arXiv:** `2611.21087v1` (published 2026-11-16T13:36:23Z; categories: `physics.flu-dyn`, `cond-mat.soft`, `nlin.CD`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** The work is listed as a 2026 Physical Review Letters paper with DOI; it combines direct experimental evidence with simulations and is cross-listed across fluid dynamics, soft matter and nonlinear science.
- **Claimed result:** The authors identify an elastic transition below the Newtonian-flow instability threshold and attribute the arrow-shaped structures to the center-mode mechanism.
- **Evidence type:** experimental and computational; electromagnetic forcing and particle-image-velocimetry measurements, compared with two-dimensional Oldroyd-B simulations.
- **Caveat:** The simulated onset differs from experiment (reported critical Weissenberg numbers about 10 versus about 3); the model is two-dimensional and uses a single polymer relaxation time, unlike the polydisperse experimental solution.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, Tuesday 17 November 2026 batch
- **Themes:** Plasma & fluid physics; Biophysics & soft matter; Computational & data methods

### 3. [EP241206a: A Sub-kilosecond X-Ray Flare Possibly from an Active Galactic Nucleus with a Flux Increase over 3 Orders of Magnitude](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21369)
- **Abstract:** Einstein Probe observations and follow-up data show a roughly 500-second soft X-ray flare positionally coincident with an AGN at redshift 0.62, but the physical association and origin remain uncertain.

- **arXiv:** `2611.21369v1` (published 2026-11-16T16:35:44Z; categories: `astro-ph.HE`)

- **Evidence type:** observational; Einstein Probe Wide-field X-ray Telescope data, optical spectroscopy, and archival/follow-up observations.
- **Caveat:** The association with the AGN is not firm; the paper reports a trial-corrected chance-coincidence probability of 4.9 × 10⁻³ for the relevant counterpart class, and the proposed jet mechanisms are interpretations rather than established causes.

### 4. [Reversal of dynamical backaction and cooling below the quantum backaction limit in Floquet-driven multimode optomechanical resonators](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21110)
- **Abstract:** A Floquet-modulated optical drive couples mechanical modes in a multimode resonator, enabling controllable reversal of dynamical backaction and cooling of a MHz mode below its single-mode quantum-backaction limit.
- **Authors:** Menno H. Jansen, Roel Burgwal, Ewold Verhagen
- **arXiv:** `2611.21110v1` (published 2026-11-16T13:58:22Z; categories: `quant-ph`, `cond-mat.mes-hall`, `physics.optics`)

- **Claimed result:** The authors cool the MHz mode to 47 ± 13 phonons—about half its single-mode quantum-backaction limit—and demonstrate that modulation frequency can switch cooling to amplification at fixed laser detuning.
- **Evidence type:** experimental and theoretical; multimode optomechanical resonator with Floquet-modulated optical drive, supported by theory and a deposited data/code record.
- **Caveat:** The reported performance is demonstrated in a particular resonator and mode configuration; the abstract does not establish how it generalizes across devices or operating regimes.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, Tuesday 17 November 2026 batch

- **Abstract:** Using 12 years of IceCube high-energy starting-event data, the authors test Earth absorption/regeneration and track-to-cascade ratios as complementary ways to constrain the neutral-current neutrino–nucleon cross section.
- **Authors:** Reinaldo Francener, Victor P. Goncalves
- **arXiv:** `2611.20887v1` (published 2026-11-16T10:57:36Z; categories: `hep-ph`, `hep-ex`)

- **Claimed result:** The authors find that present data can set upper limits but not a meaningful lower bound on the neutral-current cross section; their projections indicate IceCube-Gen2 could constrain both sides.
- **Evidence type:** observational; statistical analysis of 12 years of IceCube High-Energy Starting Events, using neutrino absorption/regeneration and event-topology rates.
- **Caveat:** The limits depend on astrophysical flavor composition and, for the absorption approach, charged-current cross-section and flux assumptions; neutral-current events are not individually identifiable in the sample.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, Tuesday 17 November 2026 batch

## Open Problems and Research Directions

- **M87* reconstruction robustness — author-stated limitation:** Sparse VLBI sampling and sensitivity to calibration, post-processing and imaging hyperparameters leave fine-scale morphology underconstrained (1). **Follow-up synthesis:** compare calibration-robust reconstructions across additional observing epochs and independently sampled baselines; this would test robustness without treating a reanalysis of the same dataset as a new observation.
- **Origin of EP241206a — author-stated limitation:** The transient's association with the AGN is not secure, and the jet, jet-in-jet and AGN-disk GRB scenarios remain alternatives (3). **Follow-up synthesis:** coordinated X-ray, optical and radio monitoring of future similar flares could test counterpart association and distinguish the proposed emission scenarios.
- **Neutral-current cross section — author-stated limitation:** IceCube currently supplies upper limits only, with sensitivity to flavor and charged-current assumptions (5). **Follow-up synthesis:** a joint fit that relaxes fixed flavor ratios, followed by the same complementary analyses with IceCube-Gen2 data, would probe whether a lower bound becomes supportable.
- **Elastic-instability threshold — author-stated limitation:** the experiment and idealized simulation give different onset Weissenberg numbers; the paper notes polymer polydispersity and a two-dimensional, single-relaxation-time model (2). **Follow-up synthesis:** measure the relaxation-time distribution and test three-dimensional simulations over matched confinement and forcing conditions.
