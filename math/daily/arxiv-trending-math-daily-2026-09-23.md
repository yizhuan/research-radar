### 4. [A proof of the Willmore-type conjecture in `CP^2`](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.26721)
- **Abstract:** The paper proves the conjectured Clifford-torus lower bound for the Willmore-type energy among closed Lagrangian surfaces in `CP^2`, while constructing non-Lagrangian deformations that lower the same energy.
- **Authors:** Peng Wang, Zhenxiao Xie, Chen Zhao
- **arXiv:** `2609.26721v1` (published 2026-09-22 17:09 UTC; category: `math.DG`)

- **Assumptions and setting:** Oriented closed Lagrangian surfaces in `CP^2`, genus `g >= 1`, versus unrestricted non-Lagrangian deformations.
- **Caveat:** The two conclusions apply to different classes; the unrestricted minimization problem is disproved rather than solved by the Lagrangian theorem.

- **Dependent sparse matrices:** Develop the proposed extension of the nonbacktracking framework to random matrices with more complex dependencies and identify when the stated spectral-edge scales remain valid ([2609.26615](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.26615)).
- **Rainbow Hamiltonicity thresholds:** Track how the theorem changes near the degree and bijumbledness thresholds, and whether analogous resilience and enumeration results hold for broader conflict systems ([2609.26655](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.26655)).
