### 1. [SBi3PCF: cosmology with Dark Energy Survey Year 3 cosmic shear](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05367)
- **Abstract:** The authors apply simulation-based inference to DES Year 3 cosmic-shear two- and three-point statistics, detecting the integrated three-point signal and jointly constraining wCDM.
- **Authors:** David Gebauer, Anik Halder, Zhengyangguang Gong, Cora Uhlemann
- **arXiv:** `2611.05367v1` (published 2026-11-03 18:56:57 UTC; categories: `astro-ph.CO`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** First survey-data analysis of the integrated three-point cosmic-shear statistic with simulation-based inference; the authors report S/N 12.8 for the three-point signal, extensive pre-unblinding checks, and a 1.59× figure-of-merit gain over the two-point-only analysis.
- **Claimed result:** The DES Y3 joint fit gives Ωm = 0.272 (+0.038/−0.033), S8 = 0.784 (+0.024/−0.021), and w0 = −1.04 (+0.25/−0.19); the authors report at most 2.4σ difference from Planck 2018 plus BAO in the three-parameter comparison.
- **Evidence type:** Observational; DES Year 3 weak-lensing shape catalogue analyzed with CosmoGridV1 N-body simulations and a learned likelihood.
- **Caveat:** The result depends on the forward model and its treatment of intrinsic alignments, baryonic feedback, photometric redshifts, and shear calibration; the reported comparison with CMB+BAO is not a discovery claim.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, Wednesday 4 November 2026 batch
- **Themes:** Cosmology & gravitation; Computational & data methods

### 2. [A New Precise Measurement of the e⁺e⁻→π⁺π⁻(γ) Cross Section with the Initial-State Radiation Method at BaBar](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.04596)
- **Abstract:** The BaBar Collaboration measures the two-pion cross section from threshold to 1.4 GeV using 460 fb⁻¹ of initial-state-radiation data and a pion-to-muon event ratio designed to cancel shared systematics.
- **Authors:** BaBar Collaboration
- **arXiv:** `2611.04596v1` (published 2026-11-03 09:50:30 UTC; categories: `hep-ex`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** A new full-dataset experimental measurement relevant to the hadronic-vacuum-polarization input to the muon magnetic anomaly; the ratio method reduces common uncertainties and the paper quantifies correlations with the 2009 BaBar result.
- **Claimed result:** For 0.3–1.4 GeV, the authors give the new two-pion leading-order HVP contribution as (525.9 ± 3.5_stat ± 3.8_syst) × 10⁻¹⁰; combining their 2009 and 2026 measurements gives (517.8 ± 2.4_stat ± 3.3_syst) × 10⁻¹⁰.
- **Evidence type:** Experimental; 460 fb⁻¹ of BaBar e⁺e⁻ collision data using initial-state radiation.
- **Caveat:** This is one experimental input over a specified energy interval, not by itself a resolution of the muon g−2 comparison; the HVP estimate inherits the cross-section measurement and its uncertainty treatment.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, Wednesday 4 November 2026 batch
- **Themes:** Particle, nuclear & high-energy physics; Instrumentation & detectors

### 3. [Measuring the ultraviolet background at z≈3 from the quasar proximity effect in DESI DR1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.04316)
- **Abstract:** Using 6,245 DESI DR1 quasars and mock-spectrum validation, the authors infer the hydrogen photoionization rate of the intergalactic ultraviolet background near redshift 3.
- **Authors:** Patrick Shaw, Rupert A. C. Croft
- **arXiv:** `2611.04316v1` (published 2026-11-03 04:47:43 UTC; categories: `astro-ph.CO`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** Large, named survey sample; the analysis calibrates its proximity-effect model on Astrid hydrodynamic mock spectra and reports statistical and systematic uncertainties separately.
- **Claimed result:** The authors obtain ΓHI = 1.376 (+0.095/−0.076 statistical) (+0.837/−0.521 systematic) × 10⁻¹² s⁻¹ at z≈3.
- **Evidence type:** Observational; DESI DR1 quasar spectra, with Hubble-trained continuum reconstruction and Astrid simulation-based validation.
- **Caveat:** The quoted systematic uncertainty is substantially larger than the statistical error; the result depends on modeling the quasar environment and its clustering bias.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, Wednesday 4 November 2026 batch
- **Themes:** Astrophysics; Cosmology & gravitation; Computational & data methods

### 4. [Fermi-LAT Detection of Extended GeV Emission toward the Middle-aged Supernova Remnant G156.2+05.7](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.04227)
- **Abstract:** An analysis of 17 years of Fermi-LAT observations finds extended GeV emission toward G156.2+05.7 and compares its spectrum and morphology with multiwavelength constraints.
- **Authors:** Wei-Liang Xu, Qing-Wen Tang, Long Li
- **arXiv:** `2611.04227v1` (published 2026-11-03 03:06:07 UTC; categories: `astro-ph.HE`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** Long-baseline Fermi-LAT data combined with radio, CO, and published X-ray constraints; the preprint says it is accepted for publication in MNRAS and reports a 3.4σ preference for spectral curvature.
- **Claimed result:** The authors detect an extended source with R68≈0.84°; their adopted multiwavelength model favors a hadronic contribution over a purely leptonic one, while also finding a proton-dominated alternative competitive.
- **Evidence type:** Observational; 17 years of Fermi-LAT data plus radio and CO observations and published X-ray constraints, interpreted with spectral-energy-distribution models.
- **Caveat:** The leptonic/hadronic partition is model-dependent; the Gaussian and uniform-disc morphologies are statistically indistinguishable, and the proton cutoff is fixed by assumption.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, Wednesday 4 November 2026 batch
- **Themes:** Astrophysics; Particle, nuclear & high-energy physics

### 5. [UV spectra of little red dots: Broad-line N/C, gas recycling and black hole masses](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05349)
- **Abstract:** JWST SPURS ultraviolet spectra reveal predicted broad emission and metal absorption features in several little red dots, which the authors interpret with radiative-transfer models of black holes embedded in dense gas.
- **Authors:** Albert Sneppen, Darach Watson, Lucy R. Ivey, Kasper E. Heintz, Alex J. Cameron et al.
- **arXiv:** `2611.05349v1` (published 2026-11-03 18:49:19 UTC; categories: `astro-ph.GA`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** Direct JWST spectral features test predictions of the Sirocco cocoon models; the paper combines emission-line ratios and absorption-line velocities to constrain abundance, density, and gas kinematics.
- **Claimed result:** For SPURS-GN-2 at z=7.19, the authors infer broad-line N/C = 12 (+3/−2) times solar and log ne = 9.7 (+0.3/−0.5); they interpret the absorption-line velocity ordering as stratified gas slowed by gravity.
- **Evidence type:** Observational; deep JWST SPURS spectroscopy of four LRDs, interpreted with Sirocco radiative-transfer simulations.
- **Caveat:** The sample is four objects, and the authors warn that strongly saturated absorption lines make direct abundance-ratio inference misleading; the physical interpretation is model-dependent.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, Wednesday 4 November 2026 batch
- **Themes:** Astrophysics; Computational & data methods

### 6. [Quantum Error Correction in Nearly Critical Toric Codes](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05375)
- **Abstract:** The authors study decoding in a transverse-field toric code near its transition out of the topological phase and find finite thresholds for bit- and phase-flip decoherence using stabilizer measurements.
- **Authors:** Zack Weinstein, Samuel J. Garratt
- **arXiv:** `2611.05375v1` (published 2026-11-03 18:59:47 UTC; categories: `cond-mat.stat-mech`, `quant-ph`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** Cross-listed between condensed matter and quantum physics; the work combines a proof about stabilizer measurements with numerically constructed optimal post-measurement decoders and reports thresholds arbitrarily close to criticality.
- **Claimed result:** For phase-flip decoherence, the authors prove the optimal post-measurement threshold is independent of transverse-field strength throughout the topological phase; near the critical point, they numerically demonstrate finite thresholds for both error types.
- **Evidence type:** Theoretical and computational; transverse-field toric-code analysis, constrained lattice-gauge Monte Carlo, and numerical decoding.
- **Caveat:** The claim that stabilizer measurements are an optimal decoding strategy is supported by theoretical evidence but not a rigorous proof; no hardware demonstration is reported.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, Wednesday 4 November 2026 batch
- **Themes:** Quantum information; Condensed matter & materials; Computational & data methods

### 7. [Beyond just a torus? Re-contextualising the AGN type paradigm using data-driven host galaxy quenching histories](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05353)

- **Evidence for ranking:** Accepted for publication in MNRAS; uses MFICA decomposition on a large, redshift- and luminosity-matched SDSS DR7 sample to test whether AGN type tracks host-galaxy star-formation history.
- **Claimed result:** The authors estimate that roughly 10% of the matched hosts rapidly quenched after a starburst within the last ≲1 Gyr; Type-2 hosts in that subset quenched at most 100 Myr more recently than Type-1 hosts.
- **Evidence type:** Observational; SDSS DR7 optical spectra of approximately 3,000 matched AGN host galaxies, analyzed with mean-field independent component analysis.
- **Caveat:** The authors give several possible explanations for the age difference, including torus covering factor, host obscuration, or AGN-type evolution; the matched observational result does not establish causality.

## Trending Research Themes

- **Survey cosmology is extracting more than two-point information.** The DES Y3 paper adds an integrated three-point weak-lensing statistic through simulation-based inference; the DESI DR1 paper estimates the ultraviolet background from quasar proximity profiles and uses Astrid mocks to calibrate systematics. These are different measurements and datasets, not independent confirmations of one cosmological result.
- **Precision inference depends on controlling nuisance structure.** BaBar’s ππ/μμ ratio is designed to cancel shared cross-section systematics, while DESI explicitly models quasar-environment absorption and reports a large systematic term. The DES lensing analysis likewise forward-models intrinsic alignments, baryonic feedback, redshift uncertainty, and shear calibration.
- **Long-baseline and multiwavelength observations are being turned into physical histories.** Fermi-LAT’s 17-year γ-ray data are combined with radio and CO observations; JWST SPURS line ratios probe the gas around early black holes; and SN 2019qem’s spectra plus optical/infrared light curves constrain pre-explosion mass loss. The astrophysical interpretations remain model-dependent in all three cases.
- **Quantum-information theory is borrowing tractable classical structure.** The near-critical toric-code study maps decoding to constrained lattice-gauge simulations; more broadly, the DES and DESI papers also rely on simulation-based inference, though their physical questions and computational methods are distinct.

## Open Problems and Research Directions

- **DES lensing — author-stated:** The paper reports at most 2.4σ difference from Planck 2018+BAO in its joint parameter space and notes its dependence on a detailed forward model. **Research direction (synthesis):** test the learned likelihood and nuisance model on independent lensing catalogues and Stage-IV-like simulations before interpreting cross-probe differences. [2611.05367]
- **DESI ultraviolet background — author-stated:** the systematic uncertainty from the quasar environment/clustering model is much larger than the statistical error. **Research direction (synthesis):** improve independent quasar-bias measurements and compare the proximity-effect estimate with other UV-background probes over matched redshift ranges. [2611.04316]
- **Fermi-LAT remnant — author-stated:** the hadronic/leptonic partition remains ambiguous, and alternative spatial templates are not decisively separated. **Research direction (synthesis):** obtain deeper gas maps and higher-resolution spectral/morphological constraints, then refit the competing particle-emission models without fixing the proton cutoff by assumption. [2611.04227]
- **Little red dots — author-stated:** saturated UV absorption limits direct abundance inference, and the study covers four objects. **Research direction (synthesis):** expand the JWST sample and combine transitions with different optical depths to test the inferred N/C enrichment and gas stratification. [2611.05349]
- **Near-critical toric codes — author-stated:** optimality of stabilizer measurements is not rigorously proved. **Research direction (synthesis):** seek analytic bounds on the bit-flip threshold and finite-size scaling, then test the decoder under realistic measurement noise. [2611.05375]
- **SN 2019qem — author-stated:** light curves alone cannot disentangle circumstellar density from shock velocity. **Research direction (synthesis):** use additional late-time spectroscopy and independent constraints on wind speed to test the inferred ≈9-solar-mass eruption. [2611.05328]
- **BaBar cross section — research direction (synthesis):** compare the new ratio-based measurement with independent e⁺e⁻ and lattice-QCD HVP determinations using consistent correlations; this single energy interval is an input, not a full muon-anomaly resolution. [2611.04596]
