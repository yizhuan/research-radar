### 1. [Construction of the 2D non-Abelian Yang-Mills-Higgs measure via stochastic quantisation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03640)
- **Abstract:** The authors construct the two-dimensional non-Abelian Yang-Mills-Higgs measure through a gauge-covariant stochastic-quantisation approximation whose limiting Langevin flow has a unique invariant measure.
- **Authors:** Bjoern Bringmann, Sky Cao, Martin Hairer, Wenhao Zhao
- **arXiv:** `2611.03640v1` (published 2026-11-02T18:58:13Z; categories: `math.AP`, `math-ph`, `math.PR`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** The authors claim a first construction for this non-Abelian model; the result combines global well-posedness, uniform-in-time bounds, and uniqueness of an invariant law, and it is cross-listed across analysis, probability, and mathematical physics. A Warwick probability-seminar talk on 28 October independently discussed the construction before the arXiv submission.
- **Claimed result:** The authors prove global well-posedness of the stochastic Yang-Mills-Higgs flow on gauge orbits over the two-dimensional torus and construct its unique invariant probability measure; the introduction identifies that measure as the sought-after field-theory construction.
- **Assumptions and setting:** A two-dimensional torus, a connected compact Lie group with an invariant inner product, and a finite-dimensional orthogonal Higgs representation with an invariant symmetric potential operator; the result is non-perturbative for fixed coupling constants.
- **Caveat:** The theorem is specific to dimension two and the stated gauge/Higgs setting; it does not establish global well-posedness or measure construction in three dimensions.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, 2026-11-03 announcement batch
- **Themes:** Analysis & PDE; Probability & statistics; Mathematical physics

### 2. [A proof of the spectral rigidity for complex projective space](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03536)
- **Abstract:** The authors claim that the spectrum of the Hodge Laplacian in any one fixed degree determines complex projective space, up to holomorphic isometry, among compact connected Kähler manifolds.
- **Authors:** Kefeng Liu, Hao Xu
- **arXiv:** `2611.03536v1` (published 2026-11-02T18:01:00Z; categories: `math.DG`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** It addresses a stated folklore inverse-spectral conjecture and strengthens the scalar-spectrum statement to every fixed degree `0 ≤ p ≤ n`; the introduction gives a theorem and proof route covering the exceptional Pell-sequence cases.
- **Claimed result:** For each fixed pair of integers `(p,n)` with `0 ≤ p ≤ n`, equality, including multiplicities, of the degree-`p` Hodge spectrum with that of Fubini–Study `CP^n` forces holomorphic isometry. The authors' proof combines spectral, wave-trace, topological, and curvature arguments.
- **Assumptions and setting:** The comparison manifold is compact, connected, Kähler, and without boundary; the hypothesis is exact equality of the full spectrum in one fixed form degree, including multiplicities.
- **Caveat:** The result concerns exact full spectra in the Kähler class, not partial spectra, approximate isospectrality, or arbitrary Riemannian manifolds.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, 2026-11-03 announcement batch
- **Themes:** Geometry & topology

### 3. [Local scaling limit of the four-dimensional uniform spanning tree](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03582)
- **Abstract:** The author proves that the uniform spanning tree of `Z^4`, viewed from the origin, converges locally after specified logarithmically corrected rescalings to a spatial Brownian Kesten tree, with a related super-Brownian occupation-measure limit.
- **Authors:** Runsheng Liu
- **arXiv:** `2611.03582v1` (published 2026-11-02T18:32:13Z; categories: `math.PR`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** The paper supplies a local scaling limit in the critical four-dimensional setting and jointly tracks intrinsic distance, volume, and Euclidean embedding; its introduction places it against previously missing local-limit results and derives sharp first-moment asymptotics.
- **Claimed result:** The author proves local rooted spatial Gromov-weak convergence to an infinite Brownian Kesten tree, and convergence of the rescaled space-time occupation measure to infinite canonical super-Brownian motion.
- **Assumptions and setting:** The wired uniform spanning forest on `Z^4`, rooted at the origin, with intrinsic metric, vertex volume, and spatial labels rescaled by the paper's dimension-specific constants and logarithmic factors.
- **Caveat:** The theorem is for the four-dimensional lattice and this local topology; the introduction notes that two scaling constants are not individually known explicitly, although their product is identified.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, 2026-11-03 announcement batch
- **Themes:** Probability & statistics

### 4. [Near-optimal bounds for the critical 2d SHF and the delta-Bose gas](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03191)
- **Abstract:** The authors improve the upper bound on the `N`-th moment of the critical two-dimensional stochastic heat flow to `exp(exp(C N^{1+o(1)}))`, close to a known lower bound, and give a fixed-`N` spectral formula for the delta-Bose ground-state energy.
- **Authors:** Xue-Mei Li, Te-Chun Wang
- **arXiv:** `2611.03191v1` (published 2026-11-02T14:21:03Z; categories: `math.PR`, `math-ph`, `math.FA`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** The abstract quantifies an improvement from an `N^2` exponent to `N^{1+o(1)}`, compares it with an existing lower bound, and connects the estimate to a prediction for the two-dimensional delta-Bose gas; it is cross-listed across probability, mathematical physics, and functional analysis.
- **Claimed result:** The authors establish the exponential scale of the ground-state energy predicted by Rajeev's conjecture up to a vanishing error in the exponent, and derive, for each fixed `N`, a formula in terms of the bottom of a principal-operator spectrum.
- **Assumptions and setting:** Critical two-dimensional stochastic heat flow and the two-dimensional `N`-body delta-Bose gas; the spectral formula is stated for each fixed `N`.
- **Caveat:** The result reaches the predicted scale only up to a vanishing error in the exponent; the abstract does not claim a complete resolution of Rajeev's conjecture.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, 2026-11-03 announcement batch
- **Themes:** Probability & statistics; Mathematical physics; Analysis & PDE

### 5. [The proof of the Harary--Hill conjecture: a detailed exposition and an extension to rotation systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03515)
- **Abstract:** The author presents a combinatorial proof of the Harary--Hill crossing-number conjecture and proves a broader weighted lower bound for four-vertex subsystems of every rotation system of `K_n`.
- **Authors:** Gelasio Salazar
- **arXiv:** `2611.03515v1` (published 2026-11-02T17:51:35Z; categories: `math.CO`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** It targets a longstanding extremal-combinatorics conjecture and states a general rotation-system inequality from which the conjectured crossing number follows. A separate October exposition and mathematical commentary discuss the underlying claimed proof; that attention is not evidence of peer review.
- **Claimed result:** For every rotation system of `K_n`, the number of four-vertex subsystems realizable as one-crossing drawings of `K_4`, plus three times the number of non-realizable subsystems, is at least `Z(n)`, the Harary--Hill lower-bound expression.
- **Assumptions and setting:** Complete graphs `K_n` for `n ≥ 3`; the main general statement is about rotation systems and four-vertex subsystems, rather than drawings themselves.
- **Caveat:** The preprint describes itself as an exposition of a short proof uploaded to GitHub in October 2026 while adding a broader combinatorial statement; this summary has not independently checked that proof.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, 2026-11-03 announcement batch
- **Themes:** Combinatorics & discrete mathematics

### 6. [Improved lower bounds for the kissing number in dimensions 49 to 61](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.01906)
- **Abstract:** The author gives explicit kissing configurations in every dimension from 49 through 61, improving the best previously published lower bound in all thirteen dimensions by a total of 506,461 points.
- **Authors:** Dmitry Kamenetsky
- **arXiv:** `2611.01906v1` (published 2026-11-01T13:49:22Z; categories: `math.MG`, `cs.IT`, `math.CO`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** The improvement covers thirteen dimensions and uses a stated combination of lattice-shell lifts, local search, and maximum-coverage optimization; the arXiv record says exact-integer certificates and code/data are provided.
- **Claimed result:** The author reports improved lower bounds in each dimension `49 ≤ d ≤ 61`, with margins from 232 to 159,220 points and exact integer verification of the listed configurations.
- **Assumptions and setting:** Euclidean kissing configurations in dimensions 49–61, constructed from a shell of the extremal even unimodular lattice `P_48p` and lower-dimensional antipodal configurations.
- **Caveat:** These are lower bounds, not exact kissing numbers; the computational certificate claim does not establish optimality.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, 2026-11-03 announcement batch
- **Themes:** Geometry & topology; Combinatorics & discrete mathematics

### 7. [Quantitative finiteness for maximal complex totally geodesic submanifolds of non-arithmetic ball quotients](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03514)
- **Abstract:** The authors bound the number of maximal complex totally geodesic submanifolds of a smooth finite-volume non-arithmetic ball quotient linearly in its volume, with a constant depending only on complex dimension.
- **Authors:** Tianzhi Hu, Runze Zhang
- **arXiv:** `2611.03514v1` (published 2026-11-02T17:51:10Z; categories: `math.AG`, `math.DG`, `math.DS`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** It upgrades previously published finiteness theorems, as identified by the authors, to a volume-order bound and connects algebraic geometry, differential geometry, and dynamics by cross-listing.
- **Claimed result:** For a smooth finite-volume non-arithmetic complex ball quotient, the number of maximal complex totally geodesic submanifolds is at most a dimension-dependent constant times the volume.
- **Assumptions and setting:** Smooth finite-volume non-arithmetic ball quotients; the implied constant depends on complex dimension.
- **Caveat:** The abstract does not state an explicit value for the dimension-dependent constant, and the theorem's non-arithmetic and smoothness hypotheses delimit its scope.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, 2026-11-03 announcement batch
- **Themes:** Geometry & topology

### 8. [Stability of negative-regularity mixing and applications to stochastic fluids](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03519)

- **arXiv:** `2611.03519v1` (published 2026-11-02T17:54:30Z; categories: `math.PR`, `math.AP`, `math.DS`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** It supplies an abstract stability result with applications to active and passive scalar equations and enhanced dissipation, and its cross-listing links probability, PDE, and dynamics.
- **Claimed result:** The authors establish perturbation stability of negative-regularity mixing and apply it to a hyperviscous Boussinesq-type system, a passively advected scalar for stochastically forced two-dimensional Navier--Stokes, and an implication from such mixing to enhanced dissipation.
- **Assumptions and setting:** Scalars advected by random vector fields; the applications use the specific systems and smooth-in-time stochastic forcing described in the paper.
- **Caveat:** The abstract's applications are to these stated systems; it does not claim a universal mixing theorem for all stochastic fluid equations.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, 2026-11-03 announcement batch

## Trending Research Themes

- **Stochastic dynamics and field models:** Bringmann et al. construct an invariant measure for a two-dimensional gauge-field/Higgs SPDE, Li and Wang sharpen moment/energy scales for a critical stochastic heat model, and Agresti et al. transfer mixing stability to stochastic-fluid applications. These are different techniques and goals, not evidence of one shared method.
- **Geometry constrained by global data:** Liu and Xu use one full Hodge spectrum to claim holomorphic rigidity of `CP^n`; Hu and Zhang bound geometric submanifolds using quotient volume. This is a small cross-paper signal, with inverse spectral rigidity and volume counting remaining distinct mechanisms.
- **Critical settings yield sharper structure:** Liu's `Z^4` spanning-tree result identifies a local continuum limit with logarithmic rescalings; Li and Wang improve the critical 2D stochastic-heat moment scale to near the known lower bound. The shared feature is a critical regime with refined quantitative conclusions, not a common technique.
- **Explicit discrete/geometric bounds:** Salazar states a rotation-system inequality for the Harary--Hill problem, while Kamenetsky reports certified improvements to kissing-number lower bounds. These are separate combinatorial and metric-geometric signals, not evidence of a broad methodological shift.

## Open Problems and Research Directions

- **Reported boundary (Bringmann et al.):** The result treats the two-dimensional torus. The introduction notes that earlier local well-posedness work in dimensions two and three left finite-time blow-up unresolved; extending global control and a corresponding invariant measure to three dimensions is an author-contextual open direction, not a result of this preprint.
- **Author-stated limitation (Li and Wang):** Their estimate reaches Rajeev's predicted exponential scale only up to a vanishing error in the exponent. A concrete follow-up is to sharpen that error enough to resolve the remaining conjectural asymptotics, including how the fixed-`N` principal-operator formula behaves as `N` grows.
- **Author-stated limitation (Liu):** Two scaling constants for the `Z^4` UST limit are not individually explicit, although their product is identified. Determining either constant or obtaining quantitative convergence rates would refine the limit theorem.
- **Verification direction (Salazar):** Independently formalize or check the earlier GitHub proof and this preprint's rotation-system inequality, and distinguish which steps are new to the generalization. This is a validation task, not a claim that the conjecture remains open.
- **Proposed extension (Kamenetsky):** The paper gives lower bounds, not matching upper bounds. Test whether stronger upper bounds can close any of the gaps in dimensions 49–61, using the reported configurations as explicit candidates; the arXiv-linked code repository was not available during this check.
- **Proposed generalization (Liu and Xu; Hu and Zhang):** Explore whether the spectral rigidity persists for partial or approximate spectral data, and whether the ball-quotient count admits an effective constant or broader hypotheses. These are synthesis directions beyond the stated theorems.
