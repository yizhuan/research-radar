### 2. Renormalization-Guided Cascade Upscaling for Lattice Field Generation
- **Authors:** Anna Hasenfratz, Ethan T. Neil, Letizia Parato, Noah Schwartz
- **arXiv ID:** [2608.28581v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28581) (Companion: [2608.28580v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28580)) (New submission)
- **Primary / Cross Categories:** `hep-lat` (High Energy Physics - Lattice)
- **Submission Date:** 2026-08-28 | **Announcement Batch:** Latest daily cycle
- **Status / Corroboration:** Submitted to *Phys. Rev. Lett.* (with 24-page PRD validation paper)
- **Physical System / Question:** Mitigating critical slowing down and sampling bottlenecks in non-perturbative lattice field configuration generation.
- **Result & Evidence:** Developed a machine learning framework combining Renormalization Group (RG) "perfect blocking" for equilibrated infrared modes with conditional normalizing flows to reconstruct ultraviolet fluctuations, demonstrating scale-invariant training and accelerated sampling on gauge configurations.
- **Limitations & Caveats:** Computational; scaling efficiency to four-dimensional dynamical fermions with non-Abelian gauge groups ($SU(3)$ QCD) remains to be demonstrated in production runs.
- **Ranking Signals:** Two companion papers submitted to PRL/PRD, direct physical bridge between Wilsonian RG and modern generative neural architectures.

### 4. How Long-Range Tails Reshape Non-Hermitian Spectra
- **Authors:** Ding Gu, Zhanpeng Fu, Yu-Min Hu, Zhong Wang
- **arXiv ID:** [2608.28577v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28577) (New submission)
- **Primary / Cross Categories:** `quant-ph`, `cond-mat.mes-hall`, `cond-mat.quant-gas`, `physics.optics`
- **Submission Date:** 2026-08-28 | **Announcement Batch:** Latest daily cycle
- **Physical System / Question:** Non-Hermitian skin effect and spectral sensitivity to long-range hopping tails in open-boundary quantum and optical lattices.
- **Result & Evidence:** Proves analytically and numerically that exponentially decaying long-range couplings, often truncated in tight-binding approximations, cause non-perturbative restructuring of the non-Hermitian spectrum and skin modes under open boundaries.
- **Limitations & Caveats:** Theoretical; assumes non-interacting single-particle Hamiltonians; many-body interactions and finite dissipation rates could modify the spectral response.
- **Ranking Signals:** Broad cross-listing (4 subfields: quant-ph, mes-hall, quant-gas, optics), fundamental theoretical significance for non-Hermitian topological systems.
