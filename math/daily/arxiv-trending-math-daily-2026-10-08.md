### 1. [The Multiple Unicast Conjecture is False](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.10108)
- **Abstract:** The authors construct an undirected network where a deterministic linear code over F₉ beats every fractional multicommodity flow, refuting the multiple-unicast conjecture.[2][3]
- **Authors:** Mark Braverman, Zhongtian He
- **arXiv:** `2610.10108v1` (published 2026-10-07 13:59:24 UTC; categories: `cs.IT` primary, `cs.CC`, `cs.DM`, `cs.DS`; included in the latest math listing)
- **Evidence for ranking:** A concrete counterexample to a central conjecture, with a quantified coding-versus-flow gap and a logarithmically growing family; the paper also links to data, a symbolic verifier, and a simulator.[2][3]
- **Claimed result:** The preprint gives a 182-vertex bipartite graph over the point-line incidence structure of PG(2,9) supporting 157 unicast sessions at common coding rate at least 1, while fractional flow is at most 147/157; an amplification theorem yields an Ω((log n)^ε) gap for some ε>0.[2][3]
- **Assumptions and setting:** Undirected multiple-unicast network coding, a deterministic linear code over F₉, and comparison with fractional multicommodity flow.[2]
- **Caveat:** This is an unrefereed v1. The authors disclose that GPT-6 helped find the nondeterministic counterexample from which they built the causal code; independent checking of the construction and verifier remains important.[3]
- **Announcement type:** new submission, 8 October 2026 batch
- **Themes:** Combinatorics & discrete mathematics

### 2. [The Exact Overlaps Conjecture for Self-Similar Measures on the Real Line](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.10511)
- **Abstract:** For finitely supported systems of contracting similarities on the real line, the authors prove the dimension formula for the associated self-similar measure in terms of random-walk entropy and the Lyapunov exponent.[4][5]
- **Authors:** Samuel Kittle, Constantin Kogler
- **arXiv:** `2610.10511v1` (published 2026-10-07 17:52:17 UTC; categories: `math.DS`, `math.PR`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** An explicit resolution of a central dimension conjecture, with a new entropy inequality and relevance to both dynamical systems and probability.[4][5]
- **Claimed result:** The authors prove dim ν = min{1, hμ/|χμ|} for self-similar measures generated by finitely many contracting similarities of ℝ, and derive the expected Hausdorff-dimension formula for the attractor when there are no exact overlaps.[4][5]
- **Assumptions and setting:** A probability measure supported on finitely many contracting similarities of ℝ; the result is one-dimensional and uses the random-walk entropy hμ and Lyapunov exponent χμ.[5]
- **Caveat:** The introduction notes that an independent proof was posted in another paper on 6 October; readers should compare the proofs and scopes. The preprint itself is not a proof verification.[5]
- **Announcement type:** new submission, 8 October 2026 batch
- **Themes:** Probability & statistics; Geometry & topology

### 3. [Mixing Times of Switch Chains via High-Dimensional Expansion](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.09506)
- **Abstract:** The paper uses high-dimensional expansion to bound the mixing time of the switch chain that samples labelled graphs with a prescribed degree sequence.[6]
- **Authors:** Sawyer Jack Robertson
- **arXiv:** `2610.09506v1` (published 2026-10-07 06:05:44 UTC; categories: `math.PR`, `cs.DM`, `math.CO`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** It states a quantitative mixing bound, extends the conclusion to irregular degree sequences, and claims resolution of a longstanding fixed-maximum-degree conjecture.[6]
- **Claimed result:** For m edges and maximum prescribed degree Δ, the classical and simplicial switch chains mix in O(Δ²m log m) steps whenever m ≥ CΔ⁸; for fixed Δ this gives O(n log n) on n-vertex sequences.[6]
- **Assumptions and setting:** Uniform sampling over labelled realizations of a graphical degree sequence, subject to m ≥ CΔ⁸ for an absolute constant C.[6]
- **Caveat:** The stated guarantee requires a strong m-to-Δ condition; it does not settle comparable bounds for all degree sequences or the parameter ranges outside that hypothesis.[6]
- **Announcement type:** new submission, 8 October 2026 batch
- **Themes:** Probability & statistics; Combinatorics & discrete mathematics

### 4. [Abundance for threefolds in positive characteristic](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.10439)
- **Abstract:** The authors claim the abundance conjecture for projective log-canonical threefold pairs over perfect fields of characteristic greater than 42.[8][9]
- **Authors:** Fabio Bernasconi, Jakub Witaszek, Zheng Xu
- **arXiv:** `2610.10439v1` (published 2026-10-07 17:15:36 UTC; categories: `math.AG`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** The stated theorem addresses a major remaining case in three-dimensional positive-characteristic birational geometry and completes the conjecture within its stated characteristic range.[8][9]
- **Claimed result:** For a projective lc threefold pair (X,B) over a perfect field of characteristic p>42, nefness of KX+B implies that KX+B is semi-ample.[8][9]
- **Assumptions and setting:** Dimension three; projective lc pair over a perfect field; characteristic p>42; KX+B nef.[9]
- **Caveat:** The authors call this version “very preliminary,” say they will continue revising it, and rely on bounds from a companion paper not yet published; it proves only the p>42 range, not the broader p>3 claim mentioned in the paper’s motivation.[9]
- **Announcement type:** new submission, 8 October 2026 batch
- **Themes:** Geometry & topology

### 5. [Sharp Hilbert transform norms on Schatten classes: classification and optimal spectral corrections](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.10158)
- **Abstract:** The author gives sharp norm formulas for Hilbert transforms on Schatten-valued Lp spaces and related Schur multipliers, reporting resolutions of several conjectures.[7]
- **Authors:** Zhouyu Long

- **Claimed result:** For 1<p<∞, the paper claims norm Cp = cot(π/(2p*)) for the Hilbert transform on Lp(ℝ; Sp) and the ordered Hilbert Schur multiplier on Sp, where p*=max{p,p/(p−1)}; it also reports resolving the Gohberg–Krupnik, Riesz–Titchmarsh, and Laeng conjectures.[7]
- **Assumptions and setting:** Schatten-p-valued functions and the stated ordered Schur multiplier, for 1<p<∞; the norm and normalization are those defined in the preprint.[7]
- **Caveat:** The claims are from a new preprint; the operator definitions, normalization, and proofs need specialist review before treating the sharp identities as settled.[7]
- **Announcement type:** new submission, 8 October 2026 batch

### 6. [The circle packing and Riemann uniformization embedding of the tree-weighted planar maps converges to Liouville quantum gravity](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.10505)
- **Abstract:** The authors prove scaling limits for spanning-tree-weighted planar maps under circle-packing and Riemann-uniformization embeddings, with related path convergence to SLE₈.[11]

- **Claimed result:** As the number of faces grows, the embedded tree-weighted planar maps converge to √2-LQG disk, sphere, or cone, respectively; the natural path on faces converges to SLE₈.[11]
- **Assumptions and setting:** Spanning-tree-weighted planar maps in the stated disk, sphere, and whole-plane topologies, under circle-packing or Riemann-uniformization embeddings.[11]
- **Caveat:** The proof builds on earlier circle-packing and random-map results and a companion comparison paper; the result concerns this tree-weighted model, not arbitrary planar maps.[11]
- **Announcement type:** new submission, 8 October 2026 batch
- **Themes:** Probability & statistics; Geometry & topology; Mathematical physics

### 7. [A proof of the fractional Lane--Emden conjecture](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.10188)
- **Abstract:** The authors prove a Liouville nonexistence result for positive entire solutions of a fractional Lane–Emden system in the strict subcritical range.[10]
- **Authors:** Linfeng Mei, Juncheng Wei
- **arXiv:** `2610.10188v1` (published 2026-10-07 14:53:43 UTC; categories: `math.AP`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** The result claims to settle a conjecture across all fractional orders 0<s<1 and avoids radial-symmetry, boundedness, finite-energy, and prescribed-decay assumptions.[10]
- **Claimed result:** For n≥2, 0<s<1, p,q>0, the system (−Δ)^s u=v^p, (−Δ)^s v=u^q has no strictly positive entire solution when 1/(p+1)+1/(q+1)>(n−2s)/n.[10]
- **Assumptions and setting:** Positive entire solutions on ℝⁿ, n≥2, fractional order 0<s<1, and positive exponents p,q satisfying the strict inequality.[10]
- **Caveat:** The theorem is limited to the strict inequality; the abstract makes no claim at the critical boundary or in the complementary range.[10]
- **Announcement type:** new submission, 8 October 2026 batch
- **Themes:** Analysis & PDE

- **Claimed result:** For binomial random subsets of vertices of a locally sparse hypergraph, the paper gives logarithmic asymptotics for the probability that the subset is independent; in the graph clique-avoidance setting, conditioned graphs approach optimizing graphons in cut distance.[12]

## Trending Research Themes

- **Dimension questions for self-similar measures:** The exact-overlaps paper gives a general one-dimensional entropy/Lyapunov formula, while the quartic Salem paper proves a strict correlation-dimension drop for a special Bernoulli-convolution family; these are related questions, not a shared proof method.[4][5][13]
- **Random discrete structures and sharp asymptotics:** The switch-chain paper uses high-dimensional expansion to obtain rapid mixing under a degree condition, while the hypergraph paper uses entropy and a guided random-greedy method for critical independent-set probabilities.[6][12]
- **Sharp analytic inequalities and PDE:** The Hilbert-transform paper seeks exact operator norms and the fractional Lane–Emden paper proves a nonexistence theorem; both are sharp analytic results but concern different objects and techniques.[7][10]
- **Discrete-to-continuum geometry:** The tree-weighted planar-map result links combinatorial maps to √2-LQG and SLE₈ under conformal embeddings; this is a distinct signal rather than a theme represented by several papers in this selection.[11]
- **Birational geometry:** The positive-characteristic abundance preprint makes a single high-impact theorem claim, but its authors explicitly label the version preliminary, so it should be read as a developing result rather than evidence of a broad field-wide shift.[8][9]

## Open Problems and Research Directions

- **Network-coding gap:** Independently reproduce the 182-vertex code and verifier, then determine whether the Ω((log n)^ε) lower bound can approach the known O(log n) upper bound.[2][3]
- **Beyond the one-dimensional overlap theorem:** Test which parts of the entropy argument extend to higher-dimensional similarities without the paper’s virtually-solvable rotation-group restriction; the authors identify this as a boundary of the approach.[5]
- **Positive-characteristic abundance:** Check the preliminary proof and its companion index bounds, then investigate whether the characteristic threshold can be lowered from p>42 toward the remaining p>3 case.[9]
- **Switch-chain parameter range:** Seek mixing bounds under weaker conditions than m≥CΔ⁸, especially when the maximum degree grows with the graph size.[6]
- **Critical and supercritical PDE regimes:** The fractional Lane–Emden result leaves the equality case and the complementary exponent range open for further existence/nonexistence analysis.[10]
- **Generalizing the Salem result:** Explore whether collision-probability methods yield quantitative dimension gaps for higher-degree Salem parameters or broader families of Bernoulli convolutions.[13]
