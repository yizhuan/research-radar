## Top papers (ranked)

### 1. [Teleportation of Logical Qubits using Lattice Surgery with Superconducting Circuits](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23165)
- **Abstract:** The authors experimentally teleport logical states between two distance-three surface-code qubits on a 41-physical-qubit superconducting circuit and find that repeated stabilizer measurements after merging patches restore fault tolerance and improve fidelity.
- **Authors:** Luca Hofele, Yongxin Song, Áron Márton, Ian Hesner, Ilya Besedin, et al.
- **arXiv:** `2611.23165v1` (published 2026-11-17T18:17:01Z; categories: `quant-ph` [primary])
- **Evidence for ranking:** Strongest direct attention signal found in this batch: the paper appeared in SciRate’s daily top-paper listing and received independent coverage from [Quantum Zeitgeist](https://quantumzeitgeist.com/logical-qubits-teleported-with-41-physical-qubits/). The experiment also reports a concrete fault-tolerant lattice-surgery operation rather than a forecast.
- **Claimed result:** The authors report logical teleportation of Z- and X-eigenstates with success probabilities up to 0.65 and 0.73, respectively, and observe that repeated stabilizer measurements restore fault tolerance after the merge step.
- **Evidence type:** experimental; lattice-surgery operations on two distance-three surface-code logical qubits in a 41-physical-qubit superconducting circuit.
- **Caveat:** This is a small-code demonstration, not evidence of logical-error suppression at larger code distances; the authors identify remaining hardware noise and suggest extending the method to arbitrary-state teleportation and full tomography.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, announcement batch 2026-11-17
- **Themes:** Quantum information; Instrumentation & detectors

### 2. [Observation of temporal routing of topological edge states](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.22199)
- **Abstract:** In a coupled nonlinear Su-Schrieffer-Heeger circuit lattice, the authors observe edge-state exchange slow with increasing excitation and eventually localize, demonstrating temporal routing in physical time.
- **Authors:** Letian Xu, Muhammad Imran, Yongtao Jia, Ying Liu, Huibin Tao, Rujiang Li
- **arXiv:** `2611.22199v1` (published 2026-11-17T04:38:26Z; categories: `nlin.PS` [primary], `cond-mat.mes-hall`, `physics.optics`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** Cross-listed across nonlinear physics, condensed matter, and optics; the arXiv comment says it is to be published in Physical Letters A, and a [publisher record](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0375960126010492) is available. It is a direct experimental report on temporal dynamics rather than a spatial-to-time analogy.
- **Claimed result:** The authors observe a progression from periodic interchain exchange to slowed exchange and then localization as excitation voltage rises, attributing the routing behavior to nonlinearity.
- **Evidence type:** experimental; temporal voltage evolution in an electrical circuit lattice of two coupled nonlinear SSH chains.
- **Caveat:** The observed dynamics are limited to a finite time window by circuit dissipation, which constrains how long the routing can be followed.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, announcement batch 2026-11-17
- **Themes:** Condensed matter & materials; Instrumentation & detectors

### 3. [Properties of Chromium and Manganese in Tycho's SNR with XRISM/Resolve](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.22471)
- **Abstract:** XRISM/Resolve spectra spatially resolve chromium and manganese emission in Tycho’s Type Ia supernova remnant, constraining its ejecta and progenitor models while leaving multiple explosion scenarios viable.
- **Authors:** R. Giuffrida, A. Decourchelle, L. Godinaud, T. Tanaka, G. Ferrand, et al.
- **arXiv:** `2611.22471v1` (published 2026-11-17T09:30:32Z; categories: `astro-ph.HE` [primary])
- **Evidence for ranking:** The arXiv comment reports acceptance in Astronomy & Astrophysics. The result uses XRISM/Resolve’s high spectral resolution to constrain elemental abundances and spatial distributions in a specific remnant.
- **Claimed result:** The authors find Cr and Mn lines strongest in the Fe-bright northeast and northwest regions; measured mass ratios are compatible with more than one one-dimensional explosion model, though the best-fitting models imply progenitor metallicities they judge too high for Tycho’s location.
- **Evidence type:** observational; spatially resolved X-ray spectroscopy of Tycho’s supernova remnant with XRISM/Resolve, interpreted using plasma and explosion models.
- **Caveat:** The northern and southern regions have limited exposure: chromium there is only marginal at 90% confidence, and low counts do not support firm conclusions; model choices also leave the progenitor scenario ambiguous.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, announcement batch 2026-11-17
- **Themes:** Astrophysics; Instrumentation & detectors

### 4. [oMEGACat-JWST I.: Project description and photometric data release](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23150)
- **Abstract:** The authors release a near-infrared JWST photometric and astrometric catalog of Omega Centauri containing about 677,000 sources, including more than 86,000 below HST’s detection limit.
- **Authors:** Maximilian Häberle, Callie Clontz, Massimo Griggio, Mattia Libralato, Isabella Carlton, et al.
- **arXiv:** `2611.23150v1` (published 2026-11-17T18:03:37Z; categories: `astro-ph.GA` [primary], `astro-ph.SR`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** A substantial JWST data release with stated source counts, completeness, and astrometric precision; it is cross-listed in Galactic astronomy and stellar astrophysics and marked as submitted to Astronomy & Astrophysics.
- **Claimed result:** From NIRCam and NIRISS observations, the authors report over 90% recovery of overlapping HST sources, 86,000-plus sources fainter than HST’s detection limit, and single-image astrometric precision near 150 microarcseconds for bright sources.
- **Evidence type:** observational; custom reduction of JWST NIRCam and NIRISS imaging from three programs, cross-matched to HST catalog data.
- **Caveat:** Precision and completeness vary with brightness and field location; the paper reports localized edge deviations reaching about 4 mas, so the best quoted precision should not be assumed across the full catalog.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, announcement batch 2026-11-17
- **Themes:** Astrophysics; Instrumentation & detectors; Computational & data methods

- **arXiv:** `2611.23060v1` (published 2026-11-17T16:53:28Z; categories: `cond-mat.supr-con` [primary])

- **Evidence type:** experimental; spectroscopic-imaging scanning tunneling microscopy on Bi₂Sr₂CaCu₂O₈, analyzed with the authors’ dual-ANN mapping method.
- **Caveat:** The currents are inferred from spectroscopic maps through a model-based ANN mapping, rather than directly measured as current; independent validation with a complementary probe remains important.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, announcement batch 2026-11-17

## Trending Research Themes

- **Operations and temporal control in physical hardware:** Two distinct experiments explore controlled dynamics in coupled systems: logical information transfer via lattice surgery in a superconducting processor ([2611.23165](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23165)) and nonlinearity-controlled edge-state routing in an electrical SSH lattice ([2611.22199](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.22199)). This is a shared hardware/control motif, not evidence that either platform independently confirms the other’s result.
- **Instrument-led astrophysics and reusable data:** XRISM/Resolve resolves iron-group emission in Tycho’s remnant ([2611.22471](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.22471)); JWST NIRCam/NIRISS supplies a large astrometric and photometric Omega Centauri catalog ([2611.23150](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23150)). These are separate targets and instruments, but both turn improved spatial or spectral information into constraints on astrophysical models.
- **Model-assisted extraction of difficult signals:** The cuprate paper maps STM spectra to a supercurrent field using a dual ANN ([2611.23060](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23060)), while the XRISM analysis compares measured line ratios with explosion models ([2611.22471](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.22471)). These are different uses of inference; in both cases, conclusions depend partly on the mapping or physical model, not solely on raw observables.

## Open Problems and Research Directions

- **Author-stated — logical operations:** The 41-qubit study tests distance-three logical qubits and discusses extending the analysis to arbitrary-state teleportation and full tomography. **Research direction (synthesis):** repeat lattice-surgery transfers at larger code distances and test whether the fidelity gains persist as circuit depth and device size increase ([2611.23165](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23165)).
- **Author-stated — circuit dissipation:** The topological-routing observation is limited to a finite window by dissipation. **Research direction (synthesis):** lower circuit losses and vary the excitation range to determine how long the nonlinearity-controlled exchange/localization regimes remain observable ([2611.22199](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.22199)).
- **Author-stated — Tycho’s ejecta:** Low exposure prevents firm Cr/Mn conclusions in some regions, while several one-dimensional explosion scenarios remain viable. **Research direction (synthesis):** obtain deeper spatially resolved spectra and compare them with three-dimensional models spanning progenitor mass and neutronization; the authors point to future NewAthena/XIFU sensitivity as one route ([2611.22471](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.22471)).
- **Synthesis — cuprate current mapping:** Because the reported current field is inferred through a trained ANN and physical mapping, validate it on held-out spectra, additional samples/dopings, and an independent current-sensitive measurement before treating the loop pattern as established ([2611.23060](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23060)).
- **Author-stated — Omega Centauri astrometry:** The catalog is intended to support future monitoring of fast central stars. **Research direction (synthesis):** combine additional JWST epochs with the long HST baseline to test the inferred motions and map field-dependent astrometric systematics ([2611.23150](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23150)).

## Takeaway

The clearest attention signal is a superconducting-circuit demonstration of logical-qubit teleportation; alongside it, a circuit-lattice experiment and two major astrophysical datasets show how better control and better instruments expose dynamics hidden to coarser methods. The cuprate current-loop report is potentially consequential but remains method-dependent and unconfirmed by an independent probe, so the list is best read as an evidence-filtered snapshot rather than a popularity chart.
