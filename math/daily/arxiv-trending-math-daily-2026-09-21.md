## Open Problems and Research Directions

- **Higher-degree dynamical Galois classification:** Ferraguti–Pagano describe a conjectural classification beyond the degree-less-than-characteristic regime, where Drinfeld-module examples appear. A natural direction is to test the proposed exceptional families in all degrees.
- **Sharpness and extensions of beta-splitting extremals:** Brandenberger–Chin–Mossel answer cited critical-model questions; extending the coding correspondence to noncritical beta parameters or other tree-growth models would test the scope of the method.
- **Beyond the stated polynomial regimes:** Jalowy’s limits assume regularity, small heat-flow time, or rotational symmetry. Quantitative rates and nonsymmetric laws are direct follow-ups identified by the paper’s stated generalization strategy.
- **Higher-dimensional knot probabilities:** Geisz–Peterson–Shonkwiler solve the six-point spherical case. Extending the method to more vertices, other ambient manifolds, or nonuniform point distributions would probe which exact-probability structure survives.
- **Algorithmic robustness of multiparameter contours:** Graf–Townsend’s method should be tested under clustered or defective eigenvalues, with explicit conditioning and error bounds beyond the reported numerical experiments.
