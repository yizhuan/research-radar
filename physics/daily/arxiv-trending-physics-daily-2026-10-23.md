## Top papers (ranked)

### 1. [Natural Chlorine Enhances Antineutrino Detection in the Deep Ocean](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30754)
- **Abstract:** The authors propose using naturally abundant seawater chlorine to tag inverse-beta-decay antineutrinos through a brighter, faster neutron-capture cascade, combining a detector simulation with a salted-water laboratory test.
- **Authors:** Ruike Cao, Iwan Morton-Blake, Donglian Xu, Qichao Chang, Shuhua Hao et al.
- **arXiv:** `2610.30754v1` (published 2026-10-22T05:22:34Z; categories: `hep-ex`, `astro-ph.IM`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** Included in neutrino physicist Mauricio Bustamante's daily arXiv picks; it pairs a detector-level simulation with a separate salted-water demonstration, giving it both an independent attention signal and a concrete experimental basis.
- **Claimed result:** The authors report correlated neutron-capture signatures in a salted-water test and estimate that chlorine tagging could extend TRIDENT's simulated core-collapse-supernova 5σ reach from about 50 kpc to over 100 kpc.
- **Evidence type:** Experimental; a China Jinping Underground Laboratory salted-water test, plus detector-level TRIDENT simulation.
- **Caveat:** In the salt-plus-background test the neutron-capture excess is about 2σ; the >100 kpc reach is simulated, and time-variable bioluminescence is not included in that sensitivity estimate.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, 2026-10-23 announcement batch
- **Themes:** Particle, nuclear & high-energy physics; Instrumentation & detectors

### 2. [Observation of Polarization Topology in Magneto-Optical BICs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31498)
- **Abstract:** The authors reconstruct the far-field polarization texture of a magneto-optical bound state in the continuum, finding a +1 topological charge whose surrounding handedness can be reversed by magnetic-bias tuning.
- **Authors:** Yaqi Li, Mingyu Tong, Fujia Chen, Li Zhang, Rui Zhao et al.
- **arXiv:** `2610.31498v1` (published 2026-10-22T16:17:07Z; categories: `physics.optics`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** A direct experimental resolution of a previously difficult nonreciprocal polarization texture, with a distinctive controlled reversal of handedness while the measured charge remains fixed.
- **Claimed result:** Using polarization-resolved angle-dependent reflection and singular-value decomposition, the authors measure a 2π winding (charge +1) and show opposite polarization handedness at two magnetic-bias strengths.
- **Evidence type:** Experimental; a fabricated magneto-optical photonic-crystal slab, reflection-matrix measurements, and a coupled-mode interpretation.
- **Caveat:** The apparatus cannot measure the central angular region (|θ| < 6°), so the topological charge is reconstructed from the surrounding accessible texture; the demonstration is on one device design.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, 2026-10-23 announcement batch
- **Themes:** Condensed matter & materials

### 3. [Measurements of the branching fractions of Λc+→ΛK+π0 and Λc+→ΛK+η decays at Belle and Belle II](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31377)
- **Abstract:** Using combined Belle and Belle II data, the collaborations measure two singly Cabibbo-suppressed charmed-baryon decays and report the first observation of Λc+→ΛK+η.
- **Authors:** M. Abumusabh, I. Adachi, A. Aggarwal, Y. Ahn, H. Aihara et al. (Belle and Belle II Collaborations)
- **arXiv:** `2610.31377v1` (published 2026-10-22T14:48:37Z; categories: `hep-ex`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** The first observation of a decay mode at greater than 10σ, using 1.55 ab−1 combined across two detector datasets; the related ΛK+π0 result substantially improves the prior precision.
- **Claimed result:** The authors measure B(Λc+→ΛK+η)/B(Λc+→Λπ+π0)=(8.73±0.35±0.43)×10−3 and observe the η mode above 10σ; its rate is larger than the cited SU(3)-flavor-symmetry predictions.
- **Evidence type:** Experimental; Belle and Belle II e+e− collision data (980 fb−1 and 570 fb−1, respectively).
- **Caveat:** Absolute branching fractions inherit the uncertainty in the world-average reference decay; the authors also note that three-body decay dynamics and possible intermediate resonances complicate interpretation of the theory discrepancy.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, 2026-10-23 announcement batch
- **Themes:** Particle, nuclear & high-energy physics

### 4. [A cosmic filament catalog for COSMOS-Web Data Release 1 across 0.5 < z < 3.5](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31168)
- **Abstract:** The authors combine photometric-redshift probability distributions with simulation-calibrated filament extraction to release a COSMOS-Web cosmic-web catalog spanning redshift 0.5–3.5.
- **Authors:** Qiaoyang Hao, Tao Wang, Weishan Zhu, Hong Guo, Peng Wang et al.
- **arXiv:** `2610.31168v1` (published 2026-10-22T12:24:46Z; categories: `astro-ph.CO`, `astro-ph.GA`, `astro-ph.IM`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** A reusable release of 12,523 filaments, 136,210 segments, and probability maps, cross-listed across cosmology, galaxies, and methods; the paper is published in Astronomy & Astrophysics (DOI 10.1051/0004-6361/202659407).
- **Claimed result:** The authors report a catalog across 0.5<z<3.5 built from COSMOS-Web DR1 and calibrated against TNG simulations, with mock-catalog tests indicating improved filament recovery over a single-redshift-slice baseline.
- **Evidence type:** Observational; COSMOS-Web DR1, with TNG simulation light-cone mocks used for calibration and validation.
- **Caveat:** The 2D extraction depends on photometric-redshift PDFs and simulation-driven thresholds; the authors specifically caution that Gaussian PDF approximations, projection effects, and method choice can change completeness and purity.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, 2026-10-23 announcement batch
- **Themes:** Astrophysics; Computational & data methods

### 5. [QUIJOTE scientific results - XII. Characterization of the polarized synchrotron emission at the power spectrum level in the QUIJOTE-MFI wide survey](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31469)
- **Abstract:** The authors combine QUIJOTE-MFI, Planck, and WMAP polarization maps to measure Galactic synchrotron power spectra and estimate their impact on future CMB B-mode searches.
- **Authors:** F. Vansyngel, J. A. Rubino-Martin, P. de Ferra, R. B. Barreiro, R. T. Genova-Santos et al.
- **arXiv:** `2610.31469v1` (published 2026-10-22T15:59:13Z; categories: `astro-ph.CO`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** Accepted for MNRAS and combines three microwave datasets to provide quantitative foreground estimates directly relevant to primordial B-mode measurements.
- **Claimed result:** For multipoles 30<ℓ<130, the authors find a possible BB-versus-EE synchrotron steepening at only about 1.6σ and estimate equivalent synchrotron power of r≈0.003 at 90 GHz and r≈0.0002 at 150 GHz.
- **Evidence type:** Observational; QUIJOTE-MFI wide survey (10–20 GHz) combined with Planck and WMAP.
- **Caveat:** The apparent BB/EE slope difference is not statistically strong (about 1.6σ), and the foreground estimates apply to the analyzed multipole range and sky masks.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, 2026-10-23 announcement batch
- **Themes:** Cosmology & gravitation; Astrophysics

### 6. [Experimental evidence of forward energy transfer induced by core-edge coupling in multiscale tokamak turbulence](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30638)
- **Abstract:** Combining two plasma-scattering diagnostics, the authors report evidence that an edge-originating quasi-coherent mode couples to core turbulence and transfers energy toward smaller scales.
- **Authors:** F. Chen, Z. M. Shen, N. Sun, P. Li, Z. Y. Qiu et al.
- **arXiv:** `2610.30638v1` (published 2026-10-22T02:58:21Z; categories: `physics.plasm-ph`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** A direct experimental result on a core-scale process relevant to fusion transport, supported by complementary collective Thomson-scattering and Doppler-backscattering measurements.
- **Claimed result:** The authors interpret the observed core-edge coupling and secondary instabilities as evidence for net forward energy transfer from ion to electron scales in the measured tokamak turbulence.
- **Evidence type:** Experimental; multichannel collective Thomson scattering and radially localized Doppler backscattering.
- **Caveat:** This is evidence from a tokamak measurement, not confirmation that the same cascade operates across machines or plasma regimes; broader replication is needed before generalizing the interpretation.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, 2026-10-23 announcement batch
- **Themes:** Plasma & fluid physics

### 7. [A Robust Estimator for the Lyman-α Forest 1D Power Spectrum at High Redshift](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31626)
- **Abstract:** The authors validate a likelihood-based estimator designed to recover the high-redshift Lyα flux power spectrum despite limited spectra, instrumental effects, noise, and missing pixels.
- **Authors:** Molly Wolfson, Joseph F. Hennawi, Frederick B. Davies, Zarija Lukić
- **arXiv:** `2610.31626v1` (published 2026-10-22T17:45:40Z; categories: `astro-ph.CO`, `astro-ph.GA`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** Cross-listed across cosmology and galaxy astrophysics; the method is tested against both known-input realizations and forward-modeled spectra with observational complications, targeting a difficult measurement regime at z≥5.
- **Claimed result:** The authors find that pixel-covariance maximum-likelihood inference recovers mock input power spectra within expected errors across tested scales under the modeled resolution, sampling, noise, and missing-data effects.
- **Evidence type:** Computational; analytic Gaussian mocks and forward-modeled high-redshift quasar-spectrum simulations.
- **Caveat:** Validation is on mocks rather than a new measurement from observed high-redshift spectra; robustness is only demonstrated for the observational effects included in those simulations.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, 2026-10-23 announcement batch
- **Themes:** Astrophysics; Computational & data methods

### 8. [Gravitational Wave Diffractive Images Reveal the Structure of Astrophysical Objects](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31441)
- **Abstract:** The authors derive how gravitational-wave diffraction can encode a lens's projected mass profile and forecast whether next-generation detectors could distinguish extended dark matter structures from point lenses.
- **Authors:** Han Gil Choi, Sunghoon Jung, Volodymyr Takhistov
- **arXiv:** `2610.31441v1` (published 2026-10-22T15:36:31Z; categories: `astro-ph.CO`, `astro-ph.HE`, `gr-qc`, `hep-ph`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** A cross-listed theoretical framework spanning four physics archives, backed by full numerical wave-optics calculations and explicit sensitivity forecasts for dark-matter structures and black-hole environments.
- **Claimed result:** The authors forecast that five years of next-generation observations could distinguish extended lenses from point masses down to dark-matter fractions of order 10−5, and halo-dressed primordial black holes down to order 10−6, for their assumed source populations.
- **Evidence type:** Forecast/proposal; analytic lensing relations and numerical wave-optics calculations for assumed source and lens populations.
- **Caveat:** These are forecasts, not detections; the reach depends on the assumed source population, source-lens geometry, lens models, and future detector sensitivities.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, 2026-10-23 announcement batch
- **Themes:** Cosmology & gravitation; Astrophysics

## Trending Research Themes

- **Sharper signals from detector and analysis design.** The neutrino paper combines a chlorine-enhanced delayed tag with multi-PMT coincidence and a laboratory test; the photonics experiment reconstructs a nonreciprocal polarization texture from the full reflection matrix; Belle/Belle II add a first charmed-baryon decay observation. These are distinct experiments, not independent replications of one result.
- **Foregrounds and cosmic structure as inference problems.** QUIJOTE-MFI with Planck/WMAP constrains synchrotron foregrounds, the Lyα paper develops a high-redshift power-spectrum estimator, and the COSMOS-Web team releases a simulation-calibrated filament catalog. Together they connect raw maps/spectra/catalogs to cosmological observables, while each remains tied to its own data and modeling assumptions.
- **Cross-scale physics with future reach.** The tokamak study links edge modes to core turbulence using two diagnostics; the gravitational-wave paper proposes diffraction as a future probe of compact and extended mass. The former is a measured plasma result, whereas the latter remains a next-generation-detector forecast.

## Open Problems and Research Directions

- **Author-stated limitation — seawater antineutrinos:** the TRIDENT sensitivity model omits time-dependent bioluminescence, and the salt-plus-background lab excess is only about 2σ. **Direction (synthesis):** measure full optical backgrounds and neutron-tag efficiency with detector modules under site-like seawater conditions before treating the simulated distance reach as operational. [2610.30754]
- **Author-stated limitation — magneto-optical BIC:** the reflection setup misses the central |θ|<6° region. **Direction (synthesis):** extend angular coverage and test whether the same handedness reversal and preserved charge persist across other device geometries and bias sweeps. [2610.31498]
- **Author-stated unresolved theory — charmed-baryon decays:** the ΛK+η rate differs from cited SU(3)-based predictions, and the paper notes possible intermediate resonant contributions. **Direction (synthesis):** fit amplitude-level invariant-mass distributions and compare independent measurements, rather than treating the branching-fraction discrepancy alone as a settled explanation. [2610.31377]
- **Author-stated limitations — cosmic filaments:** Gaussianized redshift PDFs and TNG-calibrated thresholds may not capture catastrophic outliers or a different real-universe topology. **Direction (synthesis):** rerun the extraction with realistic per-galaxy PDFs, alternate simulations, and spectroscopic subsets, then test galaxy properties not yet examined in this catalog. [2610.31168]
- **Reported uncertainty — CMB foregrounds:** the possible synchrotron BB/EE slope split is only 1.6σ. **Direction (synthesis):** add sky coverage and frequency leverage, and propagate foreground uncertainty through joint component-separation analyses before using it to set primordial B-mode limits. [2610.31469]
- **Open validation — tokamak turbulence:** the reported cascade interpretation needs tests across machines and operating regimes. **Direction (synthesis):** repeat synchronized CTS/DBS measurements while varying edge conditions and impurity content to see whether the energy-transfer signature tracks confinement changes. [2610.30638]
- **Author-stated scope — Lyα estimator:** validation covers simulated high-redshift spectra and modeled effects. **Direction (synthesis):** apply it to observed z≥5 quasar forests with end-to-end instrument calibration and compare recovered power against independent estimators. [2610.31626]
- **Forecast dependency — GW diffraction:** the proposed reach relies on next-generation detectors and assumed source-lens populations. **Direction (synthesis):** quantify selection effects and waveform/model uncertainties across realistic detector networks and lens profiles before interpreting any candidate diffraction feature. [2610.31441]

## Takeaway

This batch is strongest where it combines direct measurement with a reusable analysis or data product: the deep-sea neutron tag, magneto-optical polarization topology, Belle/Belle II decay measurement, and COSMOS-Web catalog. The cosmology and gravitational-wave results also show how foreground control and wave-optics inference may open new regimes, but their significance ranges from mock-validated methods to future forecasts—not established new detections.
