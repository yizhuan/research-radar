## Open Problems and Research Directions

- Open problem (authors' stated): For the four-dimensional null-cone quantization, the paper derives finite amplitudes and entropy within its construction; how far this framework extends beyond the treated causal-diamond setup remains a theoretical question. Research direction (synthesis): test the construction in additional geometries and matter-coupled settings, and make assumptions behind the finite-dimensional state count explicit (2611.34924).
- Open problem (authors' stated): Planet Nine orbit recovery remains degenerate without snap, while timing noise and contaminating terms affect the lower derivatives. Research direction (synthesis): apply the method to longer-baseline NANOGrav/IPTA timing data, characterize noise and nuisance terms, and combine any pulsar constraints with optical/TNO dynamical constraints; this tests a hypothesis and is not a detection claim (2611.28471).
- Open problem (authors' stated): HEP-Ex-Bench's current worlds use one experiment and have limited variation in physics modeling; the authors call for alternative generators and other experiments. Research direction (synthesis): expand to independent detector simulations and analysis classes, then compare expert references and score-weight sensitivity (2611.28194).
- Open problem (authors' stated): The look-elsewhere review identifies ambiguity in the scope of “elsewhere” and costly calibration for complex ML statistics. Research direction (synthesis): develop and validate global-significance procedures under controlled model selection, reporting the search space and calibration assumptions (2611.34888).
- Open problem (authors' stated): The Schwinger-model study controls several tensor-network and lattice errors, but extending light-front matrix elements to more general theories remains open. Research direction (synthesis): test scaling of bond dimension and gauge truncation in broader models and compare with complementary approaches (2611.31749).
