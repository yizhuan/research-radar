### 1. [The hadronic-vacuum-polarization contribution to the muon anomalous magnetic moment in isospin-symmetric lattice QCD with twisted-mass fermions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22224)
- **Abstract:** The Extended Twisted Mass Collaboration reports a continuum-extrapolated lattice-QCD determination of the muon's leading-order hadronic vacuum-polarization contribution, with about 1% uncertainty.
- **Authors:** C. Alexandrou, S. Bacchio, A. De Santis, A. Evangelista, J. Finkenrath et al. (Extended Twisted Mass Collaboration)
- **arXiv:** `2610.22224v1` (published 2026-10-15 17:03:50 UTC; categories: `hep-lat`, `hep-ph`, `hep-th`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** A substantial four-lattice-spacing calculation directly addresses a high-profile precision-test discrepancy; it is cross-listed across lattice, phenomenology, and theory. This is scientific-salience evidence, not a measured attention signal.
- **Claimed result:** The authors obtain full HVP contribution aμ = (714.9 ± 8.1) × 10⁻¹⁰ after applying the WP25 leading isospin-breaking correction; they say the resulting Standard Model prediction is consistent with the latest Fermilab result and increases tension with pre-CMD-3 data-driven dispersive determinations.
- **Evidence type:** Computational; lattice QCD using ETMC Nf=2+1+1 gauge configurations, four lattice spacings, and a continuum extrapolation.
- **Caveat:** The reported full-HVP value combines the isospin-symmetric calculation with an external isospin-breaking correction; the comparison with dispersive results remains method-dependent and the paper does not settle the broader g−2 discrepancy.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, 16 October 2026 batch
- **Themes:** Particle, nuclear & high-energy physics; Computational & data methods

### 2. [Extended strange metal and superconductivity in partially filled moiré Hubbard bands](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22307)
- **Abstract:** Measurements in twisted bilayer WSe₂ identify an extended strange-metal regime in a partially filled Hubbard band and a superconducting dome emerging from that regime.
- **Authors:** Yiyu Xia, Yichi Zhang, Zhongdong Han, Hongyuan Li, Brandon Bauer et al.
- **arXiv:** `2610.22307v1` (published 2026-10-15 17:49:42 UTC; categories: `cond-mat.mes-hall`, `cond-mat.str-el`, `cond-mat.supr-con`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** The authors report a clean-limit regime spanning carrier density and twist angle, plus a superconducting dome away from both the Mott insulator and van Hove singularity; the paper connects three condensed-matter categories. These are concrete result and cross-field signals, not popularity metrics.
- **Claimed result:** The authors report an extended strange metal persisting to 100 mK across multiple symmetry-breaking ground states, only at small twist angles, and a new superconducting dome developing from it.
- **Evidence type:** Experimental; transport observations in twisted bilayer WSe₂.
- **Caveat:** The authors say the observations favor an intrinsic strange-metal state, but that interpretation and its mechanism are not established beyond this material platform.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, 16 October 2026 batch
- **Themes:** Condensed matter & materials

### 3. [First Observation of the Radiative Decay D⁰ → γω](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21982)
- **Abstract:** BESIII reports a 6.0σ observation of the radiative charm decay D⁰ → γω and measures its absolute branching fraction.
- **Authors:** BESIII Collaboration: M. Ablikim, M. N. Achasov, P. Adlarson, X. C. Ai, S. K. Akondi et al.
- **arXiv:** `2610.21982v1` (published 2026-10-15 14:34:05 UTC; categories: `hep-ex`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** A first-observation claim with stated 6.0σ significance and a measured branching fraction, based on a sizeable BESIII data sample, makes this a notable experimental result. The ranking does not imply independent confirmation or measured popularity.
- **Claimed result:** From 20.3 fb⁻¹ of e⁺e⁻ data at 3.773 GeV, the authors report B(D⁰ → γω) = (2.12 ± 0.40(stat) ± 0.25(syst)) × 10⁻⁵ and interpret it as evidence for dominant long-distance hadronic dynamics in radiative charm decays.
- **Evidence type:** Experimental; BESIII detector data, with a Particle Transformer-based analysis.
- **Caveat:** This is a single-experiment result; the interpretation as a benchmark for non-perturbative QCD depends on theoretical calculations and will benefit from independent scrutiny.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, 16 October 2026 batch
- **Themes:** Particle, nuclear & high-energy physics; Computational & data methods

- **Abstract:** Pump-probe spectroscopy of flux-tunable transmons identifies weakly coupled microscopic degrees of freedom with intrinsic lifetimes longer than one second.
- **Authors:** Mathieu Féchant, Phillip Lenhard, Nicolas Gosling, Ritika Dhundhwal, Denis Bénâtre et al.
- **arXiv:** `2610.21853v1` (published 2026-10-15 13:31:00 UTC; categories: `quant-ph`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** The measured >1 s lifetime is a distinctive scale for defects relevant to superconducting-qubit coherence and error correction; the abstract reports a direct pump-probe measurement. The ranking is based on the result's technical relevance, not readership.
- **Claimed result:** The authors infer weak kilohertz-range coupling and lifetimes exceeding one second for microscopic degrees of freedom, identifying transmons as probes of such long-lived defects.
- **Evidence type:** Experimental; wide-band quantum-jump correlation spectroscopy and detuning-dependent pump-probe measurements on flux-tunable transmons.
- **Caveat:** The data are consistent with positional defects described by the standard tunneling model, but the authors do not exclude magnetic spin impurities or trapped quasiparticles.

### 6. [Band-Resolved Superconducting Gaps via the Thermal Hall Effect in CeRu₂](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22361)
- **Abstract:** Thermal Hall transport, constrained by specific heat and normal-state magnetotransport, is used to assign different superconducting gap magnitudes to electron and hole bands in CeRu₂.
- **Authors:** Darian Hall, Luke Pritchard Cairns, Nico Rowland, Yuanqi Lyu, Kohtaro Yamakawa et al.
- **arXiv:** `2610.22361v1` (published 2026-10-15 17:59:02 UTC; categories: `cond-mat.supr-con`, `cond-mat.str-el`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** A concise experimental report proposes a transport route to distinguish multiband gaps and gives band-specific assignments in a heavy-fermion superconductor; it spans superconductivity and strong correlations.
- **Claimed result:** The authors infer Δe/kB ≈ 13 K on heavy Ce 4f electron sheets and Δh/kB ≈ 8 K on hole sheets from a low-field sign reversal in κxy and a joint fit to thermal and normal-state transport constraints.
- **Evidence type:** Experimental; thermal Hall transport, specific heat, and normal-state magnetotransport.
- **Caveat:** Band assignment is inferred through a constrained transport analysis rather than a direct band-by-band measurement, and the conclusion depends on the modelling of the electronic contributions.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, 16 October 2026 batch
- **Themes:** Condensed matter & materials

## Trending Research Themes

- **Precision tests meet theory systematics.** The ETMC lattice result updates the muon HVP contribution, while the gallium paper uses a solar-neutrino calibration to reassess an anomaly; both show how conclusions depend on controlling inputs and analysis choices ([ETMC](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22224), [Elliott & Robertson](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22219)).
- **Transport as a window on correlated quantum matter.** Twisted bilayer WSe₂ reveals an extended strange-metal regime and a superconducting dome; CeRu₂ uses thermal Hall response to separate multiband gap assignments ([moiré study](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22307), [CeRu₂ study](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22361)). These are distinct systems, not independent confirmation of one mechanism.
- **New instrument and analysis reach.** BESIII combines a large collision sample with a transformer-based event analysis for a rare decay, while VLTI/GRAVITY+ adds astrometric and spectral constraints on a forming planet ([BESIII](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21982), [GRAVITY+](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21629)). The transmon defect study likewise uses quantum-jump spectroscopy to probe microscopic dynamics relevant to device coherence ([Féchant et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21853)).

## Open Problems and Research Directions

- **Muon g−2 inputs:** The ETMC result agrees with other lattice determinations but differs from pre-CMD-3 dispersive estimates. Further independent continuum-extrapolated calculations, cross-checks of isospin-breaking corrections, and comparisons with updated dispersive analyses could clarify which inputs drive the residual difference ([ETMC](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22224)).
- **Moiré strange metal:** The observed regime's microscopic mechanism and generality remain open. Mapping additional twist angles, carrier densities, and disorder levels, and testing whether the superconducting dome tracks the strange-metal region across devices, would probe the authors' intrinsic-state interpretation ([Xia et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22307)).
- **Radiative charm decay:** Independent measurements and improved non-perturbative QCD predictions would test the branching fraction and the authors' long-distance-dynamics interpretation ([BESIII](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21982)).
- **Long-lived defects:** The microscopic identity of the >1 s degrees of freedom is unresolved. Correlating their signatures with material/process changes and testing targeted defect control could distinguish tunneling defects from spin impurities or trapped quasiparticles ([Féchant et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21853)).
- **Protoplanet environment:** The GRAVITY+ data do not resolve a circumplanetary disk and do not tightly determine the atmosphere. Higher-S/N observations and longer-wavelength measurements are the authors' stated next steps ([GRAVITY+](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21629)).
- **CeRu₂ gap assignment:** Independent probes and tests of the constrained transport model could assess the inferred electron-/hole-sheet gap assignment ([Hall et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22361)).
