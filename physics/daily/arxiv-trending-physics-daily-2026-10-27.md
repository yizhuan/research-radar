### 1. [Indication of Electron Antineutrinos from the Diffuse Supernova Neutrino Background](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35619)
- **Abstract:** An analysis of 5,003.0 days of Super-Kamiokande data reports an indication of diffuse supernova-background electron antineutrinos using improved atmospheric-neutrino rejection and updated systematics.
- **Authors:** The Super-Kamiokande Collaboration; K. Abe, Y. Asaoka, M. Harada, Y. Hayato, K. Hiraide, et al.
- **arXiv:** `2610.35619v1` (published 2026-10-26 15:35:48 UTC; categories: `astro-ph.HE` (primary), `hep-ex` (cross-list))
- **Evidence for ranking:** Included in neutrino physicist Mauricio Bustamante’s 27 October daily arXiv picks; Super-Kamiokande’s institutional news had also highlighted the same 2.6σ result. That earlier notice is related coverage, not an independent replication. The 5,003-day data set and explicit spectral-model comparisons make the claim readily checkable.
- **Claimed result:** The authors report a best-fit flux of 3.6 +1.6/−1.5 cm⁻² s⁻¹ above 13.3 MeV and a 2.6σ (one-sided 99.5% C.L.) tension with the null hypothesis.
- **Evidence type:** experimental; Super-Kamiokande’s 5003.0-day water/Gd-water data (October 2008–March 2026), fit against six background components.
- **Caveat:** The spectral-fit significance is not a discovery; the complementary model-independent count analysis gives 1.8σ, and the inferred flux depends on signal and background modeling.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, 2026-10-27 announcement batch
- **Themes:** Astrophysics; Particle, nuclear & high-energy physics; Instrumentation & detectors

### 2. [Observation of the $T_{c\bar{c}1}(4430)^-$ state in $B^0\to D^{*-}D^0K^+$ decays](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35239)
- **Abstract:** An amplitude analysis of LHCb proton-proton collision data finds that adding the $T_{c\bar{c}1}(4430)^-$ state improves the description of the $B^0\to D^{*-}D^0K^+$ data with significance above 5σ.
- **Authors:** LHCb Collaboration; R. Aaij, M. Abdelfatah, A.S.W. Abdelmotteleb, C. Abellan Beteta, F. Abudinén, et al.
- **arXiv:** `2610.35239v1` (published 2026-10-26 11:23:25 UTC; categories: `hep-ex` (primary))
- **Evidence for ranking:** The collaboration reports significance above 5σ for a previously unobserved open-charm decay, using 9 fb⁻¹ across 7, 8 and 13 TeV; LHCb also provides machine-readable figures and tables on its public analysis page. This is a concrete result signal, not a readership measure.
- **Claimed result:** The authors report the first observation of $T_{c\bar{c}1}(4430)^-\to D^{*-}D^0$; the paper’s amplitude analysis gives 5.3σ after its assessed systematic uncertainties.
- **Evidence type:** experimental; LHCb amplitude analysis of 9 fb⁻¹ of proton-proton data at √s = 7, 8 and 13 TeV.
- **Caveat:** The internal structure of the state remains unresolved; the amplitude model and its systematic variations matter, and the result does not distinguish molecular, compact-tetraquark, or kinematic interpretations.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, 2026-10-27 announcement batch
- **Themes:** Particle, nuclear & high-energy physics; Instrumentation & detectors

### 3. [The design and flight of the Payload for Ultrahigh Energy Observations (PUEO)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35675)
- **Abstract:** The PUEO Collaboration describes the design, construction, and as-built validation of a balloon-borne antenna array intended to detect Askaryan radio emission from ultrahigh-energy neutrinos interacting in Antarctic ice.
- **Authors:** PUEO Collaboration; Q. Abarr, S. Agarwal, J. Alfaro, S. Ali, P. Allison, et al.
- **arXiv:** `2610.35675v1` (published 2026-10-26 16:16:39 UTC; categories: `astro-ph.HE` (primary), `astro-ph.IM`, `hep-ex` (cross-lists))
- **Evidence for ranking:** Selected in the 27 October specialist daily picks and cross-listed in astrophysics, instrumentation, and high-energy experiment. The paper documents a completed flight and extensive as-built validation, rather than making a neutrino-detection claim.
- **Claimed result:** The authors report a successful 23-day Antarctic balloon flight in January 2026 and describe the payload’s instrument, calibration, navigation, and first-look flight performance.
- **Evidence type:** experimental; NASA PUEO balloon payload, preflight tests, in-flight calibration and performance data.
- **Caveat:** The paper reports no neutrino flux or detection. The authors report an average duty cycle near 55% (about 12 days 21 hours of livetime), with thermal and downstream data-throughput limits affecting operations.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, 2026-10-27 announcement batch
- **Themes:** Astrophysics; Instrumentation & detectors

### 4. [PeV Diffuse Neutrinos from Magnetically Arrested Disks in Low-Luminosity Active Galactic Nuclei](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.33548)
- **Abstract:** The authors model all-flavor diffuse neutrino emission from magnetically arrested accretion flows in low-luminosity active galactic nuclei and constrain the source fraction using current IceCube data.
- **Authors:** Fan Wu, Benzhong Dai
- **arXiv:** `2610.33548v1` (published 2026-10-24 09:01:41 UTC; categories: `astro-ph.HE` (primary))
- **Evidence for ranking:** Included in the 27 October specialist daily arXiv picks and marked accepted for publication in ApJ; it gives quantitative, testable predictions against IceCube data and names IceCube-Gen2 as a future test.
- **Claimed result:** With 5% of accretion power assigned to nonthermal protons, the authors infer that at most 2.3×10⁻³ of LLAGNs can host the relevant MAD state; an assumed MAD fraction of about 1.6×10⁻³ gives a modeled intensity peak near 1.5 PeV.
- **Evidence type:** theoretical; diffuse-flux and neutrino-cascade modeling for pp and pγ production, constrained by IceCube observations.
- **Caveat:** These are model-dependent source-population limits, not an identified neutrino source; the allowed fraction changes with the assumed proton energy budget and LLAGN/LERG population relation.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, 2026-10-27 announcement batch
- **Themes:** Astrophysics; Particle, nuclear & high-energy physics; Computational & data methods

### 5. [Yb/Sr optical clock comparison at 2.2E-18 uncertainty over a 1350-km fiber link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.34250)
- **Abstract:** A phase-stabilized 1350-km fiber link is used to compare ytterbium and strontium optical lattice clocks in Wuhan and Shanghai at 2.18×10⁻¹⁸ total uncertainty.
- **Authors:** Lei Hou, Xiang-Pei Liu, Zhuan-Xian Xiong, Fa-Xi Chen, Wen-Xuan Song, et al.
- **arXiv:** `2610.34250v1` (published 2026-10-25 12:23:16 UTC; categories: `physics.optics` (primary), `physics.atom-ph` (cross-list))
- **Evidence for ranking:** The paper reports a concrete precision result over a 1350-km intercity link, with a stated total uncertainty below the 5×10⁻¹⁸ target described for second redefinition checks. No separate expert-pick or citation signal was used for its placement.
- **Claimed result:** The authors give a Yb/Sr frequency ratio of 1.207 507 039 343 337 716 0(26), with 1.14×10⁻¹⁸ Bayesian statistical and 2.18×10⁻¹⁸ total uncertainty, and report a correlation with Earth tides.
- **Evidence type:** experimental; two optical lattice clocks compared over a phase-stabilized 1350-km fiber link.
- **Caveat:** This is one clock pair and one intercity link; it demonstrates network capability but does not establish repeatability across a wider clock network.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, 2026-10-27 announcement batch
- **Themes:** Atomic, molecular & optical physics; Instrumentation & detectors; Cosmology & gravitation

### 6. [Direct measurement of the ${}^{23}\mathrm{Mg}(p, γ){}^{24}\mathrm{Al}$ reaction using the DRAGON recoil separator and a hybrid array of $γ$-ray detectors](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35860)
- **Abstract:** A direct inverse-kinematics measurement with a radioactive magnesium beam constrains the resonance strength and energy that dominate this reaction at classical nova temperatures.
- **Authors:** A. C. Edwin, G. Christian, A. Lennarz, I. Casandjian, S. M. Collins, et al.

- **Claimed result:** The authors report resonance strength ωγ = 38(7) meV and energy Ecm = 478.8(26) keV; their rate calculation is constrained to a 1σ up/down factor of 1.5 or smaller at 0.2–0.4 GK.
- **Evidence type:** experimental; a radioactive $^{23}$Mg beam, DRAGON recoil separator, and coincident LaBr₃/CeBr₃/BGO γ-ray detectors.
- **Caveat:** The stated rate constraint covers the nova-temperature range studied and depends on rate calculations; it does not remove model uncertainties in other stellar environments or other reaction channels.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, 2026-10-27 announcement batch
- **Themes:** Particle, nuclear & high-energy physics; Astrophysics; Instrumentation & detectors

### 7. [Observation of measurement-induced localization phenomena in a solid-state spin system](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.34262)
- **Abstract:** Experiments on interacting $^{13}$C nuclear spins in diamond find longer spin autocorrelation times as projective or global weak-measurement frequency increases, consistent with measurement-induced localization.
- **Authors:** Haolin Zhang, Shaoyi Xu, Zihang Zheng, Mengqi Wang, Mingzhe Liu, et al.
- **arXiv:** `2610.34262v1` (published 2026-10-25 12:48:22 UTC; categories: `quant-ph` (primary))
- **Evidence for ranking:** A direct solid-state many-body experiment with two measurement protocols and comparison to numerical simulations; selected for its concrete test of measurement-driven dynamics rather than independent media or citation momentum.
- **Claimed result:** The authors report substantially prolonged spin autocorrelation times with increasing measurement frequency and interpret this, together with simulations, as evidence for a localized regime.
- **Evidence type:** experimental; interacting $^{13}$C nuclear-spin system in diamond, with numerical simulations.
- **Caveat:** The measured autocorrelation response is evidence for localization, not a direct measurement of entanglement scaling or a full thermodynamic phase transition.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, 2026-10-27 announcement batch
- **Themes:** Quantum information; Condensed matter & materials; Computational & data methods

## Open Problems and Research Directions

- **DSNB significance and backgrounds — open problem (paper-stated):** the spectral fit is 2.6σ while the count-only analysis is 1.8σ, leaving background and spectral-model dependence material. **Direction (synthesis):** increase Gd-enhanced exposure and compare the signal spectrum with independent detectors and background models. [2610.35619]
- **LLAGN neutrino-source fraction — open problem (paper-stated):** the inferred MAD fraction depends on the nonthermal-proton energy share and on how LLAGNs are mapped to low-excitation radio galaxies. **Direction (authors’ proposal):** test the predicted PeV intensity and source fraction with IceCube-Gen2. [2610.33548]
- **PUEO livetime and event throughput — open problem (paper-stated):** thermal pauses and downstream prioritizer/storage throughput limited the first flight’s science livetime. **Direction (synthesis):** improve thermal management and sustained data handling, then analyze the flight sample for neutrino candidates. [2610.35675]
- **Nature of $T_{c\bar{c}1}(4430)^-$ — open problem (paper-stated):** molecular, compact-tetraquark, and kinematic explanations remain viable. **Direction (synthesis):** compare open- and hidden-charm channels with larger samples and amplitude models designed to separate threshold and triangle-singularity effects. [2610.35239]
- **Long-distance clock reproducibility — open problem (synthesis):** the 2.18×10⁻¹⁸ result is from a single Yb/Sr intercity link, and its tide correlation needs network-level checks. **Direction (synthesis):** repeat the comparison over independent links and clock pairs while modeling the changing geopotential. [2610.34250]
- **Measurement-induced localization — open problem (paper-stated):** the spin-autocorrelation evidence motivates testing the connection to entanglement and many-body dynamics. **Direction (synthesis):** vary system size and measurement cadence, and measure entanglement-sensitive observables under controlled decoherence. [2610.34262]
