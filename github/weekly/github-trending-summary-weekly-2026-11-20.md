## Trending Technologies and Themes

- **Agent workflow infrastructure:** `dream-num/univer`, `backnotprop/plannotator`, `alibaba/open-code-review`, and `ag-ui-protocol/ag-ui` focus on bringing agents into office products, review loops, and app interfaces.
- **Agent context and skills:** `GoogleChrome/modern-web-guidance-src`, `google-labs-code/stitch-skills`, `mvanhorn/last30days-skill`, `tradermonty/claude-trading-skills`, and `kajisho5/ffmpeg-skill` package domain instructions or concrete capabilities for agents.
- **Browser and UI tooling:** `ChromeDevTools/chrome-devtools-mcp` supplies browser control and diagnostics; `meta/astryx` and `dream-num/univer` make agent-ready UI and office interfaces part of product development.
- **Languages:** TypeScript leads (7 of 13 repositories), followed by Python (3); Go, Ruby, and Objective-C appear once each. The language mix reflects the strong representation of web-facing and agent-integration projects.

## Takeaway

This week's strongest signal is an expanding agent tooling layer: guidance, skills, browser access, human review, app protocols, and editable office environments all attracted substantial gains. At the same time, Sure and HermesEnabler show that focused end-user utilities still find an audience outside the AI-agent wave.
