### 1. [A 27 x 27 x 27 counterexample to Comon's conjecture](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28292)
- **Abstract:** The paper gives an explicit rational 27×27×27 symmetric tensor whose ordinary tensor rank is 55 over the rationals while its symmetric tensor rank is 56 over the complex numbers, contradicting Comon's conjecture over the rational, real, and complex fields.
- **Authors:** Benjamin Lovitz
- **arXiv:** `2609.28292v1` (published 2026-09-23 15:37 UTC; categories: `math.CO`, `cs.DS`, `math.AG`, `math.RA`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** Concrete counterexample to a named conjecture; cross-listed across combinatorics, algorithms, algebraic geometry, and rings/algebras; it appeared prominently in web search for the weekly window. Semantic Scholar citation counts were not available from the sources checked.
- **Claimed result:** The author reports a small explicit counterexample and proves a special case of Conjecture 6 from Shitov's 2018 work, using symmetric adjoins.
- **Assumptions and setting:** Tensor rank is compared with symmetric tensor rank for a symmetric tensor over several base fields; the construction has rational entries and size 27 in each mode.
- **Caveat:** This is a single-author arXiv preprint; the construction and its claimed implications require expert verification, and the abstract does not establish broader consequences for tensor-rank complexity.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, 2026-09-23 batch
- **Themes:** Combinatorics & discrete mathematics; Algebra & number theory

### 2. [Three Problems on Separable Quotients of Precompact Abelian Groups](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.26049)
- **Abstract:** The authors construct connected and zero-dimensional Baire Pontryagin-reflexive groups with strong subgroup properties and no nontrivial separable Hausdorff quotient, and resolve three previously posed problems about separable quotients.
- **Authors:** Li-Hong Xie, Jiang Yang
- **arXiv:** `2609.26049v1` (published 2026-09-22 11:58 UTC; categories: `math.GN`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** The abstract explicitly reports answers to Problems 1.25 and 3.12 and simultaneous realization of three regularity properties from the cited literature; it was one of the most visible math results returned by targeted weekly searches. Semantic Scholar citation counts were not available from the sources checked.
- **Claimed result:** The paper gives ZFC constructions and determines closed subgroups of finite powers for a related example, proving strong closure and h-embeddedness properties in Hausdorff quotients.
- **Assumptions and setting:** The setting is topological groups, especially precompact abelian groups, Baire and Pontryagin-reflexive groups, separable Hausdorff quotients, and finite powers.
- **Caveat:** The claims depend on specialized definitions and prior problems in the literature; the abstract does not substitute for checking the constructions and consistency of all topological properties.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, 2026-09-22 batch
- **Themes:** Geometry & topology; Logic & foundations

### 3. [Mixed multiplicities and Rees Theorems for graded families and their converses](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.23202)
- **Abstract:** For graded primary families in a Noetherian local ring, the authors extend Rees's mixed-multiplicity theorem through weak asymptotic and strong homogeneous joint reductions and establish a localized converse under formal equidimensionality.
- **Authors:** T. H. De Freitas, V. H. Jorge Pérez
- **arXiv:** `2609.23202v1` (published 2026-09-19 20:08 UTC; categories: `math.AC`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** It extends a central classical theorem to multigraded families and is designed to recover classical Rees and Böger results; it received prominent placement in targeted searches. Semantic Scholar citation counts were not available from the sources checked.
- **Claimed result:** The paper relates joint reductions to mixed multiplicities, gives a normalized-limit realization for weak asymptotic reductions, proves a localized converse, and derives a Böger-type theorem for graded families.
- **Assumptions and setting:** R is a Noetherian local ring; the families are graded and m-primary. The converse requires formal equidimensionality, while the adic specialization recovers the classical case.
- **Caveat:** The abstract explicitly says the converse to the weak asymptotic Rees theorem fails even for an adic family on a one-dimensional regular local ring; the exact boundary between the positive and negative converse results needs careful reading.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, 2026-09-19 batch
- **Themes:** Algebra & number theory

### 4. [Rayleigh-Residual Flow I: Normal Matrices](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.24901)
- **Abstract:** The paper analyzes the negative gradient flow of the squared Rayleigh-residual on complex projective space, characterizing critical points and, for normal matrices, reducing amplitude dynamics to a low-rank replicator equation.
- **Authors:** Eric Shen
- **arXiv:** `2609.24901v1` (published 2026-09-21 17:07 UTC; categories: `math.FA`, `math.DS`, `math.NA`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** It bridges functional analysis, dynamical systems, and numerical analysis; the abstract reports explicit first integrals, escape rates, and implications for spectral exploration. Semantic Scholar citation counts were not available from the sources checked.
- **Claimed result:** For arbitrary complex matrices, positive-residual critical points have a strictly descending tangent direction and local minima are eigenlines; for normal matrices, the critical set and trajectories admit an explicit reduced description.
- **Assumptions and setting:** The general statements concern complex matrices and projective-space gradient flow; the explicit dynamical reduction assumes normal matrices, with numerical examples including random spectra and a Jordan block.
- **Caveat:** The paper is Part I and the practical spectral-exploration benefits are supported in the abstract by analysis and numerical examples, not by an independent benchmark study.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, 2026-09-21 batch
- **Themes:** Analysis & PDE; Optimization & control; Numerical analysis & computation

### 6. [The Coproduct of Ideals and the Coprime Spectrum](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.23351)
- **Abstract:** The authors introduce coproducts of ideals and coprime ideals, obtaining a topological coprime spectrum and functorial results that classify fields among integral domains and recover the prime spectrum for dual rings.
- **Authors:** Frank Murphy-Hernandez, Eduardo Leon-Rodriguez
- **arXiv:** `2609.23351v1` (published 2026-09-20 04:36 UTC; categories: `math.AC`, `math.RA`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** It proposes a new lattice-theoretic object with several structural applications and cross-connects ideal theory, topology, and ring theory. Semantic Scholar citation counts were not available from the sources checked.
- **Claimed result:** The coprime spectrum is a topological space and a covariant functor on specified ring epimorphisms; it classifies fields among integral domains and is homeomorphic to the prime spectrum in dual rings.
- **Assumptions and setting:** The setting is arbitrary commutative rings, ideal lattices, ring epimorphisms that do not annihilate coprime ideals, integral domains, and dual rings.
- **Caveat:** The abstract states the structural claims but not their proof conditions in full; the relationship to established spectra and the limits of the functorial construction need expert assessment.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, 2026-09-20 batch
- **Themes:** Algebra & number theory; Geometry & topology

## Open Problems and Research Directions

- **Tensor-rank consequences:** Lovitz's construction tests Comon's conjecture over several fields. A direct follow-up is to determine the smallest dimensions and ranks admitting such separations and to quantify implications for tensor decomposition algorithms. This is a proposed direction, not a result of the paper.
- **Topological quotient classification:** Xie and Yang settle three cited problems, but their constructions invite classification of which additional precompact or reflexive groups can have no nontrivial separable Hausdorff quotient, and which properties survive beyond finite powers.
- **Converse hypotheses for graded Rees theory:** Freitas and Jorge Pérez identify a failure of the weak asymptotic converse even in a one-dimensional regular local ring. Determining sharp extra hypotheses restoring a converse is an explicit direction suggested by the positive/negative contrast.
- **Non-normal matrices and higher flow structure:** Shen's detailed reduction is for normal matrices. Extending the explicit dynamical picture to broader non-normal classes, while controlling transient growth and Jordan effects, is a natural test of the framework.
- **Universal-resolution verification:** Hu's abstract leaves the exact corrected lemmas and hypotheses implicit. A useful next step is an independent line-by-line comparison with the earlier preprint and a machine-checkable formulation where feasible.
