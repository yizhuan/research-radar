## Top papers (ranked)

### 1. [The Zaks--Perles--Wills volume conjecture I: three-dimensional lattice polytopes](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.22337)
- **Abstract:** For full-dimensional three-dimensional lattice polytopes with k ≥ 3 interior lattice points, the authors prove a volume bound 6(k+1), characterize equality by a specific Sylvester simplex, and thereby complete the conjecture in dimension three together with known k = 1, 2 cases.
- **Authors:** Feihu Liu, Jinlong Tang, Sihao Tao, Zihao Zhang
- **arXiv:** `2611.22337v1` (published 2026-11-17 07:39:36 UTC; categories: `math.CO`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** The abstract claims a complete resolution of the three-dimensional Zaks–Perles–Wills volume conjecture for all k, with an explicit extremal bound and equality classification.
- **Claimed result:** Theorem 1.2 states that a full-dimensional lattice polytope P ⊂ R³ with k ≥ 3 interior lattice points has volume at most 6(k+1), with equality exactly for affine-unimodular copies of conv(0, 2e₁, 3e₂, 6(k+1)e₃); the inequality is strict when minimum lattice width is at least three.
- **Assumptions and setting:** Three-dimensional full-dimensional lattice polytopes; k is the number of interior lattice points. The affine-unimodular equality classification preserves the lattice structure.
- **Caveat:** This paper proves the three-dimensional case; the conjecture in dimensions four and higher remains unresolved.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, announcement-batch date 2026-11-18
- **Themes:** Combinatorics & discrete mathematics; Geometry & topology

### 2. [The Bessis Moussa Villani conjecture in finite von Neumann algebras](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23122)
- **Abstract:** The authors extend the Bessis–Moussa–Villani positivity and Laplace-representation result from matrix algebras to bounded operators in finite von Neumann algebras with a faithful normal trace, using tracial spectral measures.
- **Authors:** Liviu Paunescu, Florin Radulescu, Andreas Thom
- **arXiv:** `2611.23122v1` (published 2026-11-17 17:39:52 UTC; categories: `math.OA`, `math-ph`, `math.FA`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** The claimed theorem carries an established matrix-algebra positivity statement to finite von Neumann algebras and is cross-listed across operator algebras, mathematical physics, and functional analysis.
- **Claimed result:** Theorem A states nonnegativity of traces of symmetrized words for positive operator pairs and gives a positive-measure Laplace representation of the trace exponential.
- **Assumptions and setting:** Bounded operators in a finite von Neumann algebra equipped with a faithful normal tracial state; the positivity assertion takes positive A and B.
- **Caveat:** The result is scoped to finite algebras with the specified trace; it does not claim the same theorem for arbitrary operator algebras or nontracial states.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, announcement-batch date 2026-11-18
- **Themes:** Mathematical physics

### 3. [New bounds on simplex-avoiding sets in two dimensions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23097)
- **Abstract:** The authors study measurable sets avoiding finite geometric configurations and give upper bounds of 7/11 for orthonormal-basis-free subsets of S² and 9/16 for unit-equilateral-triangle-free subsets of R².
- **Authors:** Domonkos Czifra, Ákos Dúcz, Péter Madarasi, Dániel Varga, Pál Zsámboki
- **arXiv:** `2611.23097v1` (published 2026-11-17 17:20:52 UTC; categories: `math.CO`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** The paper reports the first improvement over the elementary 2/3 sphere bound and a tighter 9/16 plane bound than the previously known 0.64355; its arXiv record links exact rational certificates and verification code.
- **Claimed result:** In Theorems 1.1–1.2, the authors prove the bounds 7/11 for normalized spherical measure and 9/16 for upper Banach density, using geometric fractional chromatic-number bounds from finite probes.
- **Assumptions and setting:** Measurable sets exclude the specified configurations; the plane result concerns upper Banach density. The two finite probes have 15 and 14 points, respectively.
- **Caveat:** These are upper bounds, not exact extremal densities; for the plane problem, the paper notes a conjectured optimal density of 1/2.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, announcement-batch date 2026-11-18
- **Themes:** Combinatorics & discrete mathematics; Geometry & topology

### 4. [A counterexample to the one-supremum conjecture and sharp logarithmic weighted bounds for Calderón--Zygmund operators](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.22097)
- **Abstract:** The author disproves a strong-type one-supremum conjecture using explicit weights, then proves matching logarithmically corrected upper and lower growth bounds for Calderón–Zygmund operators, identifying the optimal common logarithmic exponent.
- **Authors:** Xi Cen
- **arXiv:** `2611.22097v1` (published 2026-11-17 02:28:25 UTC; categories: `math.CA`, `math.FA`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** The abstract combines a counterexample for a fixed Hilbert transform with a sharp logarithmic loss and an extension to fixed Riesz transforms in dimensions n ≥ 2; it is cross-listed in classical and functional analysis.
- **Claimed result:** For every 1 < p < ∞, the proposed no-log bound fails for a fixed Hilbert transform; the paper establishes a logarithmically corrected bound and claims the common exponent γₚ = max{1/p, 1/p′} is optimal.
- **Assumptions and setting:** The strong-type one-supremum formulation with mixed Aₚ–A∞ characteristics evaluated on the same cube; explicit weights and fixed singular-integral operators.
- **Caveat:** The counterexample concerns that stated weighted formulation; it does not rule out different bounds under different hypotheses or formulations.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, announcement-batch date 2026-11-18
- **Themes:** Analysis & PDE

### 5. [Cutoff on abelian groups](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23180)
- **Abstract:** For abelian random walks whose inverse minimum jump probability is at most logarithmic in group size, the authors prove that cutoff is equivalent to the product condition, give a sharp mixing-window estimate, and construct near-threshold examples without cutoff.
- **Authors:** Guy Blachar, Jonathan Hermon, Justin Salez
- **arXiv:** `2611.23180v1` (published 2026-11-17 18:26:30 UTC; categories: `math.PR`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** The paper resolves a long-predicted equivalence in a specified sparse regime and supplies a sharpness construction showing that the sparsity restriction cannot simply be removed.
- **Claimed result:** The authors prove a quantitative mixing-window bound and show that, when the inverse smallest nonzero jump rate is O(log |G|), cutoff holds exactly when the product condition holds; reversibility is not assumed.
- **Assumptions and setting:** Sequences of continuous-time random walks on finite abelian groups; the equivalence uses the logarithmic sparsity condition on the jump distribution.
- **Caveat:** The equivalence is not asserted outside that sparsity regime; the paper constructs abelian Cayley-graph examples beyond the threshold where the product condition does not imply cutoff.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, announcement-batch date 2026-11-18
- **Themes:** Probability & statistics

### 6. [Sidorenko Inequalities in Oriented Graphs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23222)
- **Abstract:** The authors define relative Sidorenko and anti-Sidorenko inequalities for oriented graphs against arbitrary oriented host graphs, characterize the connected relatively Sidorenko class, and give families of examples of both types.
- **Authors:** Veronica Bitonti, Emma Hogan, Jonathan A. Noel, Dmitry Tsarev
- **arXiv:** `2611.23222v1` (published 2026-11-17 18:57:20 UTC; categories: `math.CO`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** Theorem 1.4 gives an if-and-only-if characterization of the connected class in terms of reversible weakly norming graphs, a concrete structural result rather than a new example alone.
- **Claimed result:** For a connected oriented graph with at least one arc, the authors prove it is relatively Sidorenko exactly when it is a one-way orientation of a reversible weakly norming graph; they also exhibit relatively anti-Sidorenko families.
- **Assumptions and setting:** The homomorphism inequalities must hold for every oriented host graph with the specified underlying graph; the characterization is for connected oriented graphs with an arc.
- **Caveat:** The authors note that a structural characterization of weakly norming graphs themselves remains open.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, announcement-batch date 2026-11-18
- **Themes:** Combinatorics & discrete mathematics

### 7. [Sharp Fractional Capacitary-Distance Hardy Inequalities](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.22706)
- **Abstract:** For open subsets of Rᵈ and a stated fractional Sobolev parameter range, the authors prove a capacitary-distance Hardy inequality and establish sharpness of its α-exponent and smoothness range.
- **Authors:** Yiqun Chen, Rupert L. Frank, Jie Xiao, Dachun Yang, Wen Yuan, Yangyang Zhang
- **arXiv:** `2611.22706v1` (published 2026-11-17 12:49:52 UTC; categories: `math.AP`, `math.CA`, `math.FA`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** The result gives explicit dependence on α and claims sharpness both for the exponent p′ and for the stated smoothness range; it is cross-listed across PDE, classical analysis, and functional analysis.
- **Claimed result:** For α ∈ (0,1], open Ω ⊂ Rᵈ, p ∈ (1,∞), and s ∈ (0, min{1+1/p, d/p}), the authors prove ∫Ω |u(x)|ᵖ/dα,s,p(x)ˢᵖ dx ≤ C α⁻ᵖ′ [u]s,pᵖ for u ∈ Cᶜ∞(Ω), with C depending only on d,s,p; they report sharpness of the α exponent and parameter range.
- **Assumptions and setting:** Fractional capacity distance is defined relative to the complement of an arbitrary open set; the inequality is stated for compactly supported smooth functions and the specified fractional Sobolev parameters.
- **Caveat:** The theorem is stated for u ∈ Cᶜ∞(Ω) and the indicated parameter range; broader function spaces are not asserted in the abstract.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, announcement-batch date 2026-11-18
- **Themes:** Analysis & PDE

### 8. [Regularity of solutions to complex Monge-Ampère equations in big classes](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23094)
- **Abstract:** On a compact Kähler manifold, the authors obtain local C²,α regularity for a non-pluripolar complex Monge–Ampère equation in an arbitrary big class under either of two stated conditions on the log density, including a smooth positive density case.
- **Authors:** Riley Guyett, Ryan Mc Gowan
- **arXiv:** `2611.23094v1` (published 2026-11-17 17:18:02 UTC; categories: `math.CV`, `math.AP`, `math.DG`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** The authors say the regularity criteria answer positively a question of Dinew, Guedj, and Zeriahi, and the result spans complex analysis, PDE, and differential geometry.
- **Claimed result:** The abstract claims local C²,α regularity on the ample locus under a weighted Laplacian lower bound on the log density, or under a quasi-plurisubharmonic decomposition; smooth positive density yields a smooth solution there, without nefness.
- **Assumptions and setting:** Non-pluripolar complex Monge–Ampère equation on a compact Kähler manifold in an arbitrary big class; regularity is local to the ample locus and depends on the stated data hypotheses.
- **Caveat:** The stated regularity is on the ample locus; it is not a regularity claim across its complement.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, announcement-batch date 2026-11-18
- **Themes:** Analysis & PDE; Geometry & topology

### 9. [An improved bound on the strong chromatic index via local flag algebras](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.22031)

## Trending Research Themes

- **Extremal combinatorics and geometry:** The lattice-polytope paper resolves a volume conjecture in dimension three; the simplex-avoidance paper improves two geometric density bounds; Sidorenko inequalities characterize a graph family; and the strong chromatic-index paper lowers a quadratic upper bound. Their tools differ—lattice-width geometry, finite probes and rational certificates, weakly norming graphs, and local flag algebras—so this is a recurring problem style, not one shared technique.
- **Sharpness and boundary structure in analysis:** Cen’s weighted Calderón–Zygmund paper gives a counterexample and matching logarithmic growth; Chen et al. establish sharp dependence for fractional capacitary Hardy inequalities. These distinct frameworks both make optimality, rather than only an upper estimate, part of the result.
- **Broader settings for established results:** The BMV paper carries matrix-algebra positivity into finite von Neumann algebras, while the Monge–Ampère paper obtains regularity in big non-nef classes under stated local hypotheses. These are separate theorem extensions, not evidence of a common new method.
- **Sharp stochastic behavior:** The abelian-walk paper characterizes cutoff in a logarithmically sparse regime; the ASEP paper gives a fixed-time law and a rarefaction-fan limit for a second-class particle. Both study sharp probability behavior, but in different models.
- **Isolated optimization signal:** The constrained SVRG paper reports a complexity improvement and acceptance by SIAM Journal on Optimization; one paper does not establish a batch-wide optimization trend.

## Open Problems and Research Directions

- **Author-stated — higher-dimensional volume bounds:** The Zaks–Perles–Wills conjecture remains unresolved in dimensions four and higher; this paper settles dimension three. **Research direction (synthesis):** test whether its lattice-width decomposition and equality analysis can constrain the higher-dimensional cases. [Liu et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.22337)
- **Author-stated — extremal densities:** For unit-equilateral-triangle avoidance in R², the paper records a density-1/2 construction conjectured to be optimal, while its upper bound is 9/16; it also records an open gap between its 7/11 spherical upper bound and a conjectured scalloped-belt value. The authors additionally ask whether finite-probe bounds approximate optimal independence density in general. **Research direction (synthesis):** test larger or structurally different probes and compare exact dual certificates with known constructions. [Czifra et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23097)
- **Author-stated — graph structure:** The oriented-graph paper notes that a structural characterization of weakly norming graphs remains open. **Research direction (synthesis):** use the reversible weakly norming characterization to test candidate graph families and identify conditions beyond the listed examples. [Bitonti et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23222)
- **Research direction (synthesis):** The cutoff paper's sharp logarithmic sparsity boundary motivates studying intermediate jump-probability regimes to identify what, if anything, replaces the product condition below that threshold. [Blachar et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23180)
- **Research direction (synthesis):** Since the Monge–Ampère regularity result is local to the ample locus, investigate whether additional hypotheses yield quantitative control near its complement. [Guyett and Mc Gowan](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23094)
- **Research direction (synthesis):** The one-supremum counterexample leaves room to test alternative weight hypotheses or formulations that retain useful bounds while respecting the paper's optimal logarithmic lower bound. [Cen](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.22097)
