### 1. [First constraints on axion-like particles with SPHEREx data](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13090)
- **Abstract:** Using 16.5 months of SPHEREx near-infrared data, the authors set new limits on axion-like dark matter over 0.496–3.32 eV and find substantial headroom for improving sensitivity through analysis methods.
- **Authors:** Connor Hainje, Elena Pinetti, David W. Hogg
- **arXiv:** `2611.13090v1` (published 2026-11-09 19:00:02 UTC; categories: `hep-ph`, `astro-ph.CO`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** First ALP search with SPHEREx observations, two-archive relevance, numerical coupling and lifetime limits, and a public analysis-code link reported in the paper; the result addresses an active dark-matter search with a new survey dataset.
- **Claimed result:** The authors report a 95% power-constrained upper limit as strong as gₐγγ < 2.10 × 10⁻¹¹ GeV⁻¹ at mₐ = 0.606 eV, with median lifetime limit τ > 6.5 × 10²⁵ s; they find no unknown high-significance line.
- **Evidence type:** observational; analysis of 16.5 months of SPHEREx QR2/QR3 data from the North and South Deep Fields.
- **Caveat:** The limit assumes an NFW Milky Way halo and a specified dark-matter velocity dispersion; masking and known spectral features reduce sensitivity, and the authors estimate methodological improvements could strengthen limits by over an order of magnitude.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, 11 November 2026 announcement batch
- **Themes:** Astrophysics; Particle, nuclear & high-energy physics; Instrumentation & detectors

### 2. [Nonperturbative string dynamics in a (2+1)D lattice gauge theory on a superconducting quantum processor](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14484)
- **Abstract:** A 63-qubit superconducting processor simulates string evolution in a two-dimensional Z₂ gauge theory, resolving charge and flux spreading, pair creation, and transverse string rearrangement across confined and deconfined regimes.
- **Authors:** Sanziana Stelea, Anthony Gandon, Francesco Tacchino, Jad C. Halimeh, Ivano Tavernelli
- **arXiv:** `2611.14484v1` (published 2026-11-10 18:45:36 UTC; categories: `quant-ph`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** A concrete many-qubit experiment reports 12 ancillas, up to 15 second-order Trotter steps, and direct access to local Gauss-law constraints as an error-detection resource; it targets real-time 2D gauge dynamics beyond the principal 1D experimental regime.
- **Claimed result:** The authors report rapid charge/flux spreading in the deconfined regime, stronger matter occupation near the pair-creation resonance, and suppression of spreading at strong electric fields.
- **Evidence type:** experimental; digital quantum simulation on 63 superconducting qubits, checked with ancilla and gauge-constraint measurements and noise rescaling.
- **Caveat:** The simulated evolution is finite-depth and noise-mitigated; classical PEPS comparisons are considered converged only over limited time windows, and later-time hardware data have larger uncertainties.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, 11 November 2026 announcement batch
- **Themes:** Quantum information; Particle, nuclear & high-energy physics; Computational & data methods

### 3. [Fast Scrambling Cosmology](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13100)
- **Abstract:** The authors propose a fast-scrambling-time correction to the cosmological Raychaudhuri equation, derive background solutions and proxy constraints, and show how neutrino mass transitions could leave a residual dark-energy contribution.
- **Authors:** Aliv Sahoo, Fatima Abbasi, Jake McNaughton, Niayesh Afshordi
- **arXiv:** `2611.13100v1` (published 2026-11-09 19:00:06 UTC; categories: `astro-ph.CO`, `gr-qc`, `hep-th`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** Highlighted in an independent 11 November arXiv digest and cross-listed across cosmology, gravitation, and high-energy theory; the paper provides explicit proxy bounds and a falsifiable route to a full cosmological likelihood analysis.
- **Claimed result:** The authors obtain proxy estimates Γ = −3.7 ± 6.1 from BBN and Γ ≃ −2.8 ± 0.9 by translating existing CMB/BAO/supernova fits, while finding a history-dependent dark-energy term of order σmν⁴ when neutrinos become non-relativistic.
- **Evidence type:** theoretical; modified-FLRW background equations, analytic solutions, and proxy constraints translated from existing cosmological fits.
- **Caveat:** This is a phenomenological, background-level model rather than an observational detection; the authors call for a dedicated likelihood analysis and a consistent treatment of structure formation and perturbations.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, 11 November 2026 announcement batch
- **Themes:** Cosmology & gravitation; Particle, nuclear & high-energy physics

### 4. [Detectability of Seyfert galaxies in point-source and stacking searches with a global network of neutrino telescopes](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13247)
- **Abstract:** A coronal stochastic-acceleration model is used to forecast detection prospects for nearby Seyfert galaxies with a joint IceCube, Baikal-GVD, KM3NeT/ARCA, and P-ONE network, favoring Circinus as a point-source target and X-ray-informed stacking for other sources.
- **Authors:** Lena Saurenhaus, Francesca Capel, Foteini Oikonomou
- **arXiv:** `2611.13247v1` (published 2026-11-09 21:21:21 UTC; categories: `astro-ph.HE`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** Featured in the independent 11 November arXiv digest; it combines four neutrino facilities with an explicit source-emission model and a conditional, dated detection forecast.
- **Claimed result:** The authors forecast a 5σ Circinus detection by 2038 at the latest only if Seyferts supply the diffuse neutrino flux up to 100 TeV and Circinus is at least as efficient as the population average.
- **Evidence type:** forecast/proposal; model-based point-source and stacking sensitivity estimates for IceCube, Baikal-GVD, KM3NeT/ARCA, and P-ONE.
- **Caveat:** The timeline depends on the assumed Seyfert contribution and Circinus efficiency; the paper does not report a new neutrino detection, and it expects other individual sources to remain difficult.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, 11 November 2026 announcement batch
- **Themes:** Astrophysics; Instrumentation & detectors

### 6. [Solving the SU(2) BFSS model](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14512)
- **Abstract:** The authors numerically solve the SU(2) BFSS ground state and use its wavefunction and finite-box spectra to extract scattering amplitudes, positive-energy bound states, and metastable resonances.
- **Authors:** Kabir Bajaj, Henry W. Lin
- **arXiv:** `2611.14512v1` (published 2026-11-10 18:57:34 UTC; categories: `hep-th`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** Ground-state correlators agree with independent matrix-bootstrap islands to about five significant figures, while the three-point amplitude agrees with a prior computation; the paper also reports a nonperturbative four-point extraction.
- **Claimed result:** The authors find the expected Born–Oppenheimer v⁴/r⁷ interaction in the appropriate regime and identify positive-energy bound states in several SO(9) irreducible representations plus metastable resonances.
- **Evidence type:** computational; numerical solution of the SU(2) matrix quantum mechanics, matrix-bootstrap comparisons, and Lüscher finite-box scattering analysis.
- **Caveat:** The study is restricted to SU(2), not the large-N holographic regime; extracting observables requires tail fits and finite-box corrections, and the authors note that larger N will be substantially harder numerically.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, 11 November 2026 announcement batch
- **Themes:** Particle, nuclear & high-energy physics; Computational & data methods

### 8. [Type IIB instanton corrections from pure modular unfolding](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13086)
- **Abstract:** The paper develops a modular-symmetry method to reconstruct higher Fourier modes of type-IIB interaction coefficients from low-mode data, without using supersymmetry differential equations.
- **Authors:** Amineh Mohseni
- **arXiv:** `2611.13086v1` (published 2026-11-09 19:00:02 UTC; categories: `hep-th`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** Featured as a technical highlight in the independent 11 November arXiv digest; the 76-page work combines Rankin–Selberg unfolding, Hecke relations, the Kuznetsov trace formula, and spectral-tail bounds to derive fixed-charge prefactor terms.
- **Claimed result:** The author reconstructs higher modes for the D⁶R⁴ coefficient and derives the gₛ⁻¹/² and constant weak-coupling instanton-prefactor terms at each fixed positive charge, matching the known expansion.
- **Evidence type:** theoretical; modular spectral analysis of type-IIB higher-derivative interaction coefficients.
- **Caveat:** Extracting cusp amplitudes assumes the conjectured simplicity of the even cusp spectrum and complete low-mode data; the paper identifies additional information needed for higher-charge D⁸R⁴ predictions.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, 11 November 2026 announcement batch
- **Themes:** Particle, nuclear & high-energy physics

### 9. [Connecting field-level and n-point function measurements of galaxy bias](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14456)
- **Abstract:** A renormalization-group treatment is used to connect galaxy-bias parameters measured at finite field-level cutoffs to low-k n-point-function measurements, with tests on perturbative mocks and N-body halo catalogues.
- **Authors:** Hsiang-Ming Huang, Henrique Rubira, Fabian Schmidt
- **arXiv:** `2611.14456v1` (published 2026-11-10 18:23:46 UTC; categories: `astro-ph.CO`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** The same RG prediction is tested against both Lagrangian-perturbation mock fields and full N-body halo catalogues; the reported agreement within predicted systematic errors bridges two cosmological-analysis schemes.
- **Claimed result:** The authors find that the RG-predicted running of bias parameters matches measurements across cutoffs and approaches the tree-level n-point estimates as the cutoff tends to zero.
- **Evidence type:** computational; forward-model galaxy fields and halo catalogues from full N-body simulations.
- **Caveat:** The demonstrated connection is established on model-generated fields and halo catalogues; applying it to survey data requires controlling the bias-expansion and simulation systematics.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, 11 November 2026 announcement batch
- **Themes:** Cosmology & gravitation; Computational & data methods

### 10. [Chiral Superconductivity Indifferent to Parent Topology in the Square-Lattice Hofstadter-Hubbard Model](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13091)
- **Abstract:** DMRG, finite-temperature DQMC, parton analysis, and weak-coupling calculations indicate that doping several distinct phases of a π-flux Hofstadter–Hubbard model produces the same chiral topological superconducting state.
- **Authors:** Jia-Xin Zhang, Wen O. Wang, Leon Balents, Lucile Savary, D. N. Sheng
- **arXiv:** `2611.13091v1` (published 2026-11-09 19:00:02 UTC; categories: `cond-mat.str-el`, `cond-mat.supr-con`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** The result is supported by several complementary methods—DMRG, DQMC, parton analysis, and perturbation theory—and is cross-listed in strongly correlated electrons and superconductivity.
- **Claimed result:** The authors report a chiral topological superconductor with spin Chern number −2 across nearly the full doped phase diagram, independent of whether the parent state is IQH, chiral-spin-liquid, or antiferromagnetic.
- **Evidence type:** computational; DMRG, finite-temperature determinant quantum Monte Carlo, parton analysis, and weak-coupling perturbation theory.
- **Caveat:** This is a result for a proposed lattice model, not an observation of superconductivity in a material; the predicted phase diagram depends on the model and numerical regimes studied.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, 11 November 2026 announcement batch
- **Themes:** Condensed matter & materials

### 11. [Planckian Charge Equilibration in Fast Scramblers](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13081)
- **Abstract:** Linear-response calculations for maximally scrambling complex SYK and Yukawa models yield a Planckian slow charge-imbalance relaxation rate whose dimensionless prefactor depends on the fermion scaling dimension.
- **Authors:** Jaewon Kim, Eduardo Fradkin
- **arXiv:** `2611.13081v1` (published 2026-11-09 19:00:01 UTC; categories: `cond-mat.str-el`, `hep-th`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** Cross-listed between strongly correlated electrons and high-energy theory; the paper combines an analytic conformal-regime expression with numerical checks in SYK and Yukawa models.
- **Claimed result:** The authors derive Γ = 2π(2Δ − d)kBT/ℏ for the slowest relative-charge mode and show that maximal scrambling does not determine its dimensionless prefactor.
- **Evidence type:** theoretical; conformal linear-response analysis and numerical real-time Green-function calculations for d = 0 and d = 1 models.
- **Caveat:** The result applies to the studied conformal models; the authors leave the conserved center-charge response and its possible ballistic-to-diffusive crossover unresolved.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, 11 November 2026 announcement batch
- **Themes:** Condensed matter & materials; Quantum information

## Trending Research Themes

- **Dark-sector constraints and multi-messenger forecasts:** SPHEREx provides a new spectral-line constraint on ALP dark matter, while the Seyfert study models future high-energy neutrino searches; these are complementary probes, not independent confirmations of a common signal.
- **Quantum dynamics beyond simple toy settings:** The superconducting processor probes nonperturbative 2D gauge-string evolution; the BFSS work numerically resolves strong-coupling wavefunctions and scattering; and the SYK/Yukawa study connects scrambling to charge equilibration. The evidence types differ—hardware measurement, matrix-model computation, and model theory.
- **Theory-to-observation links in cosmology:** Fast Scrambling Cosmology turns a horizon-timescale idea into testable background evolution, while the galaxy-bias paper relates field-level and n-point analyses using mocks and N-body catalogues. Neither paper reports a new cosmological detection.
- **Precision optical tools:** The 493-nm lithium-niobate platform targets fast visible-light control, whereas the VUV reflectance setup supplies material data for noble-liquid detectors; these are distinct instrumentation advances rather than a shared experiment.
- **Cross-domain physical mechanisms:** The ice-nucleation experiment tests negative-ion clustering in a laboratory plasma, with possible relevance to planetary and disk environments; extrapolation to those settings remains conditional.

## Open Problems and Research Directions

- **SPHEREx sensitivity and astrophysical assumptions (author-stated limitation):** The authors estimate more than an order-of-magnitude headroom from methodological changes and note masking/line-feature losses. Follow-up analysis could test alternative halo and velocity models, refine spectral-continuum handling, and repeat the search as more survey data arrive. [2611.13090]
- **Cosmological viability (author-stated limitation):** Fast-scrambling constraints are proxy/background-level only. A dedicated joint likelihood using DESI DR2, CMB, BAO, and supernova data, together with perturbation and structure-formation calculations, would test whether the model is distinguishable from ΛCDM/CPL. [2611.13100]
- **Quantum-simulation reach (synthesis from reported limits):** The 2D gauge-theory experiment is finite-depth and its classical comparison is reliable only in selected time ranges. Larger circuits and lattices, with independent gauge-constraint and calibrated error checks, could test whether the reported regime-dependent string behavior persists at later times. [2611.14484]
- **Seyfert neutrino forecast (author-stated condition):** Circinus reaches the projected 5σ threshold only under assumptions about the population’s diffuse-flux contribution and source efficiency. X-ray-informed stacking across the telescope network and future MeV gamma-ray observations could test the coronal model and refine those assumptions. [2611.13247]
- **Laboratory-to-astrophysical ice extrapolation (synthesis):** The experiment identifies hydrated negative ions as plausible precursors, but the importance of this channel depends on UV fields, low temperatures, and ion chemistry in each environment. Parameter scans varying these conditions and comparison with dust-surface nucleation models could delimit where the pathway is relevant. [2611.14441]
