### 1. [First Observation of the Radiative Decay D⁰ → γω](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21982)
- **Abstract:** The BESIII Collaboration reports a 6.0σ observation of D⁰ → γω, measuring its branching fraction as (2.12 ± 0.40(stat) ± 0.25(syst)) × 10⁻⁵.
- **Authors:** BESIII Collaboration (M. Ablikim, M. N. Achasov, P. Adlarson, X. C. Ai, C. S. Akondi, et al.; 671 additional authors)
- **arXiv:** `2610.21982v1` (published 2026-10-15 14:34:05 UTC; categories: `hep-ex`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** A concrete new decay observation with a reported 6.0σ significance and absolute branching fraction from 20.3 fb⁻¹ of BESIII data; ranked highly for the direct experimental result, not audience metrics.
- **Claimed result:** The authors say the branching fraction supports the dominance of long-distance hadronic dynamics in radiative charm decays and gives a benchmark for non-perturbative QCD calculations.
- **Evidence type:** experimental; 20.3 fb⁻¹ of e⁺e⁻ collisions at √s = 3.773 GeV recorded by BESIII, analyzed with a Particle Transformer-based strategy.
- **Caveat:** The interpretation as a probe of long-distance dynamics is not itself a direct measurement of those dynamics; the branching fraction carries separate statistical and systematic uncertainties.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, 2026-10-16 announcement batch
- **Themes:** Particle, nuclear & high-energy physics; Computational & data methods

### 2. [Discovering state-of-the-art quantum circuits with large language models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22367)
- **Abstract:** The authors use evaluator-guided evolutionary search, with LLM-proposed circuits, to reduce resource counts across quantum-computing tasks and find a new reversible architecture for modular inversion.
- **Authors:** Andi Gu, Alek H. Kemeny, Susanne F. Yelin, Mikhail D. Lukin
- **arXiv:** `2610.22367v1` (published 2026-10-15 17:59:20 UTC; categories: `quant-ph`; cross-lists: `cs.AI`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** A cross-disciplinary method with reported improvements in seven of eight tasks and a new circuit architecture; ranked for the breadth and testable resource claims, not measured readership.
- **Claimed result:** The authors report substantial reductions in Toffoli and qubit counts in seven tasks versus their state-of-the-art comparisons, plus a reversible divsteps-based modular-inversion architecture.
- **Evidence type:** computational; evaluator-scored circuit search across numerical computation, chemistry, optimization, quantum simulation, and number theory.
- **Caveat:** The claimed gains depend on the task set, evaluator, and comparison baselines; independent reproduction and hardware-level performance are not established by the abstract.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, 2026-10-16 announcement batch
- **Themes:** Quantum information; Computational & data methods

### 3. [Band-Resolved Superconducting Gaps via the Thermal Hall Effect in CeRu₂](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22361)
- **Abstract:** Thermal Hall transport, constrained by specific heat and normal-state magnetotransport, is used to assign the two reported superconducting gaps in CeRu₂ to different Fermi-surface sheets.
- **Authors:** Darian Hall, Luke Pritchard Cairns, Nico Rowland, Yuanqi Lyu, Kohtaro Yamakawa, Josue Rodriguez, Kirin Chanteloup, Didier Perrodin, Joel E. Moore, James G. Analytis
- **arXiv:** `2610.22361v1` (published 2026-10-15 17:59:02 UTC; categories: `cond-mat.supr-con`; cross-lists: `cond-mat.str-el`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** A specific experimental strategy addresses the gap-assignment problem in a multiband superconductor; the abstract reports numerical gap scales and a low-field sign reversal.
- **Claimed result:** The authors infer Δe/kB ≈ 13 K on heavy Ce 4f electron sheets and Δh/kB ≈ 8 K on hole sheets from the thermal Hall response and supporting transport constraints.
- **Evidence type:** experimental; thermal Hall, specific-heat, and normal-state magnetotransport measurements.
- **Caveat:** The assignment depends on the assumed two-band description and fitted gap/mobility parameters; the abstract does not report uncertainty ranges for the quoted gap values.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, 2026-10-16 announcement batch
- **Themes:** Condensed matter & materials

- **Abstract:** LSST and Keck observations find P/2026 N2 active across perihelion for 102 days, with imaging and dynamical modeling consistent with sustained or recurrent dust production.
- **Authors:** Bryce Bolin, Leo Alcorn, Max Brodheim, Alexa Gordon, Lordrick Kahinga, Michael Lundquist, Rita Morris, J. Xavier Prochaska
- **arXiv:** `2610.22354v1` (published 2026-10-15 17:58:04 UTC; categories: `astro-ph.EP`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** Multiple observing epochs from LSST and Keck support a specific, sustained-activity result, paired with archival-image and dynamical checks; this is a direct observation rather than a speculative forecast.
- **Claimed result:** The authors report active morphology over 102 days and similar corrected aperture brightness between July and October, supporting prolonged or recurrent dust production.
- **Evidence type:** observational; LSST observations from June–July 2026, Keck/LRIS imaging on 7 October, archival DECam imaging, and a 5,000-particle dynamical integration.
- **Caveat:** The proposed volatile sublimation (possibly water ice) is a hypothesis, not a confirmed mechanism; the comet's 2021 activity state is inconclusive.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, 2026-10-16 announcement batch
- **Themes:** Astrophysics

### 5. [Operational Limits of Gravity-Mediated Entanglement](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22097)
- **Abstract:** A theoretical model studies how finite wave-packet width, dephasing, and residual electrostatic forces affect whether gravity-generated entanglement remains useful for operational tests.
- **Authors:** Atta ur Rahman, Muhammad Noman, Ying-Jian Zhu, Jia-Xin Peng, Saeed Haddadi
- **arXiv:** `2610.22097v1` (published 2026-10-15 15:45:52 UTC; categories: `quant-ph`; cross-lists: `gr-qc`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** It links quantum-information witnesses to practical gravitational-test imperfections and identifies concrete tolerance boundaries; placement reflects cross-field relevance and a clearly stated experimental-design question.
- **Claimed result:** In a Gaussian-wave-packet, two-branch cat-state model, the authors find windows for Bell nonlocality and useful teleportation that shrink with broader packets or stronger dephasing; electrostatic phase can suppress the signal.
- **Evidence type:** theoretical; Newtonian interaction averaged over finite-width packets with phenomenological correlated dephasing.
- **Caveat:** Conclusions are model-dependent: the cat-state setup and phenomenological noise treatment do not establish that gravity-mediated entanglement has been observed.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, 2026-10-16 announcement batch
- **Themes:** Quantum information; Cosmology & gravitation

- **Abstract:** Numerical-relativity simulations test eccentric black-hole–neutron-star waveform models and examine how eccentricity and magnetic fields affect gravitational waves and merger remnants.

- **arXiv:** `2610.21560v1` (published 2026-10-15 09:18:21 UTC; categories: `gr-qc`; cross-lists: `astro-ph.HE`)

- **Claimed result:** The authors find reasonable agreement for SEOBNRv5EHM except when tidal effects matter, larger late-inspiral disagreement for the tested post-Newtonian models, and efficient damping of trapped g-modes in strongly magnetized disks.
- **Evidence type:** computational; numerical-relativity merger simulations, including magnetized-matter cases.
- **Caveat:** The study covers a subset of systems and a moderate-eccentricity range; waveform agreement is conditional, especially once tidal contributions become important.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, 2026-10-16 announcement batch
- **Themes:** Cosmology & gravitation; Astrophysics
