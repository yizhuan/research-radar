### 1. [Thouless's bandwidth conjecture: a proof by elliptic periods](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.19723)
- **Abstract:** For the critical rational Harper operator, the authors prove that the denominator times the total spectral bandwidth tends uniformly in the reduced numerator to 32G/π, with G Catalan’s constant.
- **Authors:** Yongqiang Zhao
- **arXiv:** `2611.19723v1` (published 2026-11-15 02:10:55 UTC; categories: `math-ph, math.SP`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** The abstract states a sharp, explicit uniform asymptotic for a named conjecture and connects spectral theory to genus-one periods; the arXiv record cross-lists mathematical physics and spectral theory.
- **Claimed result:** The authors prove Thouless’s bandwidth asymptotic: for coprime 1≤p<q, q|S_{p,q}| tends uniformly in p to 32G/π as q→∞, where S_{p,q} is the union of the spectra over phase and G is Catalan’s constant (arXiv abstract and Theorem 1.1).
- **Assumptions and setting:** The critical Harper operator with rational parameter p/q, standard normalization, and total bandwidth defined as the measure of the union of spectra over all phases; uniformity is over reduced numerators.
- **Caveat:** This concerns the critical rational model and its total bandwidth, not individual spectral gaps or off-critical operators.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, 17 Nov 2026 announcement batch
- **Themes:** Mathematical physics, Analysis & PDE

### 2. [The Borel Ruziewicz problem](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24066)
- **Abstract:** The authors prove uniqueness of rotation-invariant finitely additive probability measures on Borel subsets of spheres and normalized invariant finitely additive measure on bounded Borel subsets of Euclidean spaces in the stated dimensions, using finite rotation sets with spectral gap.
- **Authors:** Konstantin Slutsky
- **arXiv:** `2611.24066v1` (published 2026-11-18 08:59:05 UTC; categories: `math.DS, math.FA, math.LO`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** A single theorem treats both spherical and Euclidean settings and gives a finite-generator spectral-gap criterion; the arXiv record cross-lists dynamics, functional analysis, and logic.
- **Claimed result:** The authors establish the Borel Ruziewicz theorem: normalized surface measure is the only rotation-invariant finitely additive probability measure on Borel subsets of S^n for n≥2, and Lebesgue measure is the only normalized finitely additive measure on bounded Borel subsets of R^d invariant under orientation-preserving isometries for d≥3 (arXiv abstract).
- **Assumptions and setting:** Borel sets; sphere dimension n≥2 or Euclidean dimension d≥3; invariance can be reduced to finite sets of rotations with spectral gap (on R^d, two such sets about distinct centers).
- **Caveat:** The theorem is explicitly for Borel-domain measures and the stated dimensions. The arXiv comments disclose that the main results were obtained by AI (hexagon:2611.00309v1), so independent expert verification is especially important.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, 19 Nov 2026 announcement batch
- **Themes:** Probability & statistics, Logic & foundations

### 3. [A Poisson boundary solution to Connes' bicentralizer conjecture and Kadison's problem on MASA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24787)
- **Abstract:** The author develops Poisson-boundary methods to prove a weak Dixmier property for inclusions with expectation, yielding new proofs of Connes’ and the relative bicentralizer conjectures and a conditional solution to Kadison’s MASA problem.
- **Authors:** Shuoxing Zhou
- **arXiv:** `2611.24787v1` (published 2026-11-18 17:35:16 UTC; categories: `math.OA, math.DS`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** The abstract reports a new proof route for a major conjecture plus the relative version and a second application to Kadison’s problem; categories span operator algebras and dynamics.
- **Claimed result:** The author proves that for a countably decomposable inclusion M⊂P with a normal faithful conditional expectation, M⊂c(P) has the weak Dixmier property; by cited characterizations this implies Connes’ and the relative bicentralizer conjectures, and under extra hypotheses yields a MASA of M that is also maximal abelian in P (arXiv abstract and Theorem A).
- **Assumptions and setting:** Countably decomposable von Neumann algebras with a normal faithful conditional expectation; the MASA conclusion additionally assumes separable preduals and M′∩P=Z(M).
- **Caveat:** This is an independent new proof of Connes’ conjecture after a recent proof already appeared; the Kadison-problem conclusion has the additional relative-commutant and separability hypotheses.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, 19 Nov 2026 announcement batch
- **Themes:** Algebra & number theory, Logic & foundations

### 4. [A Proof of Stein's Equidissection Conjecture](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.20240)
- **Abstract:** The author resolves Stein’s conjecture by proving that a balanced polygon cannot be dissected into an odd number of equal-area triangles.
- **Authors:** Dmitry Krachun
- **arXiv:** `2611.20240v1` (published 2026-11-15 20:43:22 UTC; categories: `math.CO, math.NT`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** The arXiv introduction says the full result removes the rational-vertex restriction of the previously known case; the paper is cross-listed in combinatorics and number theory.
- **Claimed result:** The author states that Stein’s conjecture holds in full: no balanced polygon admits an equidissection into an odd number of triangles (arXiv abstract and Conjecture 1 in the introduction).
- **Assumptions and setting:** A balanced polygon, meaning its oriented boundary segments can be paired into opposite vectors; an equidissection is a dissection into equal-area triangles.
- **Caveat:** The result is about balanced polygons and odd equidissections, not arbitrary polygons or arbitrary dissections.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, 17 Nov 2026 announcement batch

### 5. [A disproof of the van Emde Boas--Kruyswijk conjecture on the Davenport constant](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24775)
- **Abstract:** The authors disprove the van Emde Boas–Kruyswijk formula for the Davenport constant by showing D(C_6^r)>5r+1 for every r≥23.
- **Authors:** Weidong Gao, Alfred Geroldinger, Guoqing Wang, Xiangneng Zeng
- **arXiv:** `2611.24775v1` (published 2026-11-18 17:23:47 UTC; categories: `math.CO, math.NT`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** The abstract gives a concrete disproof of a conjecture dating to 1969, with an explicit parameter range, and the record cross-lists combinatorics and number theory.
- **Claimed result:** The authors give a counterexample to the conjectured identity D(C_n^r)=r(n−1)+1, proving D(C_6^r)>5r+1 for every r≥23 (arXiv abstract).
- **Assumptions and setting:** D(G) is the least length forcing a nontrivial zero-sum subsequence in every sequence from a finite abelian group G; the counterexample family is C_6^r for r≥23.
- **Caveat:** This refutes the proposed universal formula on one explicit family; it does not determine D(C_n^r) in general.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, 19 Nov 2026 announcement batch
- **Themes:** Combinatorics & discrete mathematics, Algebra & number theory

- **Claimed result:** The authors prove the polygonal Saint-Venant inequality conjectured by Pólya–Szegö: for every n≥3, the regular n-gon uniquely maximizes torsional rigidity among possibly non-convex polygons of fixed area with at most n sides (arXiv abstract).
- **Assumptions and setting:** Planar polygons with prescribed area and at most n≥3 sides; competitors may be non-convex; objective is torsional rigidity.

### 8. [Sharpness of the Osgood threshold for anomalous dissipation and non-uniqueness in passive scalar transport](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24784)
- **Abstract:** For every concave non-Osgood modulus, the authors construct a divergence-free velocity field on the two-torus producing anomalous dissipation, establishing sharpness of the Osgood threshold and a related transport non-uniqueness threshold.
- **Authors:** Kyle L. Liss, Kunhui Luan, Changhui Tan
- **arXiv:** `2611.24784v1` (published 2026-11-18 17:30:26 UTC; categories: `math.AP`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** The abstract resolves a sharp-threshold question uniformly over a whole class of moduli and links anomalous dissipation to transport non-uniqueness.
- **Claimed result:** The authors prove that for any concave non-Osgood modulus ω there is u∈L∞([0,1];C^ω(T²)) and smooth initial data producing anomalous dissipation; they derive sharpness of the Osgood condition for uniqueness of bounded transport solutions (arXiv abstract).
- **Assumptions and setting:** Passive scalar advection-diffusion on T², divergence-free velocity in L∞ time with spatial modulus C^ω, concave non-Osgood ω, and smooth initial data.
- **Caveat:** The construction is for the two-dimensional torus and the specified concave non-Osgood moduli; it does not assert the same for every flow or domain.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, 19 Nov 2026 announcement batch
- **Themes:** Analysis & PDE

### 9. [A proof of a conjecture on bipartite correspondence packing](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.20504)

- **Claimed result:** For sufficiently large D, every correspondence cover of a finite bipartite graph with maximum cover degree at most D has a full correspondence packing when each vertex has k(D)=ceil(D/(log D−2log log D−6)) colors; the author proves the cited conjecture (arXiv abstract and Theorem 1).
- **Assumptions and setting:** Finite bipartite graph; correspondence cover degree at most D; the explicit formula is stated for D≥ceil(e^20), with k(D) asymptotic to D/log D.
- **Caveat:** The explicit theorem’s stated asymptotic regime begins at the very large threshold D≥ceil(e^20); it is a sufficient condition, not a claim of optimal finite-D constants.

### 11. [On Hilbert's tenth problem for number fields](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24748)
- **Abstract:** The authors show conditionally that the integers are Diophantine in number fields, giving a negative answer to Hilbert’s tenth problem under the abc conjecture and a rank condition.
- **Authors:** Hector Pasten, Xavier Vidaux
- **arXiv:** `2611.24748v1` (published 2026-11-18 17:06:52 UTC; categories: `math.NT, math.LO`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** A direct conditional theorem on Hilbert’s tenth problem across number fields, cross-listed in number theory and logic, with the hypotheses stated explicitly in the abstract.
- **Claimed result:** Assuming abc over a number field K and a positive-rank elliptic curve E/Q with rank E(Q)=rank E(K), the authors prove Z is Diophantine in K, hence Hilbert’s tenth problem over K is undecidable; for quadratic K the rank condition is available, leaving abc as the assumption (arXiv abstract and Theorem 1.1).
- **Assumptions and setting:** A number field K; abc over K; and a positive-rank elliptic curve over Q whose rank is unchanged over K. For quadratic K, the paper says such curves always exist.
- **Caveat:** The conclusion remains conditional on abc over K; for general K it also requires the stated elliptic-curve rank condition, and concerns the field K rather than its ring of integers.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, 19 Nov 2026 announcement batch
- **Themes:** Algebra & number theory, Logic & foundations

## Open Problems and Research Directions

- **Explicit hypothesis still open (reported by the authors):** The negative solution of Hilbert’s tenth problem for number fields in 2611.24748 depends on abc over K, plus a positive-rank elliptic curve whose rank does not rise over K. A concrete follow-up is to identify number-field classes where the rank condition is verified and isolate exactly what additional arithmetic would remove the abc assumption.
- **Scope left by a counterexample (research direction, synthesis):** 2611.24775 disproves the Davenport-constant formula for C_6^r when r≥23 but does not give the exact values. Determine the growth or exact constants for this family and test which other exponent/rank pairs fail; the paper’s explicit range provides a starting point.
- **Model extension (research direction, synthesis):** 2611.19723 proves a uniform limit for the critical rational Harper operator. It would be informative to test which parts of the elliptic-period/coarea method persist off criticality or for quantities beyond total bandwidth; this is a proposed extension, not an open problem claimed by the authors.
- **Boundary regularity (research direction, synthesis):** 2611.24784 establishes sharpness for concave non-Osgood moduli on T². Quantifying the dissipation construction near the Osgood boundary, or testing analogous thresholds on other domains, would probe the scope of the theorem without assuming its conclusions generalize.
- **Proof-assistant audit (research direction, synthesis):** 2611.19084 reports a Lean 4 formalization and computer-assisted finite-range checks. Checking the artifact’s coverage against the stated all-degree theorem and documenting the trusted computational boundary would strengthen reproducibility; this report did not audit the code.
