# Giant Watch Summary - Weekly - 2026-11-27

## Headline

Google's Gemini 4 Neon launch and OpenAI's GPT-6.2 chat rollout set the week's competitive pace, while state attorneys general sued SpaceX over Grok's sexualized imagery. The episode's throughline is frontier labs pushing agentic, long-horizon models into paid tiers while safety and cost pressures mount.

## Top Developments

### 1. Google launches Gemini 4 Neon for coding, knowledge work and long-horizon agents (Google)
Google released Gemini 4 Neon, positioned between Hydrogen and Carbon, with a 1M-token context window, four thinking levels, and API pricing of $3 per 1M input and $15 per 1M output tokens. Google reported a score of 61 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index at xhigh thinking, #2 on the Vals Index, and #2 on steerability in the Agent Arena Index, and said Neon can orchestrate parallel Hydrogen sub-agents inside Google Antigravity. Availability spans the Gemini API paid tier, Google AI Studio, Gemini for business, and the Gemini app for AI Pro and Ultra subscribers; free-tier users are excluded.
Why it matters: A direct competitive strike at Anthropic's Opus 5.5 and OpenAI's GPT-6.2 on price and agent orchestration, and the clearest model launch of the week.

### 2. OpenAI rolls GPT-6.2 into the ChatGPT chat tab for paid tiers (OpenAI)
OpenAI began rolling out a version of GPT-6.2, powered by GPT-6.2 Sol, to the ChatGPT chat tab for Plus, Pro, Business and Enterprise tiers. OpenAI reported image understanding at 71.7% (up from 47.4% for GPT-6), instruction following on challenging prompts at 78.1% (up from 62.5%), and BrowseComp at 64.5% versus 54.4% at Extra high, plus safety-policy compliance of 91–94% versus 77–80%. The release follows GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol arriving in ChatGPT Work and Codex on November 17.
Why it matters: Moves OpenAI's newest model from professional surfaces into everyday chat, raising the baseline for consumer assistants and setting up head-to-head comparisons with Neon.

### 3. Virginia and Oregon sue SpaceX over Grok's sexualized and CSAM imagery (xAI)
Virginia Attorney General Jay Jones and Oregon Attorney General Dan Rayfield filed separate but similar suits against SpaceX, which operates X and xAI (described as now called SpaceX Super Intelligence), alleging consumer-protection violations over Grok's generation of non-consensual intimate imagery and child sexual abuse material. The complaints center on Grok Imagine's 'spicy mode' and an image-editing feature, allege that safety statements were misleading, and cite a CCDH estimate of roughly three million sexualized images posted on X between December 29, 2025 and January 8, 2026. The states seek injunctions and civil penalties; X Safety said it screens every prompt and output and reports harmful content to law enforcement.
Why it matters: The first state-level litigation over a major model's image-generation safeguards, with potential precedent for how agentic and generative products are regulated.
Refs: https://www.oag.state.va.us/media-center/news-releases/jay-jones/3162-attorneys-general-jones-and-rayfield-file-suit-against-spacex-parent-company-of-x-xai-and-grok-over-creation-and-promotion-of-child-exploitation-and-non-consensual-images-on-platform, https://arstechnica.com/tech-policy/2026/11/states-sue-spacex-to-stop-grok-from-generating-child-sex-images-revenge-porn/, https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1040255/x-grok-ai-deepfakes-oregon-virginia-lawsuit?ref=aipolicydaily.org

### 4. Z.ai ships GLM-5.4, its first loop-transformer flagship (GLM)
Z.ai released GLM-5.4, a 750B model built on a Loop Transformer architecture that runs 28 layers of the GLM-5 base twice, adding computational depth without adding parameters. It adds vision and targets 'Generalized Coding' across engineering and design work, and is available through the GLM Coding Plan, ZCode and Z.ai Chat, with API access pending a security assessment and open weights promised after safety hardening. Z.ai claims parity with 2T–3T-class models, but its main controlled evidence comes from a 30B test model, and early testers reported slow runtimes and a gap to Claude Opus 5.5.
Why it matters: The first disclosed Chinese use of looped-transformer architecture in a flagship model, and a test of whether architectural efficiency can close the gap to closed frontier labs.
Refs: https://www.scmp.com/tech/big-tech/article/3375517/chinas-zai-aggressively-using-looped-transformer-method-improve-ai-models?module=Big%20Tech&pgtype=section, https://venturebeat.com/technology/z-ais-glm-5-4-aims-to-match-models-up-to-four-times-its-size-by-running-28-layers-twice, https://docs.z.ai/guides/overview/overview, https://baijiahao.baidu.com/s?id=1882653789635236887

### 5. Meta launches its Muse AI agent in the UK (Meta)
Meta launched its Muse AI agent in the UK, following rollouts in the US, Australia, Canada, Japan, Mexico and Singapore. Muse can read email, shop, book travel and pay bills, with purchases and messages requiring user approval and a separate security agent checking actions against granted permissions; Meta signed deals with UK retailers including Marks & Spencer, Currys, Sainsbury's and easyJet. The ICO said Meta committed to a voluntary monitoring and reporting scheme, and the CMA said talks produced fairer terms and conditions. Muse is powered by Muse Spark, Meta's first model from its superintelligence lab.
Why it matters: A major consumer-agent deployment into a heavily regulated market, with both UK watchdogs extracting commitments before launch.
Refs: https://www.theguardian.com/technology/2026/nov/24/meta-launches-muse-uk-ai-agent-do-christmas-shopping?eicker.news, https://www.mirror.co.uk/tech/ai-muse-meta-launches-uk-37908816

### 6. Anthropic expands Claude Duet research preview to India (Anthropic)
Anthropic said it is bringing Claude Duet, an agent app built around Claude Opus 5.5, to India. Duet runs in its own virtual machine with a browser so it can schedule meetings, build dashboards and draft forecasts while a user's device is off; it shows planned actions and waits for approval before sending messages, making payments, signing documents or deleting anything, and a separate safety check screens outgoing messages. Pro, Max and Ultra users can join a waitlist, Team customers get a public beta and enterprises a private beta, and the app supports all 35 Claude languages including Hindi, Bengali, Marathi, Telugu and Tamil.
Why it matters: Extends Anthropic's agent product into a large multilingual market with an approval-gated autonomy model that contrasts with faster-moving consumer agents.
Refs: https://www.telegraphindia.com/business/anthropic-brings-claude-duet-to-india-with-ai-that-works-when-devices-are-switched-off/cid/2191273

### 7. Claude models documented as generally available on Azure-hosted Microsoft Foundry (Anthropic)
Microsoft's Foundry documentation lists Claude models including claude-opus-5-5, claude-opus-5, claude-haiku-5-5 and claude-opus-4-8 as generally available when hosted on Azure, alongside Anthropic-hosted versions, with 1M-token context windows and 128K max output. The page also lists Fable and Mythos models as preview or gated research preview on Anthropic infrastructure, and notes EU watermarking compliance via interwoven text watermarking and C2PA provenance marking. Billing runs through an Azure Marketplace subscription in Claude Consumption Units.
Why it matters: Confirms Anthropic's models are distributed through Microsoft's enterprise channel with Azure-hosted GA tiers, broadening enterprise reach against Google and OpenAI cloud offerings.
Refs: https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/foundry/foundry-models/concepts/claude-models

### 8. DeepSeek tightens free-tier limits and adds per-conversation cost display (DeepSeek)
DeepSeek restricted free users on its web and app products, capping new-conversation input at roughly 40,000 characters, blocking continuation of long conversations inactive for more than seven days, and showing a usage-limit message after about two hours of continuous use. Its Android app version 2.10.0 then added a button showing the cost of each conversation's consumed quota, matching the API billing model, which reports read as groundwork for paid consumer tiers. Coverage ties the limits to compute constraints and rising agent token consumption, citing DeepSeek Harness daily active users growing from about 271,000 in the week of September 30 to 1.54 million by November 4.
Why it matters: A visible turn from free consumer scale toward paid API and agent economics at one of China's most prominent model developers.
Refs: https://iot.ofweek.com/2026-11/ART-132200-8420-30713335.html#comment, https://api3.cls.cn/share/article/2546211?os=ios&sv=812&app=

### 9. Alibaba releases Qwen4-Max flagship, moving from preview to general availability (Qwen)
Alibaba Cloud released Qwen4-Max, a 2.4T-parameter sparse MoE flagship with 71B activated parameters, native vision, and a 1M-token context window, using GDN plus QSA hybrid attention that the company says speeds million-token reads by more than 7x. Alibaba reported Work360 rising to 52.69 from 47.74, average per-task cost down 58% versus the prior generation, and a SuperCLUE score of 61.61 with timeout rate falling from 69.23% to 0%. The preview ran November 11–25 on Token Plan subscription only, and the official qwen4-max is now live; the model is API-only, with no fine-tuning or private deployment.
Why it matters: Alibaba's flagship shifts toward long-horizon agent work and cost efficiency, sold through subscription credits rather than pay-as-you-go.
Refs: https://developer.aliyun.com/article/1788200, https://developer.aliyun.com/article/1786793

### 10. Kimi K3.1 Pro reaches Batch API and older models retire (Kimi)
Moonshot's Kimi platform added kimi-k3.1-pro and kimi-k3 to the Batch API on both CN and Global platforms, priced at 60% of standard rates with cache-write fees and best-effort cache hits. The same changelog says kimi-k2.5 and all moonshot-v1 series models were retired platform-wide, returning 404 errors, and directs users to migrate to Kimi K3. Kimi K3.1 Pro is documented with a 1M-token context window, reasoning effort levels low/high/max, vision input and tool calling.
Why it matters: Consolidates Moonshot's lineup around K3-generation models and adds cheaper asynchronous inference for long-horizon coding and knowledge work.
Refs: https://platform.kimi.ai/docs/platform-changelog, https://platform.kimi.com/docs/guide/kimi-k3-1-pro-quickstart

### 11. OpenAI adds healthcare model configurations to its API (OpenAI)
OpenAI made healthcare model configurations available in its API, tailoring models such as Astra and Sol for healthcare use cases. With web search enabled, they can query a dedicated healthcare index to help developers build applications that find, compare and summarize medical evidence with citations. They are available through the Responses API to API organizations without requiring a ChatGPT for Healthcare workspace, and OpenAI states they do not replace professional medical judgment.
Why it matters: A vertical, regulated-domain packaging of OpenAI's newest models aimed at clinical and health-adjacent developers.
Refs: https://help.openai.com/en/articles/9624314-model-release-notes

### 12. Z.ai documents GLM-5.3-Flash and FlashX multimodal models (GLM)
Z.ai's documentation describes GLM-5.3-Flash and GLM-5.3-FlashX as the first native multimodal models in the GLM-5 series, with 320B total parameters and 18B activated, combining sparse and linear attention to cut attention computation and KV cache by 3.01x and 4.44x versus GLM-5.3. The models support a 1M-token context, visual coding from screenshots and screen recordings, Office deliverables and financial workflows, and FlashX is listed at 200 tokens/s. GLM-5.3-Flash is available on the GLM Coding Plan with 3x the quota of GLM-5.3, while FlashX is not yet on the plan.
Why it matters: Pushes native multimodal capability into Z.ai's low-cost tier, widening the price-performance frontier for coding agents.
Refs: https://docs.z.ai/guides/vlm/glm-5.3-flash, https://docs.z.ai/guides/overview/overview

Meta's chief AI officer raised the possibility of humanity losing control of self-improving AI and referenced reports that AI had cracked the Riemann hypothesis.

## Takeaway

The week's competitive axis has shifted from raw benchmark scores to agent orchestration, subscription packaging and per-task cost, with safety litigation now a live risk for consumer image and agent products. Watch whether Google's Neon pricing and OpenAI's chat rollout translate into measurable share shifts, and how the xAI suits shape default safeguards for generative features.
