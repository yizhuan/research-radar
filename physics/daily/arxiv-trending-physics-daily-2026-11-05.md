### 1. [Gravitational-Wave Constraints on $R^2$ Gravity](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.06902)
- **Abstract:** The authors map quadratic $f(R)=R+aR^2$ gravity to a one-parameter massive-scalar inspiral waveform and use two binary-neutron-star events to constrain its coupling.
- **Authors:** Pawan Kumar Gupta, Laura Sagunski
- **arXiv:** `2611.06902v1` (published 2026-11-04T18:49:50Z; categories: `gr-qc`, `astro-ph.HE`, `hep-th`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** An independent daily gr-qc discovery index places it first among 32 listed papers; it is also cross-listed across three physics areas and reports a quantitative bound from gravitational-wave data. The digest position is only a weak discoverability signal, not a readership measure. [Index](https://papers.cool/arxiv/gr-qc)
- **Claimed result:** The authors’ combined Bayesian analysis of GW170817 and GW190425 gives $a\lesssim6.2\times10^6\,\mathrm{m}^2$ at 90% credibility, corresponding to $m_s\gtrsim3.2\times10^{-11}$ eV; the posterior remains consistent with general relativity.
- **Evidence type:** Observational; Bayesian inspiral-waveform analysis of the two binary-neutron-star events.
- **Caveat:** The modified waveform is developed through 0PN order, and the constraint uses only two events; the authors identify higher-order waveform and neutron-star tidal modeling as follow-up needs.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, announcement-batch date 2026-11-05
- **Themes:** Cosmology & gravitation; Astrophysics

### 2. [Experimental activation of hidden metrological steerability in quantum channels via stochastic filtering](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.06668)
- **Abstract:** The authors use stochastic pre- and post-filtering to restore a metrological-steering-inequality violation under amplitude damping, demonstrating the effect with photonic polarization qubits.
- **Authors:** H. S. Karthik, J. Bermúdez, D. Montoya, J. Parra-Pastén, F. M. Quinteros et al.
- **arXiv:** `2611.06668v1` (published 2026-11-04T15:44:19Z; categories: `quant-ph`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** A separate daily quantum-physics discovery index includes it at position 17; the paper also reports a direct photonic test and a quantified change in the noise threshold. The index order is a limited discovery signal, not a popularity statistic. [Index](https://papers.cool/arxiv/quant-ph)
- **Claimed result:** In the authors’ experiment, the damping threshold for violating the metrological steering inequality rises from about 0.5 without filtering to about 0.75 at the strongest tested filter setting.
- **Evidence type:** Experimental; heralded single-photon polarization qubits, a two-path Sagnac amplitude-damping channel, and Brewster-angle glass-plate filters.
- **Caveat:** The restored advantage is conditional on probabilistic filtering, which reduces the event success probability; the demonstration tests amplitude damping rather than arbitrary noise channels.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, announcement-batch date 2026-11-05
- **Themes:** Quantum information; Atomic, molecular & optical physics

### 3. [The M87 Black Hole Mass from JWST and Keck Stellar Kinematics Spanning 10 pc to 10 kpc](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05486)
- **Abstract:** The authors combine JWST and Keck stellar-kinematics maps with triaxial orbit modeling to measure M87’s central black-hole mass over a wide range of radii.
- **Authors:** Emily R. Liepold, Chung-Pei Ma, Refa M. Al-Amri, Jonelle L. Walsh
- **arXiv:** `2611.05486v1` (published 2026-11-03T19:00:06Z; categories: `astro-ph.GA`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** The result joins JWST/NIRSpec and Keck/KCWI measurements, fits roughly 5,000 kinematic constraints, and reports an EHT-comparable mass-to-distance interval; this is a substantial multi-instrument observational result rather than a forecast.
- **Claimed result:** The authors infer $M_{\rm BH}=(5.38\pm0.13\pm0.22)\times10^9\,M_\odot$ and a mass-to-distance ratio whose 90% credible interval overlaps the multi-epoch EHT result.
- **Evidence type:** Observational; JWST NIRSpec and Keck KCWI stellar kinematics analyzed with triaxial Schwarzschild orbit models.
- **Caveat:** The statistical error is now smaller than the systematic uncertainty from M87’s distance; the EHT interval overlap is a comparison of independent methods, not a new EHT measurement.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, announcement-batch date 2026-11-05
- **Themes:** Astrophysics

### 4. [Search for GeV-scale axion-like particles in PbPb collisions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.00135)
- **Abstract:** The LHCb Collaboration searches forward PbPb collision data for axion-like particles produced by exclusive photon fusion and decaying to two photons, setting limits in the 3–10 GeV mass range.
- **Authors:** LHCb Collaboration, R. Aaij, M. Abdelfatah, A. S. W. Abdelmotteleb, C. Abellan Beteta et al.
- **arXiv:** `2611.00135v2` (published 2026-11-03T17:25:01Z; categories: `hep-ex`, `nucl-ex`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** This is a replacement in today’s batch and the authors describe it as the first forward search for this exclusive photon-fusion channel; the analysis gives explicit mass and coupling reach and is cross-listed in high-energy and nuclear experiment.
- **Claimed result:** Using 0.228 nb$^{-1}$ of PbPb data at $\sqrt{s_{NN}}=5.02$ TeV, the collaboration probes masses down to 3.0 GeV and couplings down to $3.2\times10^{-4}$ GeV$^{-1}$, reporting its strongest limits for masses from 3 to 5 GeV.
- **Evidence type:** Experimental; LHCb forward detector analysis of PbPb collision data.
- **Caveat:** The paper reports search limits, not an axion-like-particle detection; sensitivity is tied to the recorded luminosity, forward acceptance, and the assumed exclusive production and decay channel.
- **Announcement type:** revision, announcement-batch date 2026-11-05
- **Themes:** Particle, nuclear & high-energy physics; Instrumentation & detectors

### 5. [DESI DR2 Results III: The Mass of the Milky Way](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05518)
- **Abstract:** The DESI Collaboration combines four dynamical models, calibrated on cosmological mock surveys, to infer the Milky Way’s mass from 10,036 distant halo stars in DESI DR2.
- **Authors:** DESI Collaboration, C. Aganze, J. Aguilar, S. Ahlen, C. Allende Prieto et al.
- **arXiv:** `2611.05518v1` (published 2026-11-03T19:15:56Z; categories: `astro-ph.GA`, `astro-ph.CO`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** A DESI DR2 key-project analysis uses the largest RRL/BHB halo-star samples of their kind in the authors’ account, compares four methods on mocks, and combines method-specific estimates while modeling residual correlations.
- **Claimed result:** The authors report $M(<80\,\mathrm{kpc})=6.36^{+0.53}_{-0.48}\times10^{11}\,M_\odot$ and virial mass $M_{200c}=1.19^{+0.14}_{-0.12}\times10^{12}\,M_\odot$.
- **Evidence type:** Observational and computational; DESI DR2 six-dimensional halo-star data, with Gaia DR3-like uncertainties and cosmological mock-survey calibration.
- **Caveat:** Corrections depend on the mock-based treatment of disequilibrium, selection functions, and differences among dynamical methods and tracers.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, announcement-batch date 2026-11-05
- **Themes:** Astrophysics; Computational & data methods

### 6. [Near-Quantum-Limited Readout for the Princeton Axion Search using a sub-GHz Kinetic Inductance Traveling Wave Parametric Amplifier](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.06761)
- **Abstract:** The authors characterize a kinetic-inductance traveling-wave parametric amplifier operating at 300–700 MHz, reporting gain above 30 dB and system noise of 2.5–3.5 quanta.
- **Authors:** Joe Wiedemann, Roman Kolevatov, Jianyang Fu, Joelle-Marie Begin, Nate Otto et al.
- **arXiv:** `2611.06761v1` (published 2026-11-04T16:39:54Z; categories: `quant-ph`, `astro-ph.IM`, `hep-ex`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** It is cross-listed in quantum physics, astrophysical instrumentation, and experimental high-energy physics; the paper gives direct gain/noise measurements in a sub-GHz band with applications to axion-search readout.
- **Claimed result:** The authors report more than 30 dB peak gain and 2.5–3.5 quanta system noise across the stated operating band, with performance meeting the stated Princeton Axion Search requirements.
- **Evidence type:** Experimental; TiN kinetic-inductance amplifier characterized with a variable-temperature noise source and readout-chain tests.
- **Caveat:** This is an amplifier characterization, not an axion detection; the measured system noise may include losses between the noise source and amplifier input, and the authors call for further testing.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, announcement-batch date 2026-11-05
- **Themes:** Instrumentation & detectors; Quantum information

### 7. [New Neutron Polarizabilities from Deuterium Compton Scattering at HI$\gamma$S](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.06609)
- **Abstract:** The authors analyze 61 MeV deuterium Compton-scattering measurements at HI$\gamma$S with chiral EFT to extract neutron electric and magnetic scalar polarizabilities.
- **Authors:** D. Godagama, M. W. Ahmed, B. Crowe, G. Feldman, F. Friesen et al.
- **arXiv:** `2611.06609v1` (published 2026-11-04T14:58:24Z; categories: `nucl-ex`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** It reports direct cross-section measurements with explicit statistical, Baldin-sum-rule, and theoretical uncertainty components and a stated 12% reduction in statistical uncertainty.
- **Claimed result:** The authors obtain $\alpha^n_{E1}=12.25\pm1.1\,({\rm stat})\pm0.3\,({\rm BSR})\pm0.8\,({\rm th})$ and $\beta^n_{M1}=2.95\mp1.1\,({\rm stat})\mp0.3\,({\rm BSR})\mp0.8\,({\rm th})$, in units of $10^{-4}\,\mathrm{fm}^3$.
- **Evidence type:** Experimental; circularly polarized 61 MeV gamma beam on liquid deuterium, with NaI(Tl) detectors at three scattering angles, interpreted using chiral EFT.
- **Caveat:** These are neutron polarizabilities inferred from a deuteron target through a chiral-EFT analysis; statistical and theoretical uncertainties remain appreciable.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, announcement-batch date 2026-11-05
- **Themes:** Particle, nuclear & high-energy physics

### 8. [Quantum Noise Spectroscopy of Criticality in an Atomically Thin Magnet](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.05614)
- **Abstract:** In a v3 revision, the authors use nitrogen-vacancy-center $T_2$ noise magnetometry on trilayer CrSBr to measure critical fluctuations and infer dynamical and correlation-length exponents.
- **Authors:** Mark E. Ziffer, Francisco Machado, Benedikt Ursprung, Artur Lozovoi, Aya Batoul Tazi et al.
- **arXiv:** `2407.05614v3` (published 2026-11-03T23:51:24Z; categories: `cond-mat.mes-hall`, `quant-ph`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** A substantive v3 replacement appears in today’s batch; it is cross-listed between condensed matter and quantum physics and reports quantitative NV-center noise measurements against competing criticality models.
- **Claimed result:** The authors find that the CrSBr transition is Ising-like but deviates from short-range Ising and XY predictions, attributing the difference to long-range dipolar interactions and easy-axis anisotropy.
- **Evidence type:** Experimental; NV-center spin-decoherence noise magnetometry on a trilayer CrSBr sample, with model fitting and simulations.
- **Caveat:** The universality-class interpretation depends on the noise model and is based on one material and thickness; extension across samples and control parameters is needed.
- **Announcement type:** revision, announcement-batch date 2026-11-05
- **Themes:** Condensed matter & materials; Quantum information

### 9. [Hall-Density Collapse as Direct Evidence for Percolative Conduction Onset in Monolayer MoS$_2$ Transistors](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.06815)
- **Abstract:** Temperature-dependent transport and Hall measurements on MoS$_2$ monolayer transistors reveal a Hall-density collapse and vanishing activation energy at a carrier density the authors identify as a percolation threshold.
- **Authors:** R. V. Upadya, M. Introna, X. Wu, C. J. L. de la Rosa, K. Banerjee et al.
- **arXiv:** `2611.06815v1` (published 2026-11-04T17:29:48Z; categories: `cond-mat.mes-hall`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** The paper combines two complementary transport signatures, studies industry-relevant monolayer FETs, and is marked as submitted to Physical Review Letters; its interpretation bears on a debated metal–insulator transition.
- **Claimed result:** The authors attribute the conduction onset to classical percolation driven by dielectric-interface disorder, rather than channel quality alone, and report activation barriers exceeding room-temperature thermal energies.
- **Evidence type:** Experimental; temperature-dependent electrical transport and Hall measurements in MoS$_2$ FETs with amorphous HfO$_2$ dielectrics.
- **Caveat:** The percolation interpretation is tied to the studied device and dielectric interface; the preprint has been submitted to a journal but is not thereby peer-reviewed.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, announcement-batch date 2026-11-05
- **Themes:** Condensed matter & materials

- **Authors:** Laurens Lootens, Hersh Singh
- **arXiv:** `2611.06880v1` (published 2026-11-04T18:36:32Z; categories: `hep-lat`, `cond-mat.str-el`, `hep-th`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** The work is cross-listed in lattice high-energy theory, condensed matter, and high-energy theory; it preserves the chiral symmetry and anomaly on the lattice and compares a nonperturbative simulation with analytic predictions.
- **Claimed result:** For the 1+1D Abelian 34-50 model, the authors report that an $N=6$ finite-dimensional local Hilbert space reproduces the expected continuum infrared physics in their infinite-MPS simulation.
- **Evidence type:** Computational and theoretical; Hamiltonian lattice construction with $\mathbb{Z}_N$ gauge fields and infinite matrix-product-state simulation.
- **Caveat:** The demonstrated result is for a specific 1+1D Abelian model and finite $N$; it does not establish a general non-Abelian or higher-dimensional construction.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, announcement-batch date 2026-11-05
- **Themes:** Particle, nuclear & high-energy physics; Condensed matter & materials; Computational & data methods

## Open Problems and Research Directions

- **Open problem (authors’ stated limitation):** The $R^2$-gravity bound is based on an inspiral model truncated at 0PN and two BNS events (#1). **Direction (synthesis):** develop higher-order and tidal waveform corrections and test them on additional high-SNR BNS observations.
- **Open problem (authors’ stated limitation):** M87’s distance uncertainty now dominates the statistical error in its stellar-dynamical black-hole mass (#3). **Direction (synthesis):** improve the distance constraint and test the mass estimate against additional stellar-dynamical models and future EHT analyses.
- **Open problem (authors’ stated uncertainty):** For the KI-TWPA, source-to-device loss may contribute to the measured system noise (#6). **Direction (synthesis):** de-embed input losses and characterize noise under the field and integration conditions of the axion-search cavity.
- **Open problem (measurement uncertainty):** The neutron polarizability extraction retains statistical and chiral-EFT theory uncertainties (#7). **Direction (synthesis):** additional deuterium Compton-scattering data and EFT sensitivity studies could test the extracted values.
- **Open problem (model dependence):** DESI’s joint Milky Way mass estimates require mock-based corrections for disequilibrium and method-specific systematics (#5). **Direction (synthesis):** compare with independent halo tracers and test how alternative mock populations affect the joint result.
- **Open problem (authors’ stated scope):** The ALP search covers a particular exclusive photon-fusion channel, forward acceptance, and current PbPb luminosity (#4). **Direction (synthesis):** extend the search with larger samples and complementary production or decay channels.
- **Open problem (authors’ stated scope):** The CrSBr criticality result depends on noise-model interpretation for a trilayer sample (#8). **Direction (synthesis):** vary thickness, field, and temperature and compare with independent probes of the dynamic critical exponents.
- **Open problem (authors’ stated scope):** The chiral-gauge construction has been demonstrated for one 1+1D Abelian model at finite $N$ (#10). **Direction (synthesis):** study continuum scaling with larger $N$ and test other anomaly-cancelled models before extrapolating to broader gauge theories.
