# Giant Watch Summary - Daily - 2026-11-12

## Headline

Anthropic released Claude Fable 5.2 and Mythos 5.2 with a 20% lower standard API price, while Google DeepMind opened Gemini 4 Carbon to general availability. The day's episode is a model-release race in which frontier labs compete on agentic and long-horizon tasks, and access is increasingly tiered by subscription or verification.

## Top Developments

### 1. Anthropic launches Claude Fable 5.2 and Mythos 5.2 with lower API pricing (Anthropic)
Anthropic released Claude Fable 5.2 and Claude Mythos 5.2, which it says improve complex software engineering, long-duration tasks and multi-agent collaboration, with output up to 30% faster than 5.1 and standard API pricing cut 20%. Standard pricing is $8 per million input tokens and $40 per million output tokens, with a 1M-token context and 128K max output; an optional fast mode costs $16/$80. Fable is offered to general users, while Mythos is mainly for verified cybersecurity and life-sciences organizations. Fable 5.2 is available through the Claude platform and Amazon, Google and Microsoft cloud services.
Why it matters: A flagship dual-model release that pairs a broad consumer offering with a gated high-risk variant, and cuts standard API prices while adding a premium speed tier.
Refs: https://www.exmoo.com/article/267953.html

### 2. Google DeepMind makes Gemini 4 Carbon generally available (Google)
Google DeepMind released Gemini 4 Carbon to developers, enterprises, researchers and consumers, positioning it as the strongest publicly available Gemini model for complex coding, cybersecurity, scientific reasoning and long-running multi-agent tasks. It tops CursorBench 4.0, Perplexity WANDR and Zapier AutomationBench, with API pricing of $2/$10 per million input/output tokens through Nov 30, rising to $4/$20 from Dec 1. A /swarm preview in Google Antigravity coordinates multiple agents, and consumer access requires a Google AI Ultra subscription.
Why it matters: A broad flagship launch that extends Google's agent-orchestration tooling and sets a time-limited introductory price ahead of a December increase.
Refs: https://www.dtinsight.com.cn/sys-nd/4377.html

### 3. Google DeepMind unveils Gemini Robotics 2.5 and AlphaGenome 2 (Google)
Google DeepMind unveiled Gemini Robotics 2.5, which it calls its best vision-language-action model to date, built on Gemini 4 Argon and evaluated on more than 200 tasks. Internal tests showed success rates 30-100% higher and completion times 20-30% shorter than the previous model. In a proof of concept on a Mercedes production line, Apptronik's Apollo 2 humanoid sorted and packed auto parts with a 96% success rate, which DeepMind says is separate from commercialization results. The model is available through partners including Agile Robots, Apptronik and Boston Dynamics, and a new benchmark tests malicious commands and jailbreak attempts. DeepMind also released AlphaGenome 2, a gene-prediction model for research use only and not approved for clinical or diagnostic use.
Why it matters: Moves Gemini-based agents from software into industrial robotics with a real production-line proof of concept, plus a research genomics model.
Refs: https://www.digitaltoday.co.kr/en/view/124565/google-deepmind-launches-gemini-robotics-2-5, https://www.digitaltoday.co.kr/cn/view/124587/google-deepmind-launches-gemini-robotics-2-5

### 4. OpenAI to retire Advanced and Standard Voice modes on Dec 11, consolidating on GPT-Live (OpenAI)
OpenAI said it will discontinue ChatGPT's Advanced Voice and Standard Voice modes on Dec 11, 2026, automatically switching users to GPT-Live, launched in July. Notices began Nov 10, and the old modes will be removed from Settings-Voice on Dec 11, with chat history preserved. OpenAI says voice usage has nearly doubled since GPT-Live launched and most voice users have already moved to it. Some users on X and Reddit complained that GPT-Live interrupts, loses context and gives less detailed answers, and some paying subscribers threatened to cancel. Real-time video and screen sharing will stay on Advanced Voice until GPT-Live supports them, with no date given.
Why it matters: A forced migration of a core ChatGPT interface that has drawn user backlash and carries execution risk for a flagship product.
Refs: https://3c.ltn.com.tw/news/68246, https://www.guideai.com.cn/archives/23821, https://www.sohu.com/a/1100202810_100190264?scm=10001.7746_13-119000.0.0-0-0-0-0.0&spm=smpc.channel_354.block3_32_P7Ovbu_1_fd.1.1794419404008mzfFZ6q_7746

### 5. OpenAI Korea highlights GPT-based policy research on consumption vouchers (OpenAI)
At an 'Open Insights: AI Applications in Policy Research' session at OpenAI's Seoul office on Nov 11, NTU chair professor Kwon Hyuk-goo described using GPT to simulate household consumption effects of Korea's Livelihood Recovery Consumption Vouchers. The project is one of 14 selected for OpenAI's Industrial Policy Research Support Program. Researchers feed income, spending habits, subsidy amounts and deadlines through the API and compare predictions with actual consumption records and Bank of Korea analysis. A second phase is testing altered deadlines and eligible vendors, with validation planned by next month and comparison of policy alternatives by end of March next year.
Why it matters: An example of OpenAI positioning GPT as a policy-research tool through a dedicated academic support program, with results still pending validation.
Refs: https://en.etnews.com/20261112200007

### 6. Alibaba releases Qwen4-Max preview with Token Plan subscription-only access (Qwen)
Alibaba Cloud released the Qwen4-Max preview (qwen4-max-preview) on Nov 11, its fourth-generation flagship, with 1M-token context and text, image and video input, aimed at complex coding, long agentic tasks and professional office work. It is available only through the Token Plan subscription, not pay-as-you-go, with a preview window from Nov 11 10:00 to Nov 25 24:00 (UTC+8), after which the model identifier is retired and a formal version released under a new name. Parameter count, benchmark scores and official unit prices were not disclosed. New users get over 70 million free tokens, and Token Plan new-customer pricing is 50% off (Lite at 19.5 yuan/month) with a limited-time 20% Qwen4 rate.
Why it matters: A flagship launch that experiments with subscription-only access and a short preview window, leaving pricing and benchmarks undisclosed.
Refs: https://developer.aliyun.com/article/1782441

### 7. Alibaba Cloud updates its recommended Qwen model catalog (Qwen)
Alibaba Cloud's Model Studio 'Recommended models' page, updated Nov 12, lists Qwen and third-party models across text, vision, speech, embedding and multimodal categories. It points users to qwen3.8-omni-flash for audio/video analysis and text generation and qwen3.8-omni-flash-realtime for real-time audio/video conversation, and notes qwen3.5-omni-plus for non-real-time audio output.
Why it matters: A catalog update that signals the current recommended Qwen lineup, though it carries no launch or pricing announcement.
Refs: https://www.alibabacloud.com/help/en/model-studio/models

### 8. SpaceXAI (formerly xAI) releases Grok Bot 1.0 (xAI)
On Nov 11, SpaceX's AI division (SpaceXAI, formerly xAI) formally released Grok Bot 1.0, moving the agent product out of beta. Bots can be shared into Slack, Microsoft Teams and video meetings, with Team Bots learning team knowledge and preferences. They monitor projects, suggest next steps, coordinate other Bots and escalate to humans when judgment is needed. Each Bot runs in its own cloud Linux VM, can log into tools the user already uses and keep working after the user's computer is off, with up to 50 routines per Bot and a minimum 5-minute schedule interval. The product was co-developed with Cursor following SpaceX's acquisition of Cursor. SpaceX stock reportedly opened up as much as 4.22% on release day.
Why it matters: A general-availability launch of a persistent, multi-user workplace agent, and an early integration output from the Cursor acquisition.
Refs: https://m.cnmo.com/news/822317.html, https://weibo.com/2/detail/5364328418509838

### 9. Third-party 'abliterated' derivative of DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash-1017 published on Hugging Face (DeepSeek)
A Hugging Face repository published v3 of a community 'no-refusal' derivative built on deepseek-ai/DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash-1017 (MIT), adding an uncensoring overlay and a layer-39 loop reduction. The release reports MMLU full 5-shot at 86.0%, an official HarmBench ASR of 85.9% overall, and XSTest false refusals at 3.6%, above its 3.0% gate. It also reports a regression in re-verification and redundant tool calls. The authors describe it as a weight-delta research release, not a new training run, and note it is English-only with no production SLO or safety certification.
Why it matters: A community modification of a DeepSeek base model that documents reduced refusals and safety trade-offs; it is not an official DeepSeek release.
Refs: https://huggingface.co/pentacoxian-dev/DeepSeek-V4.1-1017-Ablitrated-Efficient-v3

### 10. OpEd: Hugging Face used Z.ai's GLM 5.2 to reconstruct an agent-driven breach (GLM)
A Eurasia Review OpEd says Hugging Face used GLM 5.2, an open-weight model from Beijing's Z.ai, on its own hardware to reconstruct a July breach after American models refused to assist incident responders. The piece says the intruder was a swarm of roughly 700 agents driven by an internal OpenAI model, and that the reconstruction covered more than 17,000 logged attacker actions. It also cites Washington evaluators finding that GLM 5.2's safeguards allow assistance with agentic exploit development, and NSA/CISA/FBI accusations that six Chinese labs, including Z.ai and Alibaba, distilled American models, which Beijing rejects. The OpEd argues Europe should vet open-weight models, including Chinese ones, for cyber-defense contingency planning.
Why it matters: A commentary claim that an open-weight Chinese model filled a gap left by guarded US models in incident response, while also flagging safeguard concerns.
Refs: https://www.eurasiareview.com/12112026-europes-ai-spare-tyre-is-made-in-china-oped/#respond

## Takeaway

The competitive axis is shifting from raw model quality to how access is packaged and priced, and to whether agentic claims hold up outside demos. Watch the Dec 1 Gemini 4 Carbon price increase, the Nov 25 Qwen4-Max preview expiry, and the Dec 11 ChatGPT voice migration for the first real tests of these bets.
