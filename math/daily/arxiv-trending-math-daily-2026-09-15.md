- **Assumptions and setting:** Type I analytic one-frequency quasiperiodic Schrödinger operators; the conclusion concerns all irrational frequencies within a global open class of potentials.
- **Caveat:** This is a new arXiv preprint; the claimed solution and the new Type I framework require expert verification of the full argument.

### 3. [A universality result for the critical 2d stochastic heat flow](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.15832)

- **Assumptions and setting:** Models must satisfy the paper’s linear-flow property; discrete models have finite-range structure, while continuum examples use Gaussian noise with smooth compactly supported covariance.
- **Caveat:** The convergence mode is finite-dimensional distribution convergence, not automatically a stronger path-space topology; the scope is limited to the hypotheses defining the model classes.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, 15 September 2026 announcement batch
- **Themes:** Probability & statistics; Mathematical physics
