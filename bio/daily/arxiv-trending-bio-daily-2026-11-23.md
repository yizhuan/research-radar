### 1. [Receptor Abundance Alone Underpredicts Red Blood Cell Adhesion in Gaucher Disease](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.27960)
- **Abstract:** A calibrated stochastic-bond red-cell model suggests the measured 1.3-fold Lu/BCAM receptor increase alone underpredicts Gaucher-disease cell adhesion, while active receptor state may account for the gap.
- **Authors:** Zhaojie Chai, Pierre A. Buffet, Melanie Franco, George Em Karniadakis
- **arXiv:** `2611.27960v1` (published 2026-11-20T18:39:09Z; categories: `physics.bio-ph`, `cond-mat.soft`, `physics.med-ph`, `q-bio.CB`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** Collector score 0.75, entirely from three relevant cross-listings (0.25 each); Semantic Scholar records 0 citations and 0 influential citations at the snapshot. No candidate-level Hugging Face or Hacker News attention is recorded.
- **Claimed contribution:** The authors combine donor-level adhesion measurements with a simulated flow-adhesion model; the reported adhesion is 1.6–1.7 times the abundance-only prediction, and matching it requires more binding-competent receptors, consistent with elevated receptor phosphorylation.
- **Caveat:** This is model-based interpretation of donor measurements, not a prospective clinical study; source donor cohorts were modest and laminin lot effects could not be assessed for too few donors.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, 2026-11-23
- **Themes:** Molecular & cell biology, Biomedical & translational medicine

## Trending Research Themes
The selected papers cluster around three methods-rich areas. Protein structure and molecular ensembles appear in BioEmu2, CrypticFlow, and the CORE Score paper: respectively, generative dynamics emulation, apo-to-holo conformation generation, and model-agreement-based confidence estimation. Spatial and microscopy computation is represented by GaLF-ST, MambaSplit, and HERMES, which reconstruct spatial expression, unmix fluorescence channels, and make pathology readouts auditable across paired modalities. Assay and mechanistic translation appear in the lipopolymer biosensor and the Gaucher red-cell adhesion study, but both remain preclinical or model-based evidence rather than clinical validation. These are patterns within eight selected arXiv papers, not evidence of a field-wide shift.

## Open Problems and Research Directions
Author-stated limitations include the need for larger experimental datasets to validate BioEmu2’s slower kinetics; unmeasured laminin-lot effects in the Gaucher red-cell source data; and pending appendix/code release for GaLF-ST. Follow-up synthesis: test biomolecular ensemble and conformational predictions against independent experimental structural and kinetic measurements, with targets and conditions held out; replicate GaLF-ST, MambaSplit, and HERMES across independent tissues, instruments, labs, and patient cohorts, reporting patient-level splits and calibration; and test the biosensor using blinded, repeated native-matrix samples with orthogonal extracellular-vesicle measurements. For the Gaucher model, a prospective donor study measuring phosphorylated/binding-competent Lu/BCAM alongside flow adhesion could distinguish receptor abundance from activation state. These are research proposals, not evidence of clinical utility.
