## Trending Research Themes

- **Geometric structure and stability:** Ge and Zhou identify Kähler--Finsler with Kähler--Berwald geometry and derive decomposition/rigidity consequences; Li and Li lower the local closeness requirement for $G_2$-flow stability to a $C^{0}$ neighborhood. These are distinct arguments, not evidence of one shared technique.
- **Symplectic capacities as obstruction and asymptotic tools:** Li and Zhang's Chiu--Tamarkin complex both produces counterexamples to a product equality and yields a Weyl law; this is a single substantial signal rather than a field-wide shift.
- **Algebraic models of combinatorial behavior:** Finkel, Fu, and Wu bridge ReLU activation-pattern combinatorics, algebraic varieties, and learning-theoretic functional dimension. Their explicit algebraic classification is conditional on shallow-network and wall-graph hypotheses.
- **Different decision and dynamics questions:** Özer and Wolfert show why Pareto ordering need not preserve aggregate preference; Koo, Lee, and Shim characterize contraction under shared stochastic forcing. Both touch optimization/control, but their methods and problems are separate.
- **Analysis beyond doubling assumptions:** Conde Alonso, Martini, and Vallarino replace metric/doubling/martingale structure with recursive admissible-set partitions for singular-integral estimates; this is a cross-list bridge to functional analysis and probability, not evidence that all endpoint analysis is moving in this direction.
- **Logic and algebraic geometry:** Bentzen's nonclassical semantics for a restricted Frege fragment and Ajit--Apsley's reduction-mod-$p$ criterion for klt singularities are isolated results in distinct subfields; no common method is implied.

## Open Problems and Research Directions

- **Open problem (authors' stated gap):** Finkel, Fu, and Wu prove generic pattern stability at arbitrary depth but leave generic combinatorial stability unresolved; their discussion also points toward the biased-network conjecture. [Paper 6](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.20346)
- **Open question (authors' conjectural view):** Li and Zhang report that the $p=2$ symplectic $p$-product equality may hold for their capacities, while expecting failures for $p\ne2$ and $k\ge2$. This is an expectation, not a theorem. [Paper 3](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21220)
- **Open problem (reported in the $G_2$ paper's introduction):** Long-time existence under a bounded-scalar-curvature condition remains open in the broader Laplacian-flow literature; the paper proves local stability near a torsion-free structure instead. [Paper 2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26144)
- **Research direction (synthesis):** Test whether additional geometric or transversality hypotheses yield generic combinatorial stability for deep ReLU networks, first without biases and then with biases. [Paper 6](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.20346)
- **Research direction (synthesis):** Use the Chiu--Tamarkin chain model to identify the precise homological obstruction behind product equality and test the authors' $p=2$ conjectural case against the $p\ne2$ counterexample regime. [Paper 3](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21220)
- **Research direction (synthesis):** Investigate how far the $C^{0}$ stability estimates for compact $G_2$ manifolds extend beyond the paper's compact, near-equilibrium setting, while preserving the closedness and cohomology constraints. [Paper 2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26144)
