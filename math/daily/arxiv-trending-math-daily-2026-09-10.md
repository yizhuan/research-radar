### 1. [Aldous' spectral gap phenomena in stochastic exchange models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.10450)
- **Abstract:** The authors prove that a broad class of conservative exchange models has a spectral gap attained by an observable of degree at most two, settling a recent Alon–Puder conjecture and identifying when one- versus two-particle modes dominate.
- **Authors:** Pietro Caputo, Matteo Quattropani, Federico Sau
- **arXiv:** `2609.10450v1` (published 2026-09-09 16:59:40 UTC; categories: `math.PR`, `math-ph`, `math.FA`, `math.SP`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** 75-page scope, four mathematical cross-lists, a concrete conjecture resolution, and a broad extension to KMP, Kac, harmonic, immediate-exchange, averaging, nonreversible, and boundary-driven models. Semantic Scholar citation counts were unavailable because its API returned HTTP 429; no measured attention signal is claimed.
- **Claimed result:** For exchange dynamics with Dirichlet reversible measures, the spectral gap is always realized by a polynomial of degree at most two in the energy variables; the paper also characterizes the geometry-dependent transition between one- and two-particle modes.
- **Assumptions and setting:** Conservative continuous-spin systems on weighted graphs or hypergraphs, with the stated exchange structure and Dirichlet reversible measures; boundary-driven variants are treated separately and are generally nonreversible.
- **Caveat:** This is a new, unrefereed preprint; the abstract reports extensive claims whose technical verification requires reading the 75-page paper.

### 2. [Superpolynomial lower bounds for vertex numbers of real projective space triangulations via a topological Figiel-Lindenstrauss-Milman theorem](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.10402)
- **Abstract:** The paper proves that every simplicial triangulation of real projective d-space has exp(Ω(√d)) vertices, determining the minimum vertex number up to exp(d^{1/2+o(1)}), with further applications to topological graph coloring and sign complexes.
- **Authors:** Florian Frick, Kaave Hosseini, Eric Myzelev, Arya Narnapatti, Aliaksei Vasileuski
- **arXiv:** `2609.10402v1` (published 2026-09-09 16:22:33 UTC; categories: `math.CO`, `math.GT`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** A sharp asymptotic resolution of a minimum-size problem, a new topological generalization of the Figiel–Lindenstrauss–Milman inequality, and applications spanning combinatorics and geometric topology. Citation/attention data were unavailable or too sparse to use.
- **Claimed result:** The minimum number μ_d of vertices in a triangulation of real projective d-space satisfies μ_d = exp(d^{1/2+o(1)}); the key index bound is obtained from a trace estimate for a constrained Hessian and Morse theory.
- **Assumptions and setting:** Simplicial triangulations of real projective space and finite strongly regular CW complexes with free cellular involution; additional applications use triangle-free topologically t-chromatic graphs and sign complexes.
- **Caveat:** The asymptotic statement relies on existing matching constructions and the authors' stated topological inequality; both the new proof and the exact scope of the applications need expert checking.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, 2026-09-10 announcement batch
- **Themes:** Combinatorics & discrete mathematics; Geometry & topology
