- **Abstract:** A Bayesian combination of nuclear-structure, INDRA-FAZIA, chiral-EFT, and astrophysical information reduces the posterior probability of nucleonic direct-Urca cooling below 1% for neutron stars at or below 1.4 solar masses within the adopted metamodel.
- **Authors:** Gabriele Montefusco, Pietro Klausner, Marco Antonelli, Caterina Ciampi, Diego Gruyer, Francesca Gulminelli
- **arXiv:** `2609.24940v1` (published 2026-09-21 17:39 UTC; categories: `nucl-th`, `astro-ph.HE`, `astro-ph.SR`, `nucl-ex`)

- **Claimed result:** Laboratory nuclear data substantially tighten the central proton-fraction posterior and strongly disfavor electronic direct-Urca onset at or below 1.4 solar masses within the nucleonic metamodel.
- **Evidence type:** computational/inferential; Bayesian equation-of-state metamodel combining nuclear observables, INDRA-FAZIA isospin transport, chiral-EFT calculations, and neutron-star observations.
- **Caveat:** The conclusion is model-dependent: massive-star proton fractions remain less certain, and the analysis concerns nucleonic direct Urca rather than every possible fast-cooling mechanism.

## Trending Research Themes

- Precision constraints are moving across scales: ATLAS tests TeV-scale resonances, while nuclear laboratory data constrain neutron-star composition and cooling (2609.24950, 2609.24940).
- Detector systematics are treated as physics results in their own right: the LIGO stray-light study turns baffle behavior into quantitative noise requirements for gravitational-wave instruments (2609.24743).
- Tunable quantum platforms recur across condensed matter and quantum information: moiré graphene probes Kondo physics, nickelates connect intertwined orders to pairing, and a microwave cavity is proposed as a long-range neutral-atom bus (2609.24734, 2609.24857, 2609.24933).
- Large, reusable survey products remain a major astronomy driver: EPOCHS-DR2 packages JWST catalogs and fitting products, while the flash-reionization analysis reuses Planck releases to test model dependence (2609.24987, 2609.24989).

## Open Problems and Research Directions

- Direct-Urca cooling: The neutron-star conclusion depends on the nucleonic metamodel and remains less restrictive for massive stars (2609.24940). Follow-up work should propagate alternative exotic-matter and high-density equations of state and seek cooling observations that discriminate among mechanisms.
- Stray-light mitigation: LIGO model/data agreement is only within factors of 3–10 in the reported tests (2609.24743). A useful next step is a broader baffle and auxiliary-arm campaign across detector configurations, tied to a full frequency-dependent noise budget.
- Kondo interpretation in moiré graphene: The transport signatures are compatible with Kondo singlets and a heavy-fermion liquid but do not by themselves establish the full phase diagram (2609.24734). Spectroscopic probes and independent devices should test the localized-moment and insulating-state interpretations.
- Nickelate superconductivity: The proposed interference mechanism and 100-K-scale calculated transition are model-dependent (2609.24857). Experiments that resolve layer- and orbital-selective order while varying strain, doping, and dimensionality would test the predicted selection rules.
- Flash reionization: The PR4 mild preference becomes an upper bound with PR3 and is not a detection (2609.24989). Joint analyses with newer polarization, lensing, and high-redshift galaxy constraints could determine whether the parameterization is warranted.
- JWST high-redshift candidates: Most EPOCHS-DR2 candidates above $z=10.5$ lack spectroscopic confirmation (2609.24987). Prioritized NIRSpec follow-up and consistent selection-function modeling are the clearest tests of the extreme-redshift tail.
