### 1. [A New Precise Measurement of the $e^+e^-\to\pi^+\pi^-(\gamma)$ Cross Section with the Initial-State Radiation Method at BaBar](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.04621)
- **Abstract:** The BaBar Collaboration reports a new $e^+e^-\to\pi^+\pi^-(\gamma)$ cross-section measurement using 460 fb$^{-1}$ of initial-state-radiation data and derives its hadronic-vacuum-polarization contribution to muon $g-2$.
- **Authors:** BaBar Collaboration
- **arXiv:** `2611.04621v1` (published 2026-11-03 10:10:49 UTC; categories: `hep-ex`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** Full 460 fb$^{-1}$ sample; a new pion/muon separation using angular distributions; and a precisely reported HVP integral make this the batch's clearest precision-data result for a high-profile observable. The shorter same-day BaBar upload `2611.04596` reports the same measurement and is not counted separately.
- **Claimed result:** The authors give $a_\mu^{\pi\pi}=525.9\pm3.5_{\rm stat}\pm3.8_{\rm syst}$ (in $10^{-10}$) for the new 2026 measurement over 0.3–1.4 GeV, and $517.8\pm2.4_{\rm stat}\pm3.3_{\rm syst}$ when combined with their 2009 result.
- **Evidence type:** experimental; 460 fb$^{-1}$ of on- and off-$\Upsilon(4S)$ BaBar data analyzed with initial-state radiation and pion/muon angular distributions.
- **Caveat:** This is the two-pion contribution over 0.3–1.4 GeV, not a complete muon-$g-2$ prediction; the paper notes that statistical separation is less precise at the low- and high-energy ends and reports nonzero systematic uncertainty.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, Wednesday, 4 November 2026
- **Themes:** Particle, nuclear & high-energy physics

### 2. [A New Bound on Light-by-Light Scattering in the Low-Energy Regime](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.04531)
- **Abstract:** The authors collide an X-ray free-electron-laser probe with an intense optical laser and use dark-field detection and polarimetry to set a low-energy light-by-light-scattering bound substantially below previous laboratory limits.
- **Authors:** Aimé Matheron, Michal Šmíd, Felix Karbstein, Sebastian Göde, Willi Hippler et al. (BIREF@HIBEF Collaboration)
- **arXiv:** `2611.04531v1` (published 2026-11-03 08:59:53 UTC; categories: `hep-ex`, `physics.optics`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** Cross-listed between high-energy experiment and optics; the EuXFEL/laser experiment reports a low-energy bound about ten orders tighter than earlier experimental limits, while explicitly distinguishing a bound from a detection.
- **Claimed result:** The authors report no significant photon excess and set the strongest direct low-energy experimental bound on light-by-light scattering to date, improving previous experimental limits by roughly ten orders of magnitude.
- **Evidence type:** experimental; X-ray free-electron-laser and high-intensity optical-laser collisions at the European XFEL, with dark-field detection and polarimetry.
- **Caveat:** No light-by-light signal was detected, and the paper says the present setup did not reach the baseline sensitivity needed to detect the QED-predicted signal.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, Wednesday, 4 November 2026
- **Themes:** Particle, nuclear & high-energy physics; Instrumentation & detectors

### 3. [Constraints on a Long-Lived X17 Vector Boson with FASER](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03904)
- **Abstract:** Reinterpreting and combining previous FASER dark-photon and axion-like-particle searches as X17 searches, the collaboration finds no excess and excludes a previously unconstrained low-electron-coupling interval.
- **Authors:** FASER Collaboration
- **arXiv:** `2611.03904v1` (published 2026-11-02 20:11:05 UTC; categories: `hep-ex`, `hep-ph`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** Cross-listed in experiment and phenomenology; it converts existing Run 3 FASER data into a quantitative new exclusion in an X17 parameter region that had remained open after earlier beam-dump reinterpretations.
- **Claimed result:** The authors report no excess in 13.6 TeV $pp$ data from 2022 and 2022–2023 searches, excluding $6\times10^{-5}<\epsilon_e<1.2\times10^{-4}$ for the studied 17 MeV protophobic-vector scenario.
- **Evidence type:** experimental; reinterpretation of FASER dark-photon and ALP searches using 13.6 TeV LHC Run 3 data.
- **Caveat:** The exclusion is conditional on the assumed X17 vector-boson model and couplings; the paper leaves a high-coupling window open, and the X17 itself is not established by this result.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, Wednesday, 4 November 2026
- **Themes:** Particle, nuclear & high-energy physics; Instrumentation & detectors

### 4. [Precise and accurate nuclear electric quadrupole moment of $^{223}$Ra from molecular spectroscopy](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.04696)
- **Abstract:** Hyperfine-resolved spectroscopy of radioactive $^{223}$Ra$^{19}$F, combined with relativistic coupled-cluster calculations, yields a sub-percent quadrupole-moment determination for $^{223}$Ra.
- **Authors:** C. M. Fajardo-Zambrano, M. Athanasakis-Kaklamanakis, L. Lalanne, J. R. Reilly, O. Ahmad et al.
- **arXiv:** `2611.04696v1` (published 2026-11-03 11:19:17 UTC; categories: `nucl-ex`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** Direct spectroscopy of a short-lived isotope, threefold better total precision than earlier atomic/ionic determinations, and a reported 0.73% total uncertainty make this a concrete nuclear-structure measurement.
- **Claimed result:** The authors determine $Q_s(^{223}\mathrm{Ra})=+1.211(7)_{\rm stat}(2)_{\rm syst}(5)_{\rm theo}\,eb$, with a 0.73% total relative uncertainty.
- **Evidence type:** experimental; hyperfine-resolved collinear resonance-ionization spectroscopy of radioactive RaF, calibrated with relativistic coupled-cluster calculations.
- **Caveat:** The extracted moment depends on the calculated molecular electric-field gradient; the quoted uncertainty includes a nonzero theoretical component as well as statistical and systematic terms.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, Wednesday, 4 November 2026
- **Themes:** Particle, nuclear & high-energy physics; Atomic, molecular & optical physics

### 5. [3D dark matter map of the JWST COSMOS-Web field using diffusion-based generative priors](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03786)
- **Abstract:** A weak-lensing forward model and simulation-trained diffusion prior are used to infer a probabilistic three-dimensional matter map from JWST imaging of the COSMOS-Web field, across 20 lens planes to about $z=2$.
- **Authors:** Diana Scognamiglio, Brandon Zhao, Katherine L. Bouman, Olivier Doré, Caitlin M. Casey et al.
- **arXiv:** `2611.03786v1` (published 2026-11-02 19:00:01 UTC; categories: `astro-ph.CO`, `cs.LG`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** Combines a major JWST imaging field with a cross-listed machine-learning method; the authors report structures associated with independently identified clusters and posterior uncertainty estimates, rather than a single deterministic map.
- **Claimed result:** The authors reconstruct matter across 20 lens planes in a 0.54 deg$^2$ field, extending the redshift reach of earlier Hubble-based 3D reconstructions to roughly $z=2$ and separating some structures that overlap in projection.
- **Evidence type:** observational; JWST COSMOS-Web imaging analyzed with weak-lensing inference and a diffusion prior trained on cosmological simulations.
- **Caveat:** This is an inference over one 0.54 deg$^2$ field, not a direct dark-matter observation; its posterior depends on the forward model and simulation-trained prior.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, Wednesday, 4 November 2026
- **Themes:** Cosmology & gravitation; Astrophysics; Computational & data methods

### 6. [Thermally enhanced magnon-phonon resonance in van der Waals antiferromagnet FePS$_3$](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.04229)
- **Abstract:** High-field Raman spectroscopy finds robust magnon-phonon coupling in FePS$_3$ as temperature rises, with decreasing detuning driving enhanced hybridization near the antiferromagnetic transition.
- **Authors:** Kai-Xuan Zhang, Beom Hyun Kim, Seungbok Lee, Hao Li, Eugene Park et al.
- **arXiv:** `2611.04229v1` (published 2026-11-03 03:09:13 UTC; categories: `cond-mat.mtrl-sci`, `physics.optics`, `quant-ph`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** A measured avoided crossing across temperature, plus cross-listing in materials, optics, and quantum physics, ties a concrete spectroscopy result to a current question about driven magnetic states.
- **Claimed result:** The authors observe that magnon-phonon coupling remains robust with warming while the zero-field detuning decreases, enhancing hybridization near the transition temperature.
- **Evidence type:** experimental; high-field Raman spectroscopy of the van der Waals antiferromagnet FePS$_3$.
- **Caveat:** The proposed connection to an earlier terahertz-driven metastable state is an interpretation of the coupling data, not a direct demonstration that this mechanism alone explains that state.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, Wednesday, 4 November 2026
- **Themes:** Condensed matter & materials; Atomic, molecular & optical physics

### 7. [Quantum Simulation of Electronic Structure using Quantum Density Functional Theory on a Programmable Integrated Photonic Circuit](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.04694)
- **Abstract:** The authors experimentally implement a qubit-efficient quantum-density-functional approach for the ground and first excited states of LiH on a programmable integrated photonic circuit.
- **Authors:** Giovanni Adriano, Akilan Rajamani, Francesco Hoch, Farrokh Labib, Nadja Z. Zaouri et al.
- **arXiv:** `2611.04694v1` (published 2026-11-03 11:18:34 UTC; categories: `quant-ph`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** Experimental photonic-hardware validation of a quantum-chemistry method attracted independent coverage from [Quantum Zeitgeist](https://quantumzeitgeist.com/fotonica-nanotecnologie-photonic-qubits-lithium-hydride/); the paper also reports both ground- and first-excited-state properties.
- **Claimed result:** The authors implement the ensemble extension of VQE for LiH on a quantum-dot single-photon source and integrated circuit, demonstrating their Q-DFT mapping with logarithmic qubit scaling in the number of orbitals.
- **Evidence type:** experimental; LiH calculation on a programmable integrated photonic circuit, compared with noiseless and noisy simulations.
- **Caveat:** This is a small-molecule proof of implementation, not a demonstration of quantum advantage; finite photon counts and about 89% photon indistinguishability limit accuracy, and the authors note that near-perfect indistinguishability is needed for chemical accuracy.

### 8. [SS 433 Eastern Jet Turbulence Revealed by Spatially-Resolved IXPE Data Analysis](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03846)
- **Abstract:** A spatially resolved reanalysis of 2023 IXPE polarimetry finds that a three-region model better describes the eastern SS 433 jet than a single-zone model, with lower polarization in the sheath.
- **Authors:** Ningyue Fan, Jack T. Dinsmore, Roger W. Romani
- **arXiv:** `2611.03846v1` (published 2026-11-02 19:15:40 UTC; categories: `astro-ph.HE`)

- **Claimed result:** The authors find lower polarization in the jet sheath than in its head and spine, with position angles consistent across regions, and infer enhanced magnetic turbulence at the boundary.
- **Evidence type:** observational; spatially resolved analysis of the 2023 Imaging X-ray Polarimetry Explorer (IXPE) observation of SS 433/W50.
- **Caveat:** The Kelvin–Helmholtz explanation for the inferred boundary turbulence is tentative; this is a reanalysis of one observation rather than an independent observation.

- **Themes:** Astrophysics; Instrumentation & detectors

## Trending Research Themes

- **Precision measurements and parameter bounds:** BaBar measures a key two-pion HVP input to muon $g-2$; the EuXFEL study improves the low-energy light-by-light-scattering bound; FASER closes part of an X17 parameter window; and RaF spectroscopy sharpens an unstable-nucleus quadrupole moment. These are distinct experiments and constraints, not independent confirmations of one result ([2611.04621](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.04621), [2611.04531](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.04531), [2611.03904](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03904), [2611.04696](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.04696)).
- **Inference from complex data:** The JWST weak-lensing reconstruction uses a simulation-trained prior to separate line-of-sight structure; the lunar study fits fracture-scattering models to Apollo waveforms; the IXPE analysis resolves polarization across jet regions. The common thread is model-assisted extraction of spatial structure from indirect signals, but the data and physical systems are unrelated ([2611.03786](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03786), [2611.04209](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.04209), [2611.03846](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03846)).
- **Engineered light–matter and wave transport:** FePS$_3$ spectroscopy probes temperature-tuned magnon–phonon hybridization; the Q-DFT result uses programmable photonic hardware for molecular states; and the gyromagnetic-crystal experiment routes chiral photonic channels. These are separate platforms, with a shared emphasis on controlling coupled excitations or wave propagation ([2611.04229](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.04229), [2611.04694](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.04694), [2611.04827](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.04827)).

## Open Problems and Research Directions

- **Open problem (authors):** The EuXFEL team did not reach the expected QED signal sensitivity, and FASER leaves a high-coupling X17 window. **Direction (synthesis):** improve laser/beam geometry and background rejection for a direct light-by-light signal, while testing the remaining X17 parameter space with additional exposure or complementary searches ([2611.04531](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.04531), [2611.03904](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03904)).
- **Open problem (authors):** The JWST map remains a posterior inferred through a learned simulation prior, and the lunar fracture interpretation is not direct imaging. **Direction (synthesis):** test the map across independent fields and alternative priors, and combine future lunar seismic observations with radar, gravity, and sample constraints to test fracture depth and geometry ([2611.03786](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03786), [2611.04209](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.04209)).
- **Open problem (authors):** The Q-DFT photonic demonstration is limited by photon distinguishability and counts, and does not establish a computational advantage. **Direction (synthesis):** improve indistinguishability and photon statistics, then test larger orbital spaces and compare total resources with classical baselines ([2611.04694](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.04694)).
- **Open problem (synthesis):** The thermal magnon–phonon result is proposed as an explanation for an earlier driven state, while the IXPE boundary-turbulence interpretation remains tentative. **Direction (synthesis):** perform temperature-resolved terahertz/Raman tests around the FePS$_3$ transition and obtain additional spatially resolved IXPE epochs to test the proposed mechanisms ([2611.04229](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.04229), [2611.03846](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03846)).

## Takeaway

The most concrete signals in this batch are precision measurements and sharper constraints, not a single emerging discovery: BaBar's two-pion HVP input, the EuXFEL bound, FASER's X17 exclusion, and the Ra quadrupole result. JWST, IXPE, and lunar-seismic work show how computational inference is extending what indirect observations can resolve, but those interpretations remain dependent on models, data coverage, and follow-up tests.
