# Giant Watch Summary - Daily - 2026-11-09

## Headline

DeepSeek's Harness agent platform reported 1.54 million daily active users less than three months after launch, the clearest sign yet that the competitive front has shifted from model benchmarks to agent infrastructure and ecosystem lock-in.

## Top Developments

### 1. DeepSeek says Harness agent platform reached 1.54M daily active users (DeepSeek)
DeepSeek disclosed that its open-source agent framework Harness hit 1.54 million daily active users as of November 4, up from roughly 271,000 at the end of September when the desktop app launched. The company said API users were consuming about 92 trillion tokens per day, that third-party plug-ins had grown to about 35,000 with roughly 60% of active users having built or installed at least one, and that it plans an official plug-in marketplace plus a developer support program with token grants.
Why it matters: The first hard usage numbers showing agent harnesses, not chat, driving token consumption at scale — and a direct positioning of Harness as an 'operating system for the agent era' against OpenAI's Codex and Anthropic's Claude Code.

### 2. Google Cloud's Gemini agent routes work across Gemini and Anthropic Claude models (Google, Anthropic)
Google Cloud introduced a Gemini agent for work that operates inside Gmail, Docs, Sheets and Calendar as well as Microsoft 365 and Slack, keeps a single set of memories in the cloud, and can carry a task across hours or days. It routes each job across Google's Gemini models and Anthropic's Claude models via a Smart Routing capability, and can create 'coworker agents' with their own Workspace account, email address and directory listing. Google said close to ninety percent of Fortune 100 companies are already Gemini Enterprise customers.
Why it matters: A notable competitive tell: Google's flagship enterprise agent ships with a routing layer that can hand work to a rival's models, framing model choice as a cost/quality dial rather than a vendor lock-in.
Refs: https://www.responsibleaifoundation.com/post/google-cloud-unveils-gemini-agent-for-enterprise-work

### 3. Meta ships Muse Spaces, custom trackers and planners built from a description (Meta)
Meta's newest Muse feature, Spaces, shipped on November 6, letting the personal agent build custom trackers and planners from a description. The same timeline records Muse's US launch on September 8, 2026, expansion to Canada, Japan and Singapore, and Zuckerberg's Q3 call statement that more than 10 million people in the US and Canada had set up Muse in about six weeks, with about 25% using it on any given day.
Why it matters: The most concrete recent product increment for Meta's consumer agent, and the usage figures give a rare public read on retention for a mass-market personal agent.
Refs: https://www.taskade.com/blog/history-of-meta-muse

### 4. Gemini 4 Carbon rolls out to cyber defenders first at Argon's introductory price (Google)
Google DeepMind introduced Gemini 4 Carbon on October 28, 2026, describing it as its strongest model with state-of-the-art cybersecurity and long-horizon capabilities. Access starts with a subset of cyber defenders and expands to more trusted partners through Google's Fairwind Program, with no general availability date given. Google reports 88.2% on SEC-bench Pro for finding vulnerabilities, 54.8% on ExploitGym and 71.0% on CWE-bench for patching, and says Carbon launches at the same introductory price as Argon — $2 per million input and $10 per million output tokens, rising to $4 and $20 after the introductory period.
Why it matters: A frontier model deliberately gated on dual-use risk, and the pricing carry-over makes the Argon introductory window the operative cost deadline for developers.
Refs: https://labforty.com/en/insight/gemini-4-carbon-cybersecurity-frontier-model, https://shattered.io/gemini-4-launch-earlier-claim-meta-muse-argon-carbon-zero-new-dates-2026/

### 5. China Mobile and Z.ai sign agreement to put GLM models on Mobile Cloud MaaS (GLM)
China Mobile and Z.ai officially signed a cooperation agreement covering domestic computing power, large model services and AI commercialization. The Z.ai GLM series models will be integrated into the Mobile Cloud MaaS platform, with the cooperation spanning the chain from underlying compute supply to model services and commercial distribution.
Why it matters: A state-carrier distribution channel for GLM, tying a model family to domestic compute and a national telecom's cloud — the kind of arrangement that shapes enterprise reach in China more than benchmark scores do.
Refs: https://www.chaincatcher.com/en/article/2302839

### 6. Alibaba runs Qwen3.8-Max and Flash through Double 11 Token Plan and night discounts (Qwen)
Alibaba Cloud's Double 11 promotion, running to November 30, layers discounts on its Qwen3.8-Max flagship MoE model and Qwen3.8-Flash: an AI savings plan covering 150-plus Bailian models with quarterly tiers down to 4.5-fold discounts, Token Plan subscription credits with new-customer half-price tiers, and a Night Plan giving 40% credit consumption between 22:00 and 08:00 Beijing time for those two models only. The two billing channels — pay-as-you-go and Token Plan — are explicitly non-interchangeable, and the old qwen3.8-max-preview model ID has been retired with traffic auto-routed to the new version.
Why it matters: Pricing mechanics as competitive strategy: Alibaba is using a retail festival to convert developers onto subscription credits and off-peak batch scheduling, with rules complex enough that misreading them is the main cost risk.
Refs: https://developer.aliyun.com/article/1781074, https://developer.aliyun.com/article/1781071

### 7. xAI wins temporary order blocking Minnesota's AI nudification ban (xAI)
xAI fought Minnesota's ban on AI tools that can generate realistic nude images of identifiable people, including minors, and won a temporary court order blocking enforcement. xAI argued the ban conflicts with the First Amendment; the state argued Grok Imagine's nudification function is a product feature and not protected speech. The one-sentence order gave no reasoning and left the statute's constitutionality for later review. The report also notes xAI sued one of its own users, Terry Harwood, 13 days before suing Minnesota, and that a class action by three Tennessee teenagers alleges Grok was used to create non-consensual sexual images of them as minors.
Why it matters: A live constitutional test of whether model output features are speech, with the court's silence on reasoning leaving the underlying question open — and a company simultaneously suing users and suing regulators.
Refs: https://fr.jpost.com/opinion/article-914304

### 8. Coforge joins SpaceX Super Intelligence Partner Network to deploy Grok in enterprises (xAI)
Indian IT services firm Coforge became a Transformation Partner in the SpaceX Super Intelligence Partner Network, deploying the Grok suite — Grok for chat, research and analysis, Grok Build for software development, Grok Models, Grok Bot, Grok Imagine and Grok Voice — into enterprise production environments. Coforge will staff Forward Deployed Engineering teams through its Momentuum Blue unit alongside AI agents, with responsibility for predefined business outcomes.
Why it matters: A services-led route to enterprise revenue for Grok, mirroring how systems integrators carried earlier cloud and ERP platforms into large accounts.
Refs: https://www.techmonitor.ai/news/coforge-joins-spacex-network-to-deploy-grok-ai-tools-for-enterprises

### 9. Anthropic documents Claude Platform on AWS as Anthropic-operated, AWS-billed (Anthropic)
Anthropic's AWS documentation describes Claude Platform on AWS as the full Anthropic platform — Messages API, Agent Skills, code execution, beta features — running on Anthropic-managed infrastructure, distinct from Amazon Bedrock where AWS operates the inference stack. AWS supplies authentication (SigV4 or API key), IAM access control and Marketplace billing via Claude Consumption Units at a fixed $0.01 per CCU, with hourly metering and postpaid arrears-only payment. The docs warn data may not reside in AWS and inference may route to Anthropic's primary cloud unless inference_geo is pinned.
Why it matters: A deliberate split between 'AWS runs the stack' (Bedrock) and 'AWS is the storefront' (Claude Platform), with data-residency caveats that procurement teams will need to read closely.
Refs: https://docs.aws.amazon.com/claude-platform/latest/userguide/welcome.md, https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/about-claude/pricing.md

### 10. Anthropic makes Claude Sonnet 5's introductory $2/$10 pricing permanent (Anthropic)
Anthropic's pricing page states that the $2 per million input and $10 per million output token pricing for Claude Sonnet 5, announced at launch as introductory through August 31, 2026, is now the standard price, and that the previously scheduled increase to $3/$15 on September 1, 2026 will not occur. The same page lists Claude Opus 5.5 at $4/$20 and Claude Haiku 5.5 at $0.10/$0.50 for prompts up to 100,000 tokens, rising to $0.50/$2.50 above that threshold.
Why it matters: A cancelled price increase is a competitive signal in a week when Google is running a dated introductory window and Alibaba is discounting — the mid-tier price floor is being defended rather than raised.
Refs: https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/about-claude/pricing.md

## Cross-Org Themes

- Agent infrastructure, not raw model quality, is the stated competitive battleground — DeepSeek's Harness, Google's Gemini agent and Meta's Muse all sell task completion
- Introductory and promotional pricing windows with hard expiry dates (Google's Argon window, Alibaba's Double 11, Anthropic's cancelled increase) are shaping developer cost planning

## Takeaway

The evidence to watch next is retention and paid conversion behind these agent numbers — DeepSeek's plug-in marketplace uptake, Meta's daily-use share, and whether Google's Gemini agent routing to Claude shows up in enterprise renewals — rather than further headline user counts.
