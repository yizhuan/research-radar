### 1. [Localization near the edge for the lattice Anderson-Bernoulli model on general dimension](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30209)
- **Abstract:** The authors prove Anderson localization near the bottom of the spectrum for the lattice Anderson model with Bernoulli potential in every dimension d ≥ 2, completing a problem left open by Bourgain and Kenig.
- **Authors:** Linjun Li, Shihe Liu, Lingfu Zhang
- **arXiv:** `2609.30209v1` (published 2026-09-24 17:42 UTC; categories: `math-ph`, `math.AP`, `math.DS`, `math.PR`, `math.SP`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** The arXiv abstract and HTML paper were independently surfaced prominently in web search; the result explicitly completes a longstanding Bourgain–Kenig problem; it is cross-listed across five mathematical/physical areas. Semantic Scholar lookup was unavailable for this record at snapshot time, so citation counts are unavailable.
- **Claimed result:** For Bernoulli random Schrödinger operators on Z^d, the paper establishes spectral and strong dynamical localization near the spectral bottom, with pure point spectrum and exponentially decaying eigenfunctions; the stated theorem covers all d ≥ 2.
- **Assumptions and setting:** The potential is i.i.d. Bernoulli (with extensions discussed for nondegenerate compactly supported laws); the main operator is the discrete Anderson model on ℓ²(Z^d), and the theorem gives an energy interval [0,E*] depending on dimension and disorder.
- **Caveat:** This is an attributed preprint claim; the detailed dynamical bounds have dimension-dependent exponents and the theorem is a near-edge result, not a full-spectrum localization theorem.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, 2026-09-24 announcement batch
- **Themes:** Analysis & PDE; Probability & statistics; Mathematical physics

### 2. [Global well-posedness and scattering for the three-dimensional focusing energy-critical NLS](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30228)
- **Abstract:** The authors prove global well-posedness and scattering for the three-dimensional focusing energy-critical nonlinear Schrödinger equation below both the ground-state energy and gradient thresholds.
- **Authors:** Qingtang Su, Zehua Zhao
- **arXiv:** `2609.30228v1` (published 2026-09-24 17:51 UTC; categories: `math.AP`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** The result was independently returned by web search with the abstract highlighted; it claims confirmation of the dimension-three threshold conjecture; Semantic Scholar reported 0 citations and 0 influential citations at the snapshot, as expected for a same-week submission.
- **Claimed result:** Every initial datum in homogeneous H¹(R³) with energy below the ground-state energy and gradient norm below the ground-state gradient has a unique global solution that scatters in both time directions.
- **Assumptions and setting:** The equation is the focusing energy-critical cubic NLS on R³; both sharp threshold inequalities are required, and no finite-mass assumption is imposed.
- **Caveat:** The statement is a preprint theorem and depends on a delicate frequency-localized interaction Morawetz argument; the thresholds are sharp, so the result does not cover data at or above them.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, 2026-09-24 announcement batch
- **Themes:** Analysis & PDE

### 3. [Bounded-orbit wandering domains do not exist](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30103)
- **Abstract:** The paper proves that transcendental entire functions have no wandering Fatou components with bounded forward orbit, and proves the stronger analogous statement for transcendental meromorphic functions.
- **Authors:** Kostiantyn Drach, Leticia Pardo-Simón, Beno Učakar
- **arXiv:** `2609.30103v1` (published 2026-09-24 16:46 UTC; categories: `math.DS`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** The paper was independently surfaced in web search with the result described as settling a longstanding question; the HTML version exposes explicit main theorems and historical context. Citation counts were unavailable from Semantic Scholar at snapshot time.
- **Claimed result:** A wandering Fatou component of a transcendental entire function has unbounded forward union; for every wandering domain of a transcendental meromorphic function, every point has an unbounded orbit along a subsequence.
- **Assumptions and setting:** The maps are transcendental entire or meromorphic functions, and the domains are Fatou components whose iterates are pairwise distinct.
- **Caveat:** The result concerns complex one-dimensional holomorphic dynamics and does not extend to general quasiregular maps; it is an attributed preprint claim.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, 2026-09-24 announcement batch
- **Themes:** Geometry & topology; Analysis & PDE

### 4. [Sharp Lovász-Theta Bounds on Random Graphs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30064)
- **Abstract:** The authors prove that the Lovász theta function of G(n,1/2) is (1+o(1))√n with high probability, closing the previous factor-of-two asymptotic gap.
- **Authors:** Aaron Potechin, Jeff Xu
- **arXiv:** `2609.30064v1` (published 2026-09-24 16:23 UTC; categories: `cs.CC`, `cs.DM`, `math.CO`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** The exact title and abstract were independently prominent in web search; the paper resolves a stated longstanding challenge and bridges combinatorics, semidefinite optimization, and theoretical computer science. Citation counts were unavailable from Semantic Scholar at snapshot time.
- **Claimed result:** For an Erdős–Rényi random graph with edge probability 1/2, the Lovász theta number is asymptotically √n with high probability.
- **Assumptions and setting:** The graph is G(n,1/2); the conclusion is asymptotic and high-probability, not a deterministic bound for every graph.
- **Caveat:** The result is specific to the stated random-graph model; implications for algorithms or other graph distributions require separate arguments.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, 2026-09-24 announcement batch
- **Themes:** Combinatorics & discrete mathematics; Optimization & control

### 5. [Thresholds and spread in set systems of bounded VC-dimension](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30263)
- **Abstract:** For set systems of VC-dimension at most d, the paper proves a logarithmic comparison between threshold and expectation threshold, verifies Talagrand's integral–fractional conjecture in fixed VC-dimension, and improves robust sunflower bounds.
- **Authors:** Chong Shangguan
- **arXiv:** `2609.30263v1` (published 2026-09-24 17:59 UTC; categories: `math.CO`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** The abstract states a conjecture verification and an optimal-up-to-constants logarithmic factor; it gives a concrete improvement over a recent sunflower bound. No independent attention or citation counts were available at snapshot time.
- **Claimed result:** The threshold satisfies p_c(F) ≤ C q(F) log(d+1), with corresponding random-containment and spread results; the paper derives improved robust and ordinary sunflower bounds for bounded VC-dimension.
- **Assumptions and setting:** F is a family of nonempty subsets of a finite set with VC-dimension at most d; constants are absolute and ε-dependent refinements are stated in the paper.
- **Caveat:** The logarithmic factor is claimed optimal only up to absolute constants, and the result does not remove the dependence on VC-dimension.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, 2026-09-24 announcement batch
- **Themes:** Combinatorics & discrete mathematics

### 6. [A 1/64 spectral gap for surfaces with δ=1/2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30261)
- **Abstract:** For a range of convex co-compact hyperbolic surfaces, the authors prove an explicit essential spectral gap and obtain a 1/64−ε gap at δ=1/2 using a new fractal uncertainty principle.
- **Authors:** Alex Cohen, Semyon Dyatlov
- **arXiv:** `2609.30261v1` (published 2026-09-24 17:59 UTC; categories: `math.AP`, `math.SP`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** The explicit constant, cross-area method, and close connection to a new two-ends Furstenberg theorem make this a high-significance recent result; Semantic Scholar reported 0 citations and 0 influential citations at the snapshot.
- **Claimed result:** For δ in (2/5,14/27), the essential spectral gap has size β = 7/8(1/2−δ)+δ/32−ε; at δ=1/2 this gives β=1/64−ε.
- **Assumptions and setting:** The surfaces are convex co-compact hyperbolic surfaces and δ is the exponent of convergence of the Poincaré series.
- **Caveat:** The gap is stated with an arbitrary ε>0 and in a restricted δ-range; broader sharpness questions remain open.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, 2026-09-24 announcement batch
- **Themes:** Analysis & PDE; Geometry & topology

- **Announcement type:** new submission, 2026-09-24 announcement batch

## Trending Research Themes

- **Threshold and resolution theorems:** The Anderson–Bernoulli paper, focusing NLS paper, and wandering-domain paper each claim to close or settle a long-running question under precise hypotheses. These are isolated breakthroughs rather than evidence of a field-wide trend.
- **Random structures with sharp asymptotics:** The Lovász-theta, VC-dimension/sunflower, and uniform-attachment-tree papers all convert probabilistic structure into quantitative bounds, with emphasis on sharp exponents or optimal logarithmic losses.
- **Cross-field bridges:** The spectral-gap paper combines fractal uncertainty with hyperbolic dynamics; the Schubert-variety paper links algebraic geometry, topology, and quasisymmetric Hopf algebras; the Anderson paper combines probability, spectral theory, and mathematical physics.
- **Explicit constants and algorithmic consequences:** Several papers emphasize usable constants or computational consequences rather than only qualitative existence, including the 1/64 spectral gap, the Lovász-theta asymptotic, and the VC-dimension threshold comparison.

## Open Problems and Research Directions

- **Anderson localization beyond the edge:** The selected Anderson paper proves localization near the bottom of the spectrum. Extending the method toward mobility-edge questions or sharper localization intervals is a natural direction tied directly to its restricted energy window.
- **Sharpness of the common-subtree exponent:** The uniform-attachment-tree paper leaves the magnitude of the largest common subtree open. Determining whether the n^0.83 lower bound is close to optimal, or improving the matching upper bound, is explicitly identified by the authors as an open question.
- **Beyond the 1/64 spectral-gap regime:** The spectral-gap result leaves the dependence on δ and ε visible. It would be useful to test whether the new fractal-uncertainty mechanism yields optimal gaps outside the stated interval or at the endpoint.
- **The Schubert/H-group conjecture:** The quasisymmetric Schubert paper conjectures an isomorphism between its direct-limit H-group and a Baker–Richter construction. Establishing or refuting that identification is the paper's clearest structural follow-up.
- **Other random graph models:** The Lovász-theta result is proved for G(n,1/2). Extending the sharp √n asymptotic to sparse, dense non-half-density, random regular, or mildly dependent models would test the robustness of the phenomenon; this is a proposed direction, not an author-stated theorem.
