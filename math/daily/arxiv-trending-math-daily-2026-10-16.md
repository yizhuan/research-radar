### 1. [A quantitative form of the planar Falconer distance theorem](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22222)
- **Abstract:** The authors give a quantitative planar multiscale argument showing that compact planar sets with Hausdorff dimension above one have distance sets of positive Lebesgue measure.[2]
- **Authors:** Guozheng Cheng, Xiang Fang, Menghan Li, Hongdou Qu, Chengbo Xiao.[2]
- **arXiv:** `2610.22222v1` (published 2026-10-15 17:03:24 UTC; categories: `math.CA`).[2]
- **Evidence for ranking:** The claimed threshold matches the planar Falconer positive-measure conjecture, and the paper gives a uniform quantitative bound for normalized Frostman measures; this is a mathematical-significance signal, not evidence of measured readership.[2]
- **Claimed result:** For every 0 < η < 1, a compact subset of the unit ball supporting a probability measure with ν(B(x,r)) ≤ 2r^(1+η) has a distance set of length at least b(η)>0. Localization yields |ΔE| ≥ c_(ε,p) D A^(−p) for every fixed p>1/ε under the stated Frostman bound.[2]
- **Assumptions and setting:** The quantitative theorem is planar, with a probability measure supported in the closed unit ball and the displayed Frostman growth condition; the corollary assumes compact E of diameter D>0 and a measure satisfying the normalized bound with A≥1.[2]
- **Caveat:** The authors explicitly do not establish endpoint p=1/ε, optimality, an asymptotic for b(η), or a pinned-distance conclusion; the cited manuscript is a preprint and its proof has not been independently assessed here.[2]
- **Announcement type:** new submission, 16 October 2026 announcement batch.[1][2]
- **Themes:** Analysis & PDE; Geometry & topology.[2]

### 2. [The Hopf conjecture with T^3-symmetry](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22173)
- **Abstract:** For closed even-dimensional positively curved manifolds admitting an isometric 3-torus action, the authors prove positive Euler characteristic and extend the result to orbifolds.[3]
- **Authors:** Jan Nienhaus, Dennis Wulle.[3]
- **arXiv:** `2610.22173v1` (published 2026-10-15 16:32:27 UTC; categories: `math.DG`).[3]
- **Evidence for ranking:** The paper proves a symmetry-restricted case of the classical Hopf conjecture and provides a rational-cohomology classification of fixed-point components; the scope includes orbifolds as well as manifolds.[3]
- **Claimed result:** Under an effective isometric T^3 action, every closed even-dimensional positively curved manifold has χ(M)>0. The authors further classify fixed-point-component rational cohomology rings among sphere, complex/quaternionic projective-space, and S²×HP-type models, and state an orbifold analogue.[3]
- **Assumptions and setting:** Closed, even-dimensional, positively sectionally curved spaces with an effective isometric T^3 action; the classification theorem is stated for oriented manifolds and orbifolds.[3]
- **Caveat:** This is a result under a strong symmetry hypothesis, not a proof of the unrestricted Hopf conjecture; the authors leave open whether one listed product-type cohomology case can occur for a 3-torus action.[3]
- **Announcement type:** new submission, 16 October 2026 announcement batch.[1][3]
- **Themes:** Geometry & topology.[3]

### 3. [A counterexample to the generalized Viterbo conjecture on the spectral distance in a disc cotangent bundle](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22175)
- **Abstract:** The authors prove unboundedness of a Lagrangian spectral distance for manifolds satisfying stated topological assumptions, disproving a generalization of Viterbo's boundedness conjecture, and classify the surface cases over Z₂.[4]
- **Authors:** Filip Broćić, Baptiste Serraille, Ibrahim Trifa.[4]
- **arXiv:** `2610.22175v1` (published 2026-10-15 16:32:49 UTC; categories: `math.SG`).[4]
- **Evidence for ranking:** It claims a counterexample to a broad conjecture and also gives a coefficient-specific classification for closed surfaces, with a generating-function computation and a Floer-theoretic covering-space argument described in the abstract.[4]
- **Claimed result:** For a closed connected R-orientable Riemannian manifold with no conjugate points, H¹(M; ℝ)≠0, and Euler characteristic nonzero in R, the spectral distance on the Hamiltonian orbit of the zero section in the unit disc cotangent bundle is unbounded. Over Z₂, the conjecture holds for surfaces only in the listed sphere, projective-plane, torus, and Klein-bottle cases.[4]
- **Assumptions and setting:** The unboundedness theorem requires all three hypotheses stated above and concerns Lagrangians Hamiltonian-isotopic to the zero section in a disc cotangent bundle; the surface classification is specifically over Z₂.[4]
- **Caveat:** The counterexample is conditional on those geometric and topological hypotheses; it does not settle the conjecture for all closed manifolds or coefficient rings.[4]
- **Announcement type:** new submission, 16 October 2026 announcement batch.[1][4]
- **Themes:** Geometry & topology.[4]

### 4. [The rank problem for metric spaces](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22351)

- **Authors:** Asger Tornquist.[5]
- **arXiv:** `2610.22351v1` (published 2026-10-15 17:57:39 UTC; categories: `math.LO`, `math.MG`).[5]
- **Evidence for ranking:** The abstract explicitly identifies an answer to a previously posed rank question and sharpness of a known upper bound; it is cross-listed in logic and metric geometry.[5]
- **Claimed result:** A complete, second-countable metric space is constructed with uncountable Scott rank; the paper also introduces a Baire-category variant and uses it to rederive known rank bounds by standard descriptive-set-theoretic methods.[5]
- **Assumptions and setting:** The construction is in complete second-countable metric spaces and concerns Scott rank and descriptive set theory.[5]
- **Caveat:** This is a result in a specialized model-theoretic/descriptive-set-theoretic setting; the arXiv abstract page provides no HTML full text, so the detailed construction and proof were not inspected here.[5]
- **Announcement type:** new submission, 16 October 2026 announcement batch.[1][5]

### 5. [Counterexamples to the ordinary Fried conjecture near hyperbolic metrics](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22281)
- **Abstract:** The authors give an AI-assisted construction of real-analytic negatively curved metrics arbitrarily close to a hyperbolic metric in dimension three, for which a torsion identity for the Ruelle zeta function fails along acyclic unitary characters tending to the trivial one.[6]
- **Authors:** Simon Becker, Benjamin Lawton.[6]
- **arXiv:** `2610.22281v1` (published 2026-10-15 17:40:30 UTC; categories: `math.DS`, `math.DG`, `math.SP`).[6]
- **Evidence for ranking:** The claimed result refutes the ordinary conjectured identity in a perturbative regime and connects dynamical systems, differential geometry, and spectral theory; the paper also discloses substantial AI involvement.[6]
- **Claimed result:** For a sequence of real-analytic negatively curved metrics approaching a closed hyperbolic 3-manifold's metric and acyclic unitary line characters approaching the trivial character, the ratio of the modulus of the ordinary Ruelle zeta value at zero to analytic torsion squared tends to zero.[6]
- **Assumptions and setting:** Closed oriented 3-manifolds, real-analytic negatively curved metrics, and acyclic unitary line characters; the claimed counterexamples occur arbitrarily near a hyperbolic metric.[6]
- **Caveat:** The authors do not determine whether the Ruelle zeta value itself vanishes at the selected metrics; they disclose that geometric and spectral inputs are not formalized, so independent expert review remains particularly important.[6]
- **Announcement type:** new submission, 16 October 2026 announcement batch.[1][6]
- **Themes:** Geometry & topology; Mathematical physics.[6]

## Open Problems and Research Directions

- **Author-stated — Falconer bounds:** The paper leaves open the endpoint power p=1/ε, the optimality and small-η behavior of its lower bound, and pinned-distance conclusions. A natural follow-up is to test whether the multiscale reconstruction can yield endpoint or pinned estimates without strengthening Frostman hypotheses.[2]
- **Author-stated — Hopf under T^3 actions:** The authors ask whether the S²×HP-type cohomology case can occur for a 3-torus action; they also note other orbifold Euler characteristics as a further question. Investigating examples or obstructions in these cases would probe the theorem's boundary.[3]
- **Author-stated — Viterbo coefficients:** The paper asks whether boundedness depends on the coefficient ring. One direction is to compare the spectral-distance behavior under different rings while relaxing one of the no-conjugate-points, H¹, or Euler-characteristic hypotheses.[4]
- **Author-stated — Fried identity:** The authors leave unresolved whether the ordinary Ruelle zeta value is zero at their selected metrics, even though the torsion-normalized ratio tends to zero. Resolving the resonance multiplicity and independently auditing the geometric/spectral construction are immediate follow-ups.[6]
