### 5. [Random independent sets in uncrowded hypergraphs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.08707)
- **Abstract:** For fixed uniformity and large degeneracy, the authors obtain an asymptotically sharp fractional-chromatic-number bound for hypergraphs of girth at least five, and derive related coloring and independence-number results.
- **Authors:** Abhishek Dhawan, Lina Li, Abhishek Methuku, Minh-Quan Vo, Kewen Yuan
- **arXiv:** `2610.08707v1` (published 2026-10-06 17:17:25 UTC; announcement batch 2026-10-07; categories: `math.CO`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** The paper supplies matching asymptotic upper and lower bounds, extends a recent graph result to hypergraphs, and reports verification of a conjecture on local-demand fractional colorings. Semantic Scholar showed 0 citations at the snapshot.
- **Claimed result:** For fixed $k\geq2$ and sufficiently large $d$, the maximum fractional chromatic number of a $k$-uniform, $d$-degenerate, girth-at-least-$g$ hypergraph (any fixed $g\geq5$) is $(1+o_d(1))\big((k-1)d/\log d\big)^{1/(k-1)}$.
- **Assumptions and setting:** Fixed uniformity $k$ and girth threshold $g\geq5$, with degeneracy $d$ tending large; the main assertion is asymptotic in $d$.
- **Caveat:** The main formula is asymptotic, not a sharp finite-$d$ estimate, and does not cover hypergraphs with short cycles below the stated girth threshold.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, 2026-10-07 announcement batch
- **Themes:** Combinatorics & discrete mathematics

## Open Problems and Research Directions

- **Author-reported gap:** The percolation introduction calls the paper the remaining nearest-neighbor $d\geq3$ Ornstein–Zernike asymptotics for finite supercritical connections. A natural extension to test is whether analogous sharp asymptotics hold for other interaction ranges or in dimension two; this is a proposed direction, not a claim made or solved here. [Aoun–Khettabi](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.08682)
- **Author-reported scope boundary:** The polynomial Littlewood–Offord preprint says it resolves the stated Nguyen–Vu conjecture under a disjoint-monomial matching condition. One follow-up is to determine whether comparable bounds hold under weaker structural hypotheses, and to subject the AI-origin proof to independent expert verification. [Grebennikov](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.08708)
- **Proposed extension:** The hypergraph estimate is asymptotic in degeneracy and assumes girth at least five. Finite-$d$ error terms and analogues allowing short cycles would test how much of the bound survives beyond the present setting. [Dhawan et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.08707)
- **Proposed boundary-regime test:** The positive-mass theorem assumes $p>n$ and decay exponent $\tau>(n-2)/2$. Establishing whether comparable conclusions persist at endpoint regularity/decay, or under weaker hypotheses, would extend the stated low-regularity framework; the abstract does not claim those endpoints. [Graf–Hafemann](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.08615)
- **Proposed geometric generalization:** The cube paper gives a complete three-dimensional classification and quantitative stability. Testing analogous classifications or stability inequalities for other polyhedral domains would extend the domain-specific result; tractability is not asserted. [Deng, Li & Liu](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.08644)
