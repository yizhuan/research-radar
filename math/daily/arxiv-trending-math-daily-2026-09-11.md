- **arXiv:** `2609.11888v1` (published 2026-09-10 17:50 UTC; categories: `math.NT`, `math.AG`)

- **Assumptions and setting:** Waring’s problem over the polynomial ring F_q[T], degree d, number of variables n > 2d, characteristic > (d−1)^2, and an explicit lower bound on q.
- **Caveat:** The theorem’s range depends materially on characteristic and q; “sharp” is qualified by the author as a uniform-in-target statement and should not be generalized beyond that setting.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, 2026-09-11 announcement batch

- **Assumptions and setting:** Closed connected Riemannian manifold, smooth strictly positive initial data; the particle result is stated on the circle with iid initialization.
