### 1. [On exponential sums with multiplicative coefficients](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22345)
- **Abstract:** For multiplicative coefficients and an additive phase close to a rational with denominator in the stated range, the paper proves optimal leading constants in exponential-sum bounds; complete multiplicativity improves the constant, and related conjectures receive counterexamples.
- **Authors:** Haozhe Gou
- **arXiv:** `2610.22345v1` (published 2026-10-15T17:56:45Z; categories: `math.NT`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** The introduction states a sharp theorem with optimal constants that confirms a longstanding folklore conjecture; the paper also provides explicit counterexamples to stronger variants. The introduction notes an independent proof of the underlying folklore bound by Robles, while distinguishing this paper's sharp constants.
- **Claimed result:** Theorem 1.1 proves a uniform bound with leading constant 4/√6 for 1-bounded multiplicative functions and 1 for completely multiplicative functions, both optimal, under the stated rational-approximation and denominator hypotheses (arXiv abstract and HTML introduction).
- **Assumptions and setting:** `|f(n)| ≤ 1`; `α` is within `1/(qx)` of reduced `a/q`; `q ≤ x/((log x)^2(log log x)^3)`. The coefficient is completely multiplicative for the improved constant 1.
- **Caveat:** The sharp theorem is range-dependent; it does not establish the same constants for arbitrary denominators or all analogous sums over smooth numbers. The paper itself gives counterexamples at stronger ranges/endpoints.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, 16 October 2026 batch
- **Themes:** Number theory

### 2. [A counterexample to the generalized Viterbo conjecture on the spectral distance in a disc cotangent bundle](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22175)
- **Abstract:** The authors prove unboundedness of the Lagrangian spectral distance under topological and geometric assumptions on the closed base manifold, disproving a generalization of Viterbo's conjecture, and classify the surface cases where it holds over `Z₂`.
- **Authors:** Filip Broćić, Baptiste Serraille, Ibrahim Trifa
- **arXiv:** `2610.22175v1` (published 2026-10-15T16:32:49Z; categories: `math.SG`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** A concrete counterexample to a named conjecture, with a second classification result; the HTML introduction states both the conjecture and the precise hypotheses of the main theorem. This is a substantial mathematical claim, not an observed readership signal.
- **Claimed result:** Theorem 1 proves unbounded spectral distance for Lagrangians Hamiltonian-isotopic to the zero section under the hypotheses below; the abstract also reports a classification of all surfaces for which the conjecture holds over `Z₂` (arXiv abstract and HTML introduction).
- **Assumptions and setting:** A closed connected `R`-orientable Riemannian manifold with a metric without conjugate points, `H¹(M,R) ≠ 0`, and Euler characteristic nonzero in the coefficient ring `R`.
- **Caveat:** The disproof is conditional on these hypotheses; it does not decide the conjecture for every closed manifold. The paper's surface classification is specifically over `Z₂`.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, 16 October 2026 batch
- **Themes:** Geometry & topology

- **arXiv:** `2610.22351v1` (published 2026-10-15T17:57:39Z; categories: `math.LO`, `math.MG`)

- **Claimed result:** The author constructs a complete, second-countable metric space with uncountable Scott rank and develops a Baire-category variant used to reprove known rank bounds (arXiv abstract).
- **Assumptions and setting:** The object is a complete, second-countable metric space; the rank notion is Scott rank in the descriptive-set-theoretic setting.
- **Caveat:** The abstract gives the existence result and application but not enough construction detail to assess how explicit or canonical the example is; the proof has not been independently checked here.

### 4. [The Hopf conjecture with `T³`-symmetry](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22173)
- **Abstract:** The authors prove positive Euler characteristic for closed even-dimensional manifolds of positive sectional curvature that admit an isometric three-torus action, and extend the result to orbifolds.
- **Authors:** Jan Nienhaus, Dennis Wulle
- **arXiv:** `2610.22173v1` (published 2026-10-15T16:32:27Z; categories: `math.DG`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** The paper establishes a direct theorem in a central curvature/topology conjecture's setting and includes an orbifold extension; the result is stated in the arXiv abstract.
- **Claimed result:** Every closed even-dimensional manifold with positive sectional curvature and an isometric `T³` action has positive Euler characteristic; the authors prove the analogous result for orbifolds (arXiv abstract).
- **Assumptions and setting:** Closed, even-dimensional, positive sectional curvature, with an isometric action by a 3-torus; a corresponding orbifold result is also claimed.
- **Caveat:** This is a symmetry-restricted case; it does not settle the Hopf conjecture without the `T³`-symmetry assumption.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, 16 October 2026 batch
- **Themes:** Geometry & topology

### 5. [Spectral comparison on Specht modules and mixing of the interchange process](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22151)
- **Abstract:** The paper characterizes when minimal eigenvalues of interchange-process Laplacians on two Specht modules are comparable independently of edge rates, gives an inverse-polynomial comparison bound in those cases, and derives mixing-time consequences.
- **Authors:** Gil Alon
- **arXiv:** `2610.22151v1` (published 2026-10-15T16:19:06Z; categories: `math.PR`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** It links representation-theoretic spectral comparisons to mixing times, proves unconditional bounds, and formulates a precise constant conjecture with implications for Oliveira's mixing-time conjecture (arXiv abstract).
- **Claimed result:** In comparable cases, the paper establishes an inverse-polynomial lower bound for the rate-independent comparison constant and obtains unconditional mixing-time bounds for interchange and exclusion processes (arXiv abstract).
- **Assumptions and setting:** Interchange-process Laplacians on Specht modules, with comparisons required to hold uniformly over edge rates.
- **Caveat:** The proposed universal constant `2/3` is a conjecture, not a proved theorem; the abstract does not state the explicit unconditional mixing-time bounds.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, 16 October 2026 batch
- **Themes:** Probability & statistics; Combinatorics & discrete mathematics

- **Abstract:** The authors develop a perturbation theory for minimal submanifolds with strongly isolated singularities and give a criterion for their persistence across open sets of background metrics, including examples from spherical suspensions of Veronese embeddings.
- **Authors:** Alessandro Carlotto, Yangyang Li, Zhihan Wang
- **arXiv:** `2610.22229v1` (published 2026-10-15T17:08:58Z; categories: `math.DG`, `math.AP`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** The abstract states a new persistence criterion and concrete examples in four sphere dimensions; the cross-listing connects differential geometry and PDE/analysis.
- **Claimed result:** The authors establish a “sporadic persistence” criterion and produce families of continuously varying stationary varifolds with isolated singularities observable on an open set of metrics (arXiv abstract).
- **Assumptions and setting:** Perturbative setting of varying background Riemannian metrics; the singularities are strongly isolated. Examples are spherical suspensions of Veronese embeddings associated with the normed division algebras.
- **Caveat:** The stated examples concern specific suspensions in spheres of dimensions 4, 7, 13 and 25; the abstract does not imply persistence for general singular minimal submanifolds.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, 16 October 2026 batch
- **Themes:** Geometry & topology; Analysis & PDE

## Open Problems and Research Directions

- **Explicit conjecture (Gou):** The paper asks whether its sharp constants persist in larger denominator ranges and whether analogous bounds hold for sums over smooth numbers; it also supplies counterexamples to some stronger formulations. A follow-up could delineate the maximal denominator ranges and identify which smooth-number hypotheses avoid those obstructions. [2610.22345]
- **Explicit conjecture (Alon):** The paper conjectures that the Specht-module comparison constant can always be `2/3`; its proof would imply Oliveira's interchange-process mixing conjecture through the stated comparison method. A direct proof or a counterexample would clarify the mixing-time consequence. [2610.22151]
- **Scope extension (synthesis):** The Hopf-conjecture result leaves open what can be proved under weaker symmetry or no torus action; the Viterbo counterexample likewise leaves cases outside its geometric/topological hypotheses unresolved. Mapping the boundary of these assumptions is a natural next step, not a claim made as a new result by these authors. [2610.22173; 2610.22175]
