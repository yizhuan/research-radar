### 1. [$C^{0}$ stability of torsion-free $G_{2}$ structures along the Laplacian flow](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26144)
- **Abstract:** The authors claim that torsion-free $G_{2}$ structures are dynamically stable under Laplacian flow for closed initial structures in a $C^{0}$ neighbourhood.
- **Authors:** Chuanhuan Li, Yi Li
- **arXiv:** `2611.26144v1` (published 2026-11-19 13:59:38 UTC; categories: `math.DG`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** Collector score 4.277 (citation component 3.584; influential-citation component 0.693); Semantic Scholar snapshot: 5 citations, 1 influential citation; 0 cross-lists.
- **Claimed result:** The authors prove that on a compact 7-manifold, closed $G_{2}$ structures sufficiently close in $C^{0}$ to a torsion-free structure flow for all time and converge smoothly to its identity-component diffeomorphism orbit.
- **Assumptions and setting:** A compact 7-manifold, a torsion-free reference structure, and a sufficiently $C^{0}$-close closed initial structure in the reference cohomology class.
- **Caveat:** The stability statement depends on compactness, the reference torsion-free structure, and the stated neighbourhood and cohomology-class hypotheses.

### 2. [Ordering Is Not Deciding: Towards Preference-Consistent Multi-Objective Design–Decision Optimisation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25875)
- **Abstract:** Constructed examples show that Pareto filtering can discard the solution with the highest aggregated actor preference, while the proposed IMAP method identifies it.
- **Authors:** E. Özer, A. R. M. Wolfert
- **arXiv:** `2611.25875v1` (published 2026-11-19 10:32:21 UTC; categories: `math.OC`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** Collector score 2.890 (citation component 2.197; influential-citation component 0.693); Semantic Scholar snapshot: 2 citations, 1 influential citation; 0 cross-lists.
- **Claimed result:** Across constructed design-decision problems, the authors show Pareto optimality does not guarantee preference-consistent choice; IMAP can identify a best-fit solution that Pareto filtering removes.
- **Assumptions and setting:** Examples use two system performances, up to four preference functions from up to two actors, and independent or interdependent performance relations with monotone or non-monotone preferences.
- **Caveat:** The comparison uses deliberately simplified constructed cases and does not establish comparative performance on real-world decision problems.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, announcement-batch date 2026-11-19
- **Themes:** Optimization & control

### 3. [Uniform Positivity of the $F$-Signature and Pure Descent of klt Singularities](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26163)
- **Abstract:** The authors characterize klt-type singularities through uniform positive lower bounds for the $F$-signatures of reductions modulo sufficiently large primes, and apply this to pure descent.
- **Authors:** Rahul Ajit, Daniel Apsley
- **arXiv:** `2611.26163v1` (published 2026-11-19 14:17:42 UTC; categories: `math.AG`, `math.AC`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** Collector score 2.329 (citation component 1.386; influential-citation component 0.693; cross-list component 0.250); Semantic Scholar snapshot: 1 citation, 1 influential citation; 1 cross-list.
- **Claimed result:** For normal complex singularities spread over a finite-type model, the authors prove that klt type gives uniform positive lower bounds for $F$-signatures on an open set of large-characteristic reductions, while non-klt type forces those signatures below every positive threshold on an open set; they also give a reduction-mod-$p$ proof of pure descent.
- **Assumptions and setting:** Normal complex varieties spread out over a finite-type model over $\mathbb{Z}$; the conclusions concern closed points in nonempty open subsets of that model.
- **Caveat:** The authors explicitly leave unresolved the separate conjecture that klt type is equivalent to strong $F$-regularity of reductions for all sufficiently large primes; their criterion uses uniform $F$-signature bounds.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, announcement-batch date 2026-11-19
- **Themes:** Algebra & number theory

## Open Problems and Research Directions

- **Open problem (authors, paper 1):** The introduction notes that long-time existence for Laplacian flow under only bounded scalar curvature remains open. A follow-up direction is to test whether the new $C^{0}$ stability estimates can relax other curvature or regularity assumptions; that extension is a synthesis, not a result of this paper.
- **Open problem (authors, paper 3):** The authors leave open whether strongly $F$-regular-type singularities admit uniform positive lower bounds for their $F$-signatures; they identify this as relevant to the unresolved strong-$F$-regularity characterization conjecture.
- **Open problem (authors, paper 9):** The authors formulate a broader non-extendibility conjecture for triangular $D(n)$-pairs; the proved result covers only a finite explicitly determined parameter set.
- **Research direction (synthesis, papers 2 and 4):** Test whether the preference-filtering counterexamples persist with more objectives and realistic design constraints, and whether the reported scheduling savings persist under different price years, workloads, and operational limits.
- **Research direction (synthesis, papers 5 and 7):** For paper 5, examine nondegeneracy outside the near-local half-space Neumann regime; for paper 7, determine sharper ranges or explicit values for the cohomological lower bounds. These are extensions suggested by the reported scope, not claims of novelty or tractability.
