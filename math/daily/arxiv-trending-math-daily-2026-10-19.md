## Top papers (ranked)

### 1. [Near optimal gap in the Lonely Runner Conjecture](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.23790)
- **Abstract:** For every finite set of n ≥ 2 distinct positive integer speeds, the authors prove a uniform lower bound ML(V) > (1+c)/(2n) for an absolute c > 0, improving the basic 1/(2n) bound.[3]
- **Authors:** Xiyu Hu, Yifan Jing
- **arXiv:** `2610.23790v1` (published 2026-10-16 17:19:46 UTC; categories: `math.NT`, `math.CO`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** The paper reports a constant-factor improvement over the union-bound baseline, rather than a lower-order correction; it is cross-listed in number theory and combinatorics, and received an independent report that describes the result as major (that report itself says it is awaiting review).[3][8]
- **Claimed result:** The authors prove a positive absolute relative improvement over 1/(2n), using a refinement of a large-GCD argument and a new interval-multiplicity “spillover” inequality.[3]
- **Assumptions and setting:** V is any set of n ≥ 2 distinct positive integers; the exact Lonely Runner conjectured threshold 1/(n+1) is not proved.[3]
- **Caveat:** The constant c is absolute and explicit in the paper, but the authors do not optimize it; the result remains short of the conjectured bound.[3]
- **Announcement type:** new submission, 19 Oct 2026 announcement batch
- **Themes:** Number theory; Combinatorics & discrete mathematics

### 2. [Brennan's Conjecture and the Universal Integral Means Spectrum](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.23853)
- **Abstract:** The authors present a shorter proof of a stronger statement after checking recent preprints claiming Brennan's conjecture and a broader conjecture of Kraetzer; they say much of the earlier argument can be removed.[2]
- **Authors:** Ilia Binder, Oleg Ivrii, Istvan Prause, Stanislav Smirnov
- **arXiv:** `2610.23853v1` (published 2026-10-16 17:59:11 UTC; categories: `math.CV`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** It addresses a famous conjecture in geometric function theory and claims a stronger statement than the recently posted proofs it discusses; this is a mathematical-significance signal, not measured popularity.[2]
- **Claimed result:** The authors claim a drastically shorter proof of a significantly stronger statement than the cited recent proofs; the abstract says the broader context and consequences are deferred to follow-up work.[2]
- **Assumptions and setting:** Brennan's claim concerns area integrability of derivatives of conformal maps from simply connected proper plane domains, for exponents 4/3 < p < 4; this note focuses on the proof and universal integral-means spectrum.[2]
- **Caveat:** This is a short, unrefereed note that explicitly sacrifices context and consequences; treat the theorem as the authors' preprint claim, not as peer-reviewed consensus.[2]
- **Announcement type:** new submission, 19 Oct 2026 announcement batch
- **Themes:** Analysis & PDE

### 3. [Sharp maximum estimates for linear uniformly elliptic equations with measurable coefficients](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.23596)
- **Abstract:** The authors claim sharp exponents for Aleksandrov–Bakelman–Pucci maximum estimates with measurable coefficients, proving Pucci's 1966 conjecture and giving a further sharp formulation for higher-dimensional uniform ellipticity.[4]
- **Authors:** Shibing Chen, Neil S. Trudinger, Xu-Jia Wang
- **arXiv:** `2610.23596v1` (published 2026-10-16 15:15:27 UTC; categories: `math.AP`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** The abstract explicitly targets a conjecture dating to 1966 and sharpens the standard higher-dimensional ellipticity formulation; those are concrete theorem-scope signals.[4]
- **Claimed result:** For trace-normalized ellipticity in dimensions n ≥ 3, the stated maximum estimate holds for every p above Pucci's proposed critical exponent; the paper also gives a sharp threshold under the standard eigenvalue-ratio formulation.[4]
- **Assumptions and setting:** Linear nondivergence-form uniformly elliptic equations, measurable symmetric positive-definite coefficient matrices, and the Sobolev/continuity class stated in the paper; the estimates apply on finite-volume open sets without boundary regularity.[4]
- **Caveat:** The threshold depends on the chosen formulation of uniform ellipticity, and the claimed result is still a new, unrefereed preprint.[4]
- **Announcement type:** new submission, 19 Oct 2026 announcement batch
- **Themes:** Analysis & PDE

### 4. [Height of minimal surfaces: sharp thresholds](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.23629)
- **Abstract:** The authors identify sharp aperture thresholds for proper holomorphic curves and describe distinct volume-growth regimes for stationary integral varifolds near the borderline of linear height growth.[5]
- **Authors:** Tobias Holck Colding, William P. Minicozzi II
- **arXiv:** `2610.23629v1` (published 2026-10-16 15:34:19 UTC; categories: `math.DG`, `math.AP`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** The result supplies explicit sharp thresholds and a quantitative near-cone growth exponent, and bridges differential geometry with analysis of PDEs through its cross-listing.[5]
- **Claimed result:** For stationary integral varifolds, narrow cones of aperture ε force polynomial volume growth with exponent n+O(ε²), while wide cones permit arbitrarily fast growth; the ε² order is shown sharp in R³.[5]
- **Assumptions and setting:** Proper holomorphic submanifolds and stationary integral varifolds under height/aperture conditions; the holomorphic-curve thresholds also depend on the plane's Kähler angle.[5]
- **Caveat:** The geometric conclusions are conditional on those regularity and cone/height hypotheses; the abstract does not make a universal claim about arbitrary minimal surfaces.[5]
- **Announcement type:** new submission, 19 Oct 2026 announcement batch
- **Themes:** Geometry & topology; Analysis & PDE

### 5. [Disproof of Wright's Problems 14 and 15 on lacunary maximal functions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.23563)
- **Abstract:** The authors disprove two proposed weak-endpoint bounds by constructing a compactly supported singular probability measure with Fourier decay and showing the associated maximal operator has infinite weak-L¹ and H¹-to-weak-L¹ norms.[6]
- **Authors:** Xi Cen, Guixiang Hong, Xinfeng Wu
- **arXiv:** `2610.23563v1` (published 2026-10-16 14:50:24 UTC; categories: `math.CA`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** This is a direct counterexample to named open problems from a 2017 harmonic-analysis problem collection, with an explicit failure mechanism rather than a partial estimate.[6]
- **Claimed result:** For every d ≥ 1, the authors exhibit a fixed compactly supported singular probability measure with Fourier decay of every order 0 < ε < 1/2 for which both stated endpoint operator norms are infinite.[6]
- **Assumptions and setting:** The result concerns dyadic convolution maximal operators and the specific weak-L¹ and H¹ endpoint bounds posed in Wright's Problems 14 and 15.[6]
- **Caveat:** It refutes those endpoint bounds; it does not settle other lacunary maximal-function estimates or remove the paper's specific measure/operator setup.[6]
- **Announcement type:** new submission, 19 Oct 2026 announcement batch
- **Themes:** Analysis & PDE

### 6. [Analyticity of the correlation length in subcritical FK-percolation and Potts models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.23843)
- **Abstract:** The authors prove that the correlation length is analytic throughout the subcritical parameter regime for FK-percolation and Potts models on the lattice Z^d.[7]
- **Authors:** Lucas D'Alimonte, Loïc Gassmann
- **arXiv:** `2610.23843v1` (published 2026-10-16 17:57:39 UTC; categories: `math.PR`, `math-ph`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** It gives a full-regime regularity result in probability/statistical mechanics and is cross-listed with mathematical physics, adding a distinct area to the day's leading theorem-driven submissions.[7]
- **Claimed result:** The authors establish analyticity of the correlation length throughout the subcritical phase, using the leading eigenvalue of the FK–Ruelle operator and analytic perturbation theory; they also derive a restriction on rotational invariance.[7]
- **Assumptions and setting:** FK-percolation and Potts models on Z^d, restricted to the subcritical parameter regime.[7]
- **Caveat:** The result is confined to the subcritical phase; the abstract does not address criticality or supercritical regularity.[7]
- **Announcement type:** new submission, 19 Oct 2026 announcement batch
- **Themes:** Probability & statistics; Mathematical physics

## Open Problems and Research Directions

- **Open problem (authors' framing):** The Lonely Runner conjecture still asks for ML(V) ≥ 1/(n+1), while this paper improves only on 1/(2n); the authors' constant is not optimized. Tighten the universal constant and test whether the interval-multiplicity method can approach the conjectured threshold.[3]
- **Research direction (informed synthesis):** The Brennan note says fuller context and consequences are deferred. A useful follow-up is to state precisely which universal-spectrum consequences follow from the shorter proof and compare its hypotheses with the earlier cited arguments.[2]
- **Research direction (informed synthesis):** The FK/Potts theorem stops at subcriticality. Study whether the same operator-analytic framework yields useful one-sided regularity near criticality, where the preprint does not claim analyticity.[7]
