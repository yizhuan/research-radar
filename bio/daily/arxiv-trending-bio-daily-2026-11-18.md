### 1. [Structured AI ideation yields physical strategies for epithelial barrier repair](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.22988)
- **Abstract:** Abelio structures AI-driven hypothesis generation and refinement; its PEG and cholesterol-anchored complementary-DNA proposals were tested in damaged Caco-2 intestinal epithelial monolayers.
- **Authors:** Geonho Nah; Johyun Park; Kyung-Ah Cho; Suyoung Hwang; Yoon Hee Lim; Eun-kyung Min; Seunghyun Moon; Donghyun Koh; Seungwon Kim; Jiho Park; Hojin Yoo; Myungsun Nam; Haneul Choi; Kyungsu Kim; In-Sun Hong; YunJae Jung; Minhyeong Lee
- **arXiv:** `2611.22988v1` (published 2026-11-17T16:05:22Z; categories: `cs.AI`, `q-bio.QM`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** q-bio.QM cross-list; benchmarked on 50 curated research objectives against four frontier LLMs, with two AI-proposed approaches tested in cell monolayers.
- **Claimed contribution:** The authors report that complementary DNA caused sequence-specific, reversible barrier-readout changes; their PEG experiments found effects depended on membrane-surface configuration.
- **Caveat:** Biological evidence is in vitro in Caco-2 cells; the proposed membrane geometries were not directly visualized, and scientific-soundness scoring partly uses an LLM judge.
- **Announcement type:** new submission (cross-listed), 2026-11-18
- **Themes:** Molecular & cell biology; DNA & genomics; Quantitative methods

### 2. [Discovery of new antiviral agents through artificial intelligence: in vitro and in vivo results](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.22230)
- **Abstract:** A reinforcement-learning platform generated neuraminidase-targeting molecules; two selected compounds inhibited influenza A/B in enzyme and cell assays and showed activity in mice.
- **Authors:** Roza Izmailyan; Mher Matevosyan; Hamlet Khachatryan; Anastasiya Shavina; Smbat Gevorgyan; Artur Ghazaryan; Irina Tirosyan; Yeva Gabrielyan; Marusya Ayvazyan; Boris Martirosyan; Vardan Harutyunyan; Hovakim Zakaryan
- **arXiv:** `2611.22230v1` (published 2026-11-17T05:22:24Z; categories: `q-bio.BM`, `cs.LG`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** Primary q-bio.BM listing; peer-reviewed publication is identified in the arXiv record (Antiviral Research, 2024; DOI 10.1016/j.antiviral.2024.105818), with enzyme, cell, and mouse evidence.
- **Claimed contribution:** The authors report two compounds with cell-assay EC50 values of 0.29–2.31 μM and 65–100% protection in the reported mouse infection groups; groups had six mice each.
- **Caveat:** This is an accepted manuscript of a 2024 article, not new 2026 experimental work. Evidence is preclinical; the compounds were tested at higher doses than oseltamivir and no human efficacy or safety is established.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, 2026-11-18
- **Themes:** Biochemistry & proteins; Biomedical & translational medicine; Quantitative methods

### 3. [Rule-based curation and validation in batch fermentation of a genome-scale metabolic model of the mycoprotein fungus Fusarium venenatum A3/5](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21775)
- **Abstract:** The authors built the iJL1143 genome-scale metabolic model for Fusarium venenatum A3/5 and compared it with a draft model in four oxygen-enriched glucose/fructose batch fermentations.
- **Authors:** Jaewook Lee; Thomas Vinestock; Miao Guo
- **arXiv:** `2611.21775v1` (published 2026-11-16T19:05:11Z; categories: `q-bio.MN`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** Primary q-bio.MN listing and comparison with four fermentation experiments; reported biomass discrepancies are quantitative and tied to measured batch runs.
- **Claimed contribution:** The authors report that curation reduced mean final-biomass discrepancy from 20% to 6%; adding a fitted lag time reduced biomass time-course error from 4.88 to 0.42 g/L.
- **Caveat:** Validation covers one strain, oxygen-enriched batch conditions, and the tested substrates; transfer to other feedstocks or industrial conditions remains unshown.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, 2026-11-18
- **Themes:** Biochemistry & proteins; Molecular & cell biology; Quantitative methods

### 4. [OCCAM: Improved Single-Cell Representation Learning and Perturbation Effect Prediction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21959)
- **Abstract:** OCCAM combines denoising-based cell representations with gene-interaction, text, and protein-sequence features to predict genetic-perturbation responses across contexts and gene combinations.
- **Authors:** Ihab Bendidi; Kian Kenyon-Dean; Alisandra K. Denton; Auguste Genovesio; Emmanuel Noutahi
- **arXiv:** `2611.21959v1` (published 2026-11-16T22:40:16Z; categories: `q-bio.QM`, `cs.AI`, `cs.LG`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** q-bio.QM listing and cross-listing in AI/ML; evaluated on cross-context few-shot settings, held-out cell lines, multiple assays, and double perturbations.
- **Claimed contribution:** The authors report improved perturbation-pattern matching over four retrained competitors or transfer baselines, and better double-perturbation estimates than summed single effects.
- **Caveat:** The paper says expression-level prediction remains challenging; these are computational benchmark results, not evidence of patient or therapeutic effects.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, 2026-11-18
- **Themes:** Omics & single-cell methods; Bioinformatics & sequence analysis; Quantitative methods

### 5. [ST-FateFlow: Continuous Gene-Spatial Reconstruction with Differentiation-Potential Learning from Time-Resolved Spatial Transcriptomics](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.22256)
- **Abstract:** ST-FateFlow uses structure-aware transport coupling and flow matching to infer continuous gene-expression and spatial dynamics between unpaired spatial-transcriptomic snapshots.
- **Authors:** Zhe Yu; Xiaobo Sun; Hao Wu
- **arXiv:** `2611.22256v1` (published 2026-11-17T06:00:29Z; categories: `q-bio.GN`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** Primary q-bio.GN listing; tested computationally across three public developmental and regenerative spatial-transcriptomics datasets.
- **Claimed contribution:** The authors report improved expression-distribution and spatial interpolation metrics and differentiation-associated orderings consistent with previously described trajectories.
- **Caveat:** It reconstructs missing states from public data rather than measuring lineage transitions; results depend on cross-time coupling, registration, and missing-state assumptions. The learned potential is not a fate probability or physical energy.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, 2026-11-18
- **Themes:** RNA & transcriptomics; Omics & single-cell methods; Quantitative methods

### 6. [Deep Learning-based Multiparametric Quantitative Ultrasound for Hepatic Steatosis Assessment](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.11178)
- **Abstract:** A U-Net combines quantitative-ultrasound maps to estimate spatially resolved liver fat fraction against MRI-PDFF measurements.
- **Authors:** Allison Lee; Ahmed El Kaffas; Aya Kamaya; Jeremy J. Dahl; Jihye Baek
- **arXiv:** `2611.11178v2` (first submitted 2026-11-08T06:41:34Z; updated 2026-11-17T18:37:44Z; categories: `physics.med-ph`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** Replacement revision in the 2026-11-18 batch; evaluated on ultrasound data from 30 people with healthy, suspected, or confirmed MASLD and compared with MRI-PDFF.
- **Claimed contribution:** The authors report that H-scan, attenuation, and Nakagami inputs performed best, with Spearman correlation 0.92 and AUC 0.98 in this cohort.
- **Caveat:** The cohort is small and severe steatosis underrepresented; the authors call for larger, more diverse validation and direct assessment across liver lobes. This is not a validated screening or diagnostic test.
- **Announcement type:** revision, 2026-11-18
- **Themes:** Biomedical & translational medicine; Medical physics & imaging; Quantitative methods

## Trending Research Themes

- **Reconstructing unobserved biology from sparse measurements:** ST-FateFlow and HoloGene infer continuous expression or tissue structure from spatial-transcriptomic snapshots. These are complementary computational reconstructions, not direct observations of unsampled states.
- **Prediction and optimization meet experimental readouts:** Abelio, OCCAM, and the antiviral study connect AI methods to cell, enzyme, animal, or perturbation measurements, but at very different evidence levels; only the antiviral manuscript is tied to a prior peer-reviewed paper.
- **Robust inference under biological and technical confounding:** The transcription-factor motif study tests close-motif competition; the EEG revision shows analysis-pipeline sensitivity; the QUS and fermentation studies compare model outputs with measured signals.
- **Mechanistic models clarify what measurements can and cannot establish:** Molecular memory and the IFFL analysis formalize timing and dose ordering; neither substitutes for biochemical validation.

## Open Problems and Research Directions

- **Validate inferred dynamics prospectively:** For ST-FateFlow and HoloGene, compare predictions with denser spatial sampling, lineage- or perturbation-defined intermediate states, and independently collected tissues. Report sensitivity to registration, batch effects, and missing time points; these limitations are explicitly noted for ST-FateFlow.
- **Test transfer beyond current experimental settings:** For the Fusarium model, evaluate new feedstocks and fermentation conditions. For the QUS model, recruit a larger multi-centre cohort with more severe steatosis and multi-lobe measurements, as its authors recommend. For motif discovery, test additional tissues and close paralogues.
- **Strengthen the bridge from AI proposals to biology:** Independently reproduce Abelio’s cell-monolayer results and test other barrier models; for influenza compounds, resolve target engagement and pharmacokinetics before broader preclinical work. These are research directions, not clinical advice.
- **Separate method sensitivity from biological signal:** Extend the Alzheimer EEG pipeline comparison to larger, independent cohorts and prespecify which null model matches each scientific question; the paper’s 20/20 case-control dataset and pseudo-study analysis motivate this follow-up.

## Takeaway

This batch is led by computational biology that predicts perturbations or fills gaps in sparse measurements, but the strongest empirical anchors are modest: cell monolayers, four fermentation runs, a 30-person ultrasound cohort, and a previously published mouse antiviral study. Treat reconstructed trajectories and model predictions as hypotheses until they survive independent and appropriately matched validation.
