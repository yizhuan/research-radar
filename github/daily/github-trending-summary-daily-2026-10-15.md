## Takeaway

The snapshot points to a practical, tool-oriented phase of AI development: agents are being connected to real software, audited, given specialized research or security workflows, and optimized for context. The standout REA daily gain is unusually large and should be read as a platform-reported momentum figure, not independent proof of lasting adoption. Outside AI, xtool and the enduring presence of Rust, Ghidra, and nvm show continued appetite for foundational developer tools.
