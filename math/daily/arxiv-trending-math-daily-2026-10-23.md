### 1. [Lenses make many points: Pure point spectrum disproves Simon's $L^2$ conjecture](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31637)
- **Abstract:** For every $d\ge2$ and $0\le\sigma<1/3$, the authors construct, with LLM assistance, a smooth bounded nonnegative lens-type potential with arbitrarily small stated weighted $L^2$ and supremum norms for which $-\Delta+V$ has only pure point spectrum at positive energies, contradicting Simon's conjecture.
- **Authors:** Simon Becker, Mengxuan Yang
- **arXiv:** `2610.31637v1` (published 2026-10-22 17:52:23 UTC; categories: `math.SP`, `math-ph`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** The paper's main theorem claims a counterexample to a named spectral conjecture for every dimension $d\ge2$, and it is cross-listed in spectral theory and mathematical physics; the introduction states the potential construction and spectral-averaging route.
- **Claimed result:** The authors claim that for each $d\ge2$ and $0\le\sigma<1/3$ there is a smooth bounded $V\ge0$ with arbitrarily small $\|V\|_\infty+\int_{\mathbb R^d}|x|^{1-d+\sigma}|V(x)|^2\,dx$ such that $-\Delta+V$ has pure point spectrum in $(0,\infty)$, disproving Simon's stated absolutely-continuous-spectrum conjecture.
- **Assumptions and setting:** Schrödinger operators on $\mathbb R^d$, all $d\ge2$, with the weighted-integrability exponent restricted to $0\le\sigma<1/3$; the asserted potential is smooth, bounded and nonnegative.
- **Caveat:** This is an unrefereed preprint and its elaborate multiscale construction has not been independently proof-checked here; the authors explicitly disclose LLM assistance.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, 2026-10-23
- **Themes:** Analysis & PDE; Mathematical physics

### 2. [Bounded symbols and one-component geometry](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31382)
- **Abstract:** The author characterizes exactly when all bounded truncated Toeplitz operators on a Hardy model space have bounded symbols: precisely when the defining inner function has a connected sublevel set at some level in $(0,1)$, with linked embedding, weak-factorization and de Branges-space consequences.
- **Authors:** Sam Looi
- **arXiv:** `2610.31382v1` (published 2026-10-22 14:51:44 UTC; categories: `math.FA`, `math.CV`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** The paper states a necessary-and-sufficient geometric characterization resolving a named bounded-symbol conjecture and, by known equivalences, two related conjectures; it is cross-listed across functional and complex analysis. The author reports that the results were publicly posted earlier with a DOI.
- **Claimed result:** The author proves that every bounded truncated Toeplitz operator on a Hardy model space has a bounded symbol if and only if its nonconstant defining inner function is one-component; the paper also gives a sharp logarithmic loss in a quantitative symbol-norm bound and related de Branges results.
- **Assumptions and setting:** Hardy model spaces defined by a nonconstant inner function; “one-component” means that one sublevel set $\{z:|\Theta(z)|<t\}$ is connected for some $0<t<1$.
- **Caveat:** The author says the complete results were first posted on 2026-10-07 under DOI [10.5281/zenodo.23214254](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23214254); this arXiv announcement is a delayed deposit, not a newly obtained result. The main characterization concerns Hardy model spaces, not arbitrary bounded operators.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, 2026-10-23
- **Themes:** Analysis & PDE

### 3. [Deep holes of full-length Reed--Solomon codes: uniform bounds and exact classifications](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31460)
- **Abstract:** For full-length Reed–Solomon codes, the author classifies deep holes at interpolation degrees $k+m$ for every finite field when $m=3,4,5,6,7$, and proves additional field-size-dependent exclusions and classifications for all $m\ge3$.
- **Authors:** Xiang Fan
- **arXiv:** `2610.31460v1` (published 2026-10-22 15:53:11 UTC; categories: `cs.IT`, `math.CO`, `math.NT`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** The main theorem gives unconditional finite-field classifications for five consecutive excess degrees, with uniform bounds beyond them; the paper bridges coding theory, combinatorics and number theory and includes reproducible finite certificates for remaining small cases.
- **Claimed result:** The author claims that for $m=3,\ldots,7$ the only deep-hole exceptions are the specified Segre and Frobenius-power families (at $m=4,6$, respectively, in dimension two over characteristic-two fields); for general $m\ge3$, the paper also proves uniform exclusion/classification results under explicit field-size conditions.
- **Assumptions and setting:** Full-length $\operatorname{RS}_q(k)$ evaluated over all of $\mathbb F_q$; received words whose interpolation polynomial has degree $k+m$, with $m\ge3$ and $k+m\le q-1$. The unconditional classification is only for $m=3,\ldots,7$; the broader statements require the paper's stated bounds on $q$.
- **Caveat:** These results concern the specified interpolation-degree ranges, not every deep hole for every code parameter. The paper also discusses recent parallel work, so its claims should be read in that evolving context.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, 2026-10-23
- **Themes:** Combinatorics & discrete mathematics; Algebra & number theory

### 4. [Exact controllability and rapid stabilization of linear difference delay equations and one-dimensional hyperbolic systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31225)
- **Abstract:** For finite pointwise-delay systems, the authors establish a necessary-and-sufficient Hautus-type frequency criterion for exact controllability in every $L^r$, $1\le r<\infty$, answer a prior conjecture, and derive rapid-stabilization and one-dimensional hyperbolic-system consequences.
- **Authors:** Yacine Chitour, Sébastien Fueyo, Pierre Lissy, Guilherme Mazanti
- **arXiv:** `2610.31225v1` (published 2026-10-22 13:12:03 UTC; categories: `math.OC`, `math.AP`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** The authors explicitly identify and resolve a prior conjecture on necessity of the controllability criterion for $r>1$; the result connects control theory with PDE through the method of characteristics.
- **Claimed result:** The authors prove the necessity, complementing known sufficiency, of a Hautus-type criterion for exact controllability in $L^r$; they further show it suffices for arbitrarily fast exponential stabilization in $L^2$ and extend the criterion to one-dimensional linear hyperbolic PDEs.
- **Assumptions and setting:** Linear difference-delay systems with finitely many pointwise delays, $1\le r<\infty$; the PDE application is to one-dimensional linear hyperbolic systems reducible to those delay systems.
- **Caveat:** The stated rapid-stabilization result is a sufficient consequence of the exact-controllability criterion, not a necessary-and-sufficient stabilization characterization.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, 2026-10-23
- **Themes:** Optimization & control; Analysis & PDE

### 5. [Conway's thrackle conjecture: a new upper bound of $\frac43n$](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31589)
- **Abstract:** For a planar thrackle, where every pair of edges meets exactly once, the author proves an upper bound of $\lfloor4(n-1)/3\rfloor$ edges on $n\ge4$ vertices by combining a computer-assisted small-case verification with a discharging argument.
- **Authors:** Dániel Gábor Simon
- **arXiv:** `2610.31589v1` (published 2026-10-22 17:23:50 UTC; categories: `math.CO`, `math.GN`, `math.GT`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** It gives a concrete new upper bound on a named longstanding extremal-graph conjecture and is cross-listed in combinatorics and two geometry/topology areas; its proof combines finite computation with structural embedding arguments.
- **Claimed result:** The author proves that every thrackle on $n\ge4$ vertices has at most $\lfloor4(n-1)/3\rfloor$ edges.
- **Assumptions and setting:** A thrackle is a plane drawing of a graph in which every pair of edges meets exactly once, at a shared endpoint or a proper crossing; the bound is for $n\ge4$ vertices.
- **Caveat:** The bound is weaker than Conway's conjectured $n$-edge bound, so the conjecture remains open; the proof uses a computer-assisted verification for graphs with at most twelve vertices.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, 2026-10-23
- **Themes:** Combinatorics & discrete mathematics; Geometry & topology

### 7. [Non-uniqueness for the forced one-dimensional Vlasov-Poisson equation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31629)
- **Abstract:** For every finite $p$, the author constructs a forced one-dimensional Vlasov–Poisson problem with two distinct nonnegative distributional solutions sharing zero initial data and uniformly $L^p$-bounded spatial densities, while uniqueness holds in the bounded-density class described in the paper.
- **Authors:** Mikaela Iacobelli
- **arXiv:** `2610.31629v1` (published 2026-10-22 17:47:45 UTC; categories: `math.AP`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** The paper supplies a sharp-looking nonuniqueness counterexample together with a contrasting uniqueness statement at bounded density, making the regularity threshold mathematically explicit.
- **Claimed result:** The author constructs a nonnegative ion density smooth for positive times and a source $S\in C([0,1];L^1(\mathbb R^2))$, $S(0)=0$, for which two distinct nonnegative distributional solutions in $C([0,1];L^1(\mathbb R^2))$ have the same zero initial data and spatial densities uniformly bounded in each prescribed finite $L^p(\mathbb R)$; uniqueness holds when the spatial density is bounded and the electric field is Lipschitz.
- **Assumptions and setting:** Forced Vlasov–Poisson equation on the one-dimensional whole space over $[0,1]$, with the source and solution classes stated above; the counterexample reaches every finite density exponent $p$ but not the bounded-density endpoint.
- **Caveat:** This is a forced-equation construction; it does not by itself establish nonuniqueness for the unforced equation or other solution classes.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, 2026-10-23
- **Themes:** Analysis & PDE

### 8. [Upper tail large deviations of regular subgraph counts in sparse random regular graphs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31590)
- **Abstract:** For counts of a fixed connected regular graph in sparse random regular graphs, the authors derive upper-tail large-deviation rates and conditioned structures in two asymptotic regimes, with a distinct cycle rate and structure caused by the degree constraint.
- **Authors:** Anirban Basak, Shaibal Karmakar
- **arXiv:** `2610.31590v1` (published 2026-10-22 17:24:21 UTC; categories: `math.PR`, `math.CO`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** The paper gives both a rate-function result and a structural description conditioned on a rare event, and it explicitly identifies where regular-graph behavior agrees with or differs from the Erdős–Rényi model; it is cross-listed in probability and combinatorics.
- **Claimed result:** For connected $\Delta$-regular $H$, the authors obtain the upper-tail speed and rate for copies of $H$ in $\mathbb G_n^d$ when $np^{\Delta/2}\gg(\log n)^{1/(v_H-2)}$ and $p=d/n\ll1$. For $\Delta\ge3$ the rate matches the Erdős–Rényi case; for cycles the rate differs and conditioned graphs contain disjoint clique-/biclique-like structures with high probability. They also prove matching Erdős–Rényi rates in a complementary strictly balanced-graph regime stated in the abstract.
- **Assumptions and setting:** Fixed connected $\Delta$-regular graph $H$, sparse random $d$-regular graph $\mathbb G_n^d$, $p=d/n\to0$, and the asymptotic regimes in the abstract; the cycle case ($\Delta=2$) is treated separately.
- **Caveat:** The asymptotic conclusions are regime-dependent; neither rate functions nor conditioned structures should be extrapolated beyond the hypotheses stated in the paper.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, 2026-10-23
- **Themes:** Probability & statistics; Combinatorics & discrete mathematics

- **Claimed result:** The authors give a geometric proof that if $X$ is a smooth hyperkähler fourfold, $\operatorname{Pic}(X)=\mathbb Z[L]$, and $L$ is nef, nontrivial, not big, with $\kappa(L)\ge0$, then a positive multiple of $L$ defines a Lagrangian fibration.
- **Assumptions and setting:** Smooth hyperkähler fourfold; Picard group generated by the specified line bundle $L$; $L$ nef, nontrivial, not big, and of nonnegative Kodaira dimension.
- **Caveat:** The theorem is conditional on $\kappa(L)\ge0$ and the Picard-rank-one hypothesis; it does not prove semiampleness for arbitrary hyperkähler manifolds or line bundles.

### 10. [A unified quantitative approach to Chiti and Kohler--Jobin inequalities](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31063)
- **Abstract:** The authors prove quantitative stability versions of Chiti's reverse Hölder and Kohler–Jobin inequalities for first Dirichlet eigenvalues, controlling eigenfunction distance to the ball optimizer and a resolvent distance, respectively.
- **Authors:** Vincenzo Amato, Luca Barbato, Emanuele Cristoforoni, Alba Lia Masiello, Carlo Nitsch, et al.
- **arXiv:** `2610.31063v1` (published 2026-10-22 10:41:55 UTC; categories: `math.AP`, `math.SP`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** It supplies two quantitative refinements of classical spectral/rearrangement inequalities and is cross-listed between PDE and spectral theory; one estimate is uniform over every $1\le q<p<\infty$.
- **Claimed result:** The authors prove that the Chiti deficit controls, up to translations, the $L^q$ distance between a bounded-domain first eigenfunction and that of a ball with the same first eigenvalue, for every $1\le q<p<\infty$; they also give a quantitative Kohler–Jobin estimate using the $L^\infty\!\to L^2$ distance between Dirichlet resolvents.
- **Assumptions and setting:** First Dirichlet eigenvalues and eigenfunctions of bounded domains; the first stability result assumes $1\le q<p<\infty$ and compares after translations, while the second concerns the stated resolvent norm.
- **Caveat:** The estimates are tied to these first-eigenvalue inequalities and norms; they are not a general stability theorem for arbitrary spectral data or domains.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, 2026-10-23
- **Themes:** Analysis & PDE

## Trending Research Themes

- **Sharp criteria and classification continue to make a strong appearance in this selected set.** The model-space characterization in Looi, the finite-field Reed–Solomon classification by Fan, and the controllability criterion of Chitour–Fueyo–Lissy–Mazanti each turn a qualitative property into an exact or near-exact test, but their techniques and fields are distinct; this is a shared theorem style, not evidence of one common method.
- **Extremal and near-extremal behavior is being quantified in several separate settings.** Suzuki's Gaussian stability statement and Amato et al.'s eigenfunction/resolvent deficits both measure distance from an optimizer; Basak–Karmakar instead describe the typical structure conditioned on a rare graph-count event. These are analogous questions about rigidity, not a shared proof technique.
- **Counterexamples expose boundaries of qualitative principles.** Becker–Yang challenge a spectral-preservation conjecture, Iacobelli exhibits nonuniqueness below a bounded-density threshold, and Simon's thrackle bound narrows—but does not settle—an extremal conjecture. These are isolated results across analysis and combinatorics, not a field-wide shift.
- **The batch is broad, with primary-category volume led by analysis of PDEs (45/392), combinatorics (39/392), optimization/control (35/392), and numerical analysis (29/392).** The selected papers were intentionally spread across subject areas rather than chosen to mirror category volume.

## Open Problems and Research Directions

- **Open problem (explicitly still unresolved in the paper):** Conway's conjectured upper bound of $n$ edges for an $n$-vertex thrackle remains open after the new $\lfloor4(n-1)/3\rfloor$ bound. A follow-up direction is to improve the factor $4/3$ or remove/replace the finite computer-assisted base case (Simon, [2610.31589](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31589)).
- **Open scope (paper-stated limitations):** Fan's unconditional classification covers only excess degrees $m=3,\ldots,7$; the broader $m\ge3$ results impose field-size hypotheses. Extending the unconditional range and closing the small-field/parameter gaps are concrete directions, while accounting for the parallel work discussed by the author (Fan, [2610.31460](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31460)).
- **Direction (synthesis):** Independently verify the LLM-assisted multiscale spectral construction and test how far its weighted smallness range can be strengthened or generalized to other decay assumptions; this is a validation and extension proposal, not an author-claimed open conjecture (Becker–Yang, [2610.31637](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31637)).
- **Direction (synthesis):** Map the regularity boundary between Iacobelli's finite-$L^p$ nonuniqueness examples and uniqueness for bounded spatial density, and test whether analogous thresholds persist for related forcing or solution classes (Iacobelli, [2610.31629](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31629)).
- **Direction (synthesis):** Resolve the transition between the two stated sparse-graph asymptotic regimes and investigate whether the conditioned cycle structures interpolate between the clique/biclique-like and edge-disjoint patterns (Basak–Karmakar, [2610.31590](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31590)).
