## Top papers (ranked)

### 1. [Fractal dimension of critical Gaussian free field sign clusters](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.03703)
- **Abstract:** In low-dimensional settings, the authors identify equivalent notions of macroscopic Gaussian-free-field sign clusters and prove that subsequential scaling limits in dimensions 3, 4 and 5 have Hausdorff and Minkowski dimensions 1 + d/2.
- **Authors:** Alexander Drewitz, Alexis Prévost, Pierre-François Rodriguez
- **arXiv:** `2610.03703v1` (published 2026-10-02 17:55:57 UTC; categories: `math.PR`, `math-ph`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** The abstract states a dimension formula matching Werner's conjecture for every subsequential scaling limit in three dimensions and links probability with mathematical physics; these are concrete, checkable claims, not evidence of readership.
- **Claimed result:** The authors prove macroscopic cluster equivalence on a large class of sufficiently low-dimensional transient weighted graphs and show the scaling-limit cluster dimensions equal 1 + d/2 for d=3,4,5.
- **Assumptions and setting:** Metric-graph sign clusters of the Gaussian free field; transient weighted graphs below the mean-field regime; small-mesh critical clusters on εZ^d for d=3,4,5.
- **Caveat:** The abstract establishes properties of every subsequential scaling limit, not uniqueness or convergence of the full family of scaling limits.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, 5 October 2026 batch
- **Themes:** Probability & statistics; Mathematical physics

### 2. [Quantitative Gowers uniformity of the primes in intervals of length X^{5/8+ε}](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.03707)
- **Abstract:** The paper proves quantitative Gowers-norm bounds for the von Mangoldt function minus a Siegel-corrected model in short intervals of length at least X^{5/8+ε}, with quasipolynomial savings.
- **Authors:** Joni Teräväinen
- **arXiv:** `2610.03707v1` (published 2026-10-02 17:57:00 UTC; categories: `math.NT`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** A stated first quantitative bound at a specific short-interval scale, a new inverse-theorem ingredient, and an application to shifted-prime patterns make the claimed contribution unusually concrete.
- **Claimed result:** The author claims the first quantitative Gowers bounds in this setting and an efficient non-abelian Type II inverse theorem; the abstract also describes a joint application with Florian Richter to infinitely many `{x, x+y, xy}` patterns in the shifted primes.
- **Assumptions and setting:** Intervals `(X, X+H]` with `H ≥ X^{5/8+ε}`; norms are for the von Mangoldt function after subtracting a Siegel-corrected model.
- **Caveat:** The theorem is stated at the specified short-interval scale and for the corrected model; it is not an unrestricted uniformity result for arbitrary intervals.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, 5 October 2026 batch
- **Themes:** Algebra & number theory

### 3. [Simpson's closedness conjecture in arbitrary rank](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.03542)
- **Abstract:** For a compact Riemann surface and a stable graded Higgs bundle, the author proves that Simpson's associated locus of flat bundles is Zariski closed in the de Rham moduli space, in arbitrary rank.
- **Authors:** Tianzhi Hu
- **arXiv:** `2610.03542v1` (published 2026-10-02 16:25:12 UTC; categories: `math.AG`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** The abstract explicitly claims resolution of a named conjecture in arbitrary rank and gives a specific determinant-line/Quillen-geometry mechanism.
- **Claimed result:** The author claims the closedness conjecture by proving exactness of a determinant connection form and using it to control one-parameter degenerations.
- **Assumptions and setting:** Compact Riemann surface; stable graded Higgs bundle; locus of flat bundles admitting the corresponding Simpson filtration.
- **Caveat:** This is a new preprint claim; the abstract's setting is the stated compact-curve and stable-bundle framework.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, 5 October 2026 batch
- **Themes:** Geometry & topology

### 4. [Geometric triangle-free graphs of large chromatic number](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.03517)
- **Abstract:** The paper gives geometric constructions of triangle-free and large-girth graphs with high chromatic number, including box-intersection graphs in three dimensions whose chromatic number is `(log n)^{1-o(1)}`.
- **Authors:** István Tomon
- **arXiv:** `2610.03517v1` (published 2026-10-02 16:11:02 UTC; categories: `math.CO`, `math.MG`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** The abstract reports a first improvement over a 1965 double-logarithmic lower bound, a near-match to an O(log n) upper bound, and an answer to a named question; it also spans combinatorics and metric geometry.
- **Claimed result:** Among several constructions, the author obtains a triangle-free intersection graph of n boxes in R³ with independence number `n(log n)^{-1+o(1)}` and chromatic number `(log n)^{1-o(1)}`.
- **Assumptions and setting:** The headline bound concerns intersection graphs of boxes in R³; the paper also treats unit-distance graphs, line-intersection graphs, circle tangency graphs, and ordered graphs.
- **Caveat:** These are specific constructions and asymptotic bounds; the abstract does not settle the unrestricted extremal questions for all geometric graph classes.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, 5 October 2026 batch
- **Themes:** Combinatorics & discrete mathematics; Geometry & topology

### 5. [Counterexamples to the Strong Roberson Conjecture](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.03550)
- **Abstract:** The author constructs connected graphs refuting the Strong Roberson Conjecture and its immersion analogue, with explicit infinite families including cubic bipartite examples.
- **Authors:** Arnar Á. Kristjánsson
- **arXiv:** `2610.03550v1` (published 2026-10-02 16:33:02 UTC; categories: `math.CO`, `cs.DM`, `cs.LO`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** The abstract claims explicit counterexamples to two conjectural statements and cross-lists the work across combinatorics, discrete mathematics and logic in computer science.
- **Claimed result:** For certain connected excluded graphs H, homomorphism counts from graphs excluding H as a minor determine the number of homomorphisms from H to any target; analogous counterexamples are given for immersions.
- **Assumptions and setting:** Homomorphism-count distinguishability for graph classes defined by excluding a fixed graph as a minor or immersion.
- **Caveat:** The counterexamples concern the conjectures' stated minor- and immersion-closed settings; they do not imply failure of every homomorphism-count characterization.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, 5 October 2026 batch
- **Themes:** Combinatorics & discrete mathematics; Logic & foundations

### 6. [Limit law for Brownian cover time of the two dimensional torus](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.03555)
- **Abstract:** For Brownian motion on the unit-area two-dimensional torus, the centered square root of the time to come within ε of every point converges to a randomly shifted Gumbel law as ε tends to zero.
- **Authors:** Amir Dembo, Jay Rosen, Ofer Zeitouni
- **arXiv:** `2610.03555v1` (published 2026-10-02 16:36:21 UTC; categories: `math.PR`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** A precise limiting distribution for a classical cover-time observable is the paper's concrete central claim; the abstract identifies an explicit centering.
- **Claimed result:** The authors claim convergence in distribution of `sqrt(C_ε) - m_ε` to a randomly shifted Gumbel law for an explicit deterministic centering `m_ε`.
- **Assumptions and setting:** Brownian motion on the area-one flat two-dimensional torus; `C_ε` is the time to come within ε of every point; limit as ε→0.
- **Caveat:** The result is for this two-dimensional continuous-time torus cover observable; the abstract does not assert the same law in other geometries or dimensions.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, 5 October 2026 batch
- **Themes:** Probability & statistics

### 7. [A Matsushima theorem, uniqueness and K-polystability for Kähler-Ricci shrinkers](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.03592)
- **Abstract:** For noncompact smooth Kähler-Ricci shrinkers, the authors claim a Matsushima-type centralizer identity, uniqueness up to biholomorphism on a fixed complex manifold, and K-polystability of an associated polarized Fano fibration.
- **Authors:** Carlos Esparza, Junsheng Zhang
- **arXiv:** `2610.03592v1` (published 2026-10-02 16:59:56 UTC; categories: `math.DG`, `math.AG`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** Three linked structural claims in differential and algebraic geometry, including uniqueness and stability, are stated for noncompact shrinkers.
- **Claimed result:** The authors identify the centralizer of the soliton field with the complexification of its Killing centralizer, prove the stated uniqueness, and establish K-polystability of the associated fibration.
- **Assumptions and setting:** Noncompact smooth Kähler-Ricci shrinkers; uniqueness is asserted on a fixed complex manifold.
- **Caveat:** The abstract does not spell out the full technical hypotheses behind the associated polarized Fano fibration or uniqueness theorem; consult the paper for precise conditions.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, 5 October 2026 batch
- **Themes:** Geometry & topology

### 8. [Hausdorff dimension for exactly approximable matrices](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.03442)
- **Abstract:** For a non-increasing approximation function ψ and its lower order τψ, the authors give a Hausdorff-dimension formula for exactly ψ-approximable m×n matrices, including inhomogeneous shifts, when τψ>n/m.
- **Authors:** Yubin He, Bixuan Li, Baowei Wang
- **arXiv:** `2610.03442v1` (published 2026-10-02 15:25:23 UTC; categories: `math.NT`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** The abstract gives an explicit formula across matrix dimensions and includes the inhomogeneous case; this is a sharply stated theorem rather than a popularity signal.
- **Claimed result:** The authors claim `dim_H Exact_{m,n}(ψ,θ) = m(n−1) + (m+n)/(1+τψ)` under the stated lower-order condition.
- **Assumptions and setting:** Non-increasing `ψ:N→(0,∞)`, shift `θ∈R^m`, integers `m,n≥1`, and `τψ>n/m`.
- **Caveat:** The displayed formula is stated only above the threshold `τψ>n/m`; behavior at and below the boundary is not specified in the abstract.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, 5 October 2026 batch
- **Themes:** Algebra & number theory; Geometry & topology

## Trending Research Themes

- **Explicit threshold and dimension laws:** The Gowers-uniformity result specifies the short-interval threshold `H≥X^{5/8+ε}` (2610.03707), while the Diophantine-approximation paper gives a dimension formula conditional on `τψ>n/m` (2610.03442). These are separate results, not evidence of one shared research trend.
- **Geometric probability and limiting structures:** The Gaussian-free-field paper studies subsequential scaling-limit dimensions (2610.03703), while the Brownian paper proves a cover-time limit law (2610.03555). Both concern asymptotic probabilistic geometry, but in distinct models and with no common method asserted by the abstracts.
- **Discrete structures under extremal constraints:** The geometric graph constructions combine high chromatic number with triangle-free or large-girth conditions (2610.03517); the homomorphism-count paper gives counterexamples to minor/immersion conjectures (2610.03550). Both are combinatorial, with different questions and techniques.
- **Geometry of moduli and stability:** The arbitrary-rank closedness claim (2610.03542) and Kähler-Ricci shrinker results (2610.03592) address structural questions in complex/algebraic geometry; the papers should not be read as a single demonstrated shift.

## Open Problems and Research Directions

- **Scaling-limit uniqueness (proposed follow-up):** The Gaussian-free-field abstract characterizes every subsequential limit but does not state uniqueness. Investigating whether the full family converges, and whether the result extends beyond d=3,4,5 under suitable hypotheses, follows directly from the stated scope (2610.03703). This is a suggested direction, not an open problem explicitly quoted from the authors.
- **Boundary of the dimension formula (abstract leaves open):** The exactly-approximable-matrix formula is stated for `τψ>n/m`; determine whether analogous formulas hold at the threshold or under weaker approximation-function hypotheses (2610.03442). This is a proposed extension, not a claim that the paper proves such cases.
- **Beyond the constructed graph families (proposed follow-up):** The geometric graph results give near-logarithmic chromatic bounds for specified constructions; testing whether comparable bounds hold in other geometric intersection models or improve the remaining logarithmic gap would extend the reported results (2610.03517).
