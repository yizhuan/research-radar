## Open Problems and Research Directions

- **Open problem (authors; [`2611.27430`](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.27430)):** conductivity fits use ion-specific charge-smearing lengths. **Research direction (synthesis):** test whether parameters calibrated in one solvent predict independent conductivity datasets, and quantify sensitivity to uncertain solvent and ion inputs.
- **Open problem (authors; [`2611.27980`](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.27980)):** large-angle-scattering corrections can be non-negligible, so leading-log estimates may miss relaxation behavior. **Research direction (synthesis):** compare the full collision-integral predictions across interaction potentials with measurements of angular-mode relaxation in clean two-dimensional Fermi liquids.
- **Open problem (authors; [`2611.27981`](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.27981)):** the thermopower overshoot remains a prediction. **Research direction (synthesis):** measure its wave-vector and temperature dependence in a high-mobility thermocouple while varying disorder to test the predicted logarithmic scaling.
- **Open problem (authors; [`2611.26737`](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26737)):** finite systems show residual finite-size effects, and the entropy formula presumes ramp-time averaging. **Research direction (synthesis):** map the crossover with larger numerical systems and controlled ramp-noise distributions.
- **Open problem (authors; [`2611.27367`](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.27367)):** regularity conditions are derived in spherical symmetry for a selected class of second-order theories. **Research direction (synthesis):** test their persistence for less symmetric geometries and additional matter couplings before drawing broader conclusions.
- **Open problem (authors; [`2611.27581`](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.27581)):** the relation between the size-effect length and microfracture percolation is proposed, not established for real materials. **Research direction (synthesis):** compare disorder-controlled crack-tip imaging and size-effect measurements across material systems and dimensionalities.
- **Open problem (authors; [`2611.26693`](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26693)):** half-chiral protection is demonstrated in constructed Hamiltonians. **Research direction (synthesis):** identify candidate lattices with the required projected symmetry and test for robust zero-energy corner states under symmetry-preserving perturbations.
- **Open problem (authors; [`2611.27584`](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.27584); 2611.27328):** probe-trajectory ambiguity affects thermometry, while the cavity paper reports hardware characterization rather than the intended long-duration interferometer demonstration. **Research direction (synthesis):** evaluate adaptive thermometry under varied probe/bath conditions and use the characterized cavity in a full millimeter-separation, minute-scale interferometry sequence.
- **Open problem (authors; [`2611.27955`](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.27955)):** the predicted breakdown of a universal screening law is based on model hierarchies. **Research direction (synthesis):** measure screening lengths across electrolytes with controlled solvent polarity and ion structure to distinguish model-dependent from broadly shared scaling.
