### 1. [Lenses make many points: Pure point spectrum disproves Simon's L² conjecture](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31637)
- **Abstract:** The authors construct, for every dimension d ≥ 2 and 0 ≤ σ < 1/3, smooth bounded nonnegative potentials of arbitrarily small weighted L² size for which the Schrödinger operator has only pure point spectrum at positive energies, claiming a counterexample to Simon's conjecture.
- **Authors:** Simon Becker, Mengxuan Yang
- **arXiv:** `2610.31637v1` (published 2026-10-22 17:52:23 UTC; categories: `math.SP`, `math-ph`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** A search surfaced independent mathematical commentary on the claimed refutation, including a source that explicitly cautions the proof has not been independently verified; the result also spans spectral theory and mathematical physics. The ranking is therefore based on a consequential, checkable author claim and visible discussion, not citation momentum.
- **Claimed result:** The authors' Theorem 1.1 constructs V with ‖V‖∞ + ∫|x|^(1−d)|V|² dx + ∫|x|^(1−d+σ)|V|² dx arbitrarily small, while −Δ+V on H²(Rᵈ) has spectrum [0,∞) and no continuous spectral component.
- **Assumptions and setting:** d ≥ 2, 0 ≤ σ < 1/3, δ > 0; V is smooth, bounded, and nonnegative. The claim refutes the higher-dimensional condition that the unweighted radial L² integral alone forces absolutely continuous spectrum.
- **Caveat:** The authors disclose substantial LLM assistance in developing the construction and proof and take responsibility for the manuscript; this new preprint's proof has not been independently assessed here.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, announcement batch 2026-10-23
- **Themes:** Analysis & PDE; Mathematical physics

### 2. [Aleshin graphs are expanders](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30662)
- **Abstract:** The authors prove two-sided expansion and linear diameter bounds for Aleshin level graphs, with explicit spectral and edge-expansion constants, and show the undirected Aleshin graphs are not Ramanujan from level ten onward.
- **Authors:** Olga Kharlampovich, Alina Vdovina
- **arXiv:** `2610.30662v1` (published 2026-10-22 03:23:21 UTC; categories: `math.CO`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** An independent MathDB note flags the preprint as a claimed resolution of the Malyshev–Pak expander conjecture, while carefully saying it has not been independently checked. Semantic Scholar listed 0 current citations (not period gains); placement rests on the explicit conjecture-level result, not citation volume.
- **Claimed result:** The authors establish a normalized spectral gap of at least 1 − 2√2/3 and edge-expansion constant at least 3 − 2√2 for Aleshin graphs, improving the previous quadratic diameter bounds to linear order in the graph size.
- **Assumptions and setting:** The result concerns the named finite-level Aleshin and Bellaterra graph families; the spectral-gap constants are stated for Aleshin graphs, and the non-Ramanujan result applies to undirected Aleshin graphs at levels n ≥ 10.
- **Caveat:** The claim is about these particular graph families, not a general theorem for automaton graphs; the paper also establishes that the undirected family is not Ramanujan for n ≥ 10.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, announcement batch 2026-10-23
- **Themes:** Combinatorics & discrete mathematics

### 3. [The Critical 2D KPZ Process](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31614)
- **Abstract:** The authors construct a non-Gaussian distribution-valued critical two-dimensional KPZ process as a scaling limit of centred log partition functions of Gaussian directed polymers and as a renormalised logarithm of the critical stochastic heat flow.
- **Authors:** Quentin Berger, Francesco Caravenna, Jeremy Clark, Alexander Dunlap, Yu Gu et al.
- **arXiv:** `2610.31614v1` (published 2026-10-22 17:40:36 UTC; categories: `math.PR`, `math-ph`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** A substantial, cross-listed probability/mathematical-physics result defines a critical process and gives two linked limiting constructions. Semantic Scholar listed 0 current citations (not period gains), so the placement reflects the specific mathematical contribution rather than established popularity.
- **Claimed result:** The authors prove convergence in law to a non-trivial critical 2D KPZ field, identify it as a measurable function of the critical stochastic heat flow through a renormalised Cole–Hopf transform, and report non-Gaussianity with log-log covariance blow-up near the diagonal.
- **Assumptions and setting:** The constructions are for critical two-dimensional Gaussian directed polymers and the critical stochastic heat flow; the stated joint convergence is at finitely many fixed positive times. Averaging at shrinking spatial scales yields Gaussian fluctuations.
- **Caveat:** The paper establishes these limits in its stated Gaussian-polymer/SHF setting, not universality for every microscopic model of 2D KPZ; the authors also disclose extensive generative-AI input.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, announcement batch 2026-10-23
- **Themes:** Probability & statistics; Mathematical physics

### 4. [Positive sectional curvature on exotic spheres in infinitely many dimensions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31528)
- **Abstract:** The author constructs strictly positively curved exotic spheres in infinitely many dimensions, specifically dimensions n ≡ 3 (mod 4), n ≥ 11, and n not congruent to 255 (mod 256).
- **Authors:** Fernando Galaz-García
- **arXiv:** `2610.31528v1` (published 2026-10-22 16:36:34 UTC; categories: `math.DG`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** The claimed infinite-dimensional existence result challenges the prior landscape described in the abstract and is supported by archived programs and outputs at Zenodo; this is a concrete geometric advance, not a readership signal.
- **Claimed result:** The author computes an Eells–Kuiper invariant for a family of homotopy spheres and proves it is nonzero for suitable choices in the stated infinite set of dimensions, hence those spheres are exotic and admit strictly positive sectional curvature.
- **Assumptions and setting:** The construction uses particular quotients of S³×S³ bundles, indexed by a representation and an equivariant map; the theorem covers n ≡ 3 (mod 4), n ≥ 11, excluding n ≡ 255 (mod 256).
- **Caveat:** The dimension congruence exclusions are real limits of the theorem as stated; the computational calculations and archived outputs support parts of the argument but do not make this a classification of all positively curved manifolds.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, announcement batch 2026-10-23
- **Themes:** Geometry & topology

- **Authors:** Samuel J. Ferguson, Joshua Iosevich
- **arXiv:** `2610.30712v1` (published 2026-10-22 04:30:10 UTC; categories: `math.CO`)

- **Claimed result:** The authors prove the conjecture for F₂⁸ and F₁₁³; in F₂⁹ they show every tile is spectral and every spectral set has power-of-two size, with all spectral cardinalities except 64 shown to tile, alongside further reductions.
- **Assumptions and setting:** All main statements concern finite vector spaces over the specified prime fields; the equivalence is between translational tiling and being a spectral set, with supporting finite computations described in appendices.
- **Caveat:** The abstract leaves the cardinality-64 case in F₂⁹ unresolved and does not settle Fuglede's conjecture across other dimensions or fields.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, announcement batch 2026-10-23

### 6. [Conway's thrackle conjecture: a new upper bound of 4/3 n](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31589)
- **Abstract:** The author proves that every thrackle on n ≥ 4 vertices has at most ⌊4(n−1)/3⌋ edges, combining a computer-assisted check through 12 vertices with a discharging argument on suitable embeddings.
- **Authors:** Dániel Gábor Simon
- **arXiv:** `2610.31589v1` (published 2026-10-22 17:23:50 UTC; categories: `math.CO`, `math.GN`, `math.GT`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** The result improves a sequence of published bounds on a classical graph-drawing problem and connects combinatorics with general and geometric topology; its proof has a concrete small-instance computational component.
- **Claimed result:** For any thrackleable finite simple graph with n ≥ 4 vertices and m edges, the author proves m ≤ ⌊4(n−1)/3⌋.
- **Assumptions and setting:** A thrackle is a plane drawing in which every pair of edges meets exactly once, at a shared endpoint or a proper crossing; the theorem is an upper bound for finite simple thrackleable graphs.
- **Caveat:** Conway's conjectured stronger bound m ≤ n remains open; the paper's proof incorporates a computer-assisted verification for graphs on at most 12 vertices.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, announcement batch 2026-10-23
- **Themes:** Combinatorics & discrete mathematics; Geometry & topology

## Open Problems and Research Directions

- Open problem stated in the thrackle paper: Conway's stronger m ≤ n conjecture remains unresolved after the new ⌊4(n−1)/3⌋ bound (Simon). Synthesis direction: look for structural conditions that improve the discharging argument or characterize families close to the new bound.
- Open cases stated in the Fuglede abstract: the 64-element spectral-set case in F₂⁹ remains unresolved, as do many other field/dimension combinations (Ferguson–Iosevich). Synthesis direction: use the supplied classifications and verification code to test whether the remaining case admits a complementary tiling construction or obstruction.
- The Simon conjecture is claimed to be refuted, rather than left open, by Becker–Yang. The immediate evidence-tied direction is independent verification of the LLM-assisted construction; a further mathematical boundary question is whether analogous counterexamples persist at or beyond the stated σ < 1/3 range.
- Synthesis direction for critical KPZ: test whether the same limiting field and renormalised Cole–Hopf description persist for other microscopic approximations or disorder assumptions; the current theorem is tied to the paper's Gaussian-polymer/SHF setting (Berger et al.).
- Synthesis direction for positive-curvature geometry: investigate whether the excluded congruence classes in Galaz-García's construction reflect a genuine obstruction or only the limits of the invariant calculation. The paper does not settle positive curvature for all exotic spheres.
- Synthesis direction for flops: examine whether the methods or contraction-algebra characterisation extend beyond smooth simple threefold flops (Brown–Wemyss); the abstract's theorem does not cover those broader cases.
