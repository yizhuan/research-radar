### 1. [Proof of the Erdős-Szemerédi sunflower conjecture](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.26686)
- **Abstract:** The authors prove the Erdős-Szemerédi conjecture: for every fixed number of petals k≥3, a family of subsets of [n] containing no k-sunflower has size at most c_k^n for some c_k<2.[2]
- **Authors:** Matija Bucić, Fankang He, Hyunwoo Lee
- **arXiv:** `2610.26686v1` (published 2026-10-19 16:32:32 UTC; categories: `math.CO`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** Highest editorial placement because the paper claims a resolution for every k≥3, a long-standing extremal-combinatorics conjecture; the full text also gives a quantitative exponential bound. This is result-significance evidence, not a popularity measure.[2][3]
- **Claimed result:** Theorem 1 states an exponentially improved upper bound below 2^n for sunflower-free set families for each k≥3; the authors reduce the set problem to a vector sunflower bound proved with a polynomial-method dimension argument.[3]
- **Assumptions and setting:** Fixed integer k≥3 and n-element ground set; the family is assumed to contain no k sets whose pairwise intersections all coincide.[2][3]
- **Caveat:** The paper’s abstract and theorem are author claims in a newly posted preprint; this summary has not independently checked the proof. The known lower bounds discussed in the paper leave room to investigate the sharp exponential base.[3]
- **Announcement type:** new submission, 20 October 2026 batch
- **Themes:** Combinatorics & discrete mathematics

### 2. [Isolated zeros of the Riemann zeta function and zeros of linear combinations of Dirichlet L-functions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.26841)
- **Abstract:** The paper proves unconditional positive proportions of isolated Riemann-zeta zeros and moderate consecutive gaps, then applies an unconditional refinement of the Bombieri–Hejhal method to improve a lower bound for critical-line zeros of certain two-term Dirichlet L-function combinations.[4]
- **Authors:** Youness Lamzouri
- **arXiv:** `2610.26841v1` (published 2026-10-19 17:58:53 UTC; categories: `math.NT`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** Strong quantitative claimed advances in analytic number theory, including a marked improvement over the prior unconditional proportion reported in the abstract; ranked by claimed result and scope, not citation counts.[4][5]
- **Claimed result:** The paper proves that at least 50.875% of nontrivial zeta zeros are 1/6-isolated in the limiting lower-proportion sense, obtains a proportion above 13/20 for a sufficiently small isolation constant, and raises the stated lower bound for critical-line zeros of qualifying two-term Dirichlet L-function combinations to 31%.[4][5]
- **Assumptions and setting:** The 31% application concerns two distinct primitive Dirichlet characters with the same conductor and parity and nonzero real coefficients; isolation is measured relative to the average local zero spacing.[4][5]
- **Caveat:** The 13/20 proportion requires the isolation constant to be sufficiently small; the 31% statement applies only to the specified character pairs and linear combinations.[4]
- **Announcement type:** new submission, 20 October 2026 batch
- **Themes:** Algebra & number theory

### 3. [Tight lower bounds for Lipschitz geodesically convex optimization on hyperbolic space](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.26815)
- **Abstract:** The author proves a dimension-independent first-order query lower bound matching the known Riemannian-subgradient upper bound in its dependence on curvature and target accuracy.[6]
- **Authors:** Christopher Criscitiello
- **arXiv:** `2610.26815v1` (published 2026-10-19 17:51:34 UTC; categories: `math.OC`, `math.DG`, `math.NA`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** The abstract gives a sharp, cross-listed complexity result, and the paper text supplies the algorithm model and matching rate; ranked for technical sharpness and the bridge between optimization and geometry.[6][7]
- **Claimed result:** For 0<ε≤2^-9, the paper establishes worst-case query complexity Θ(ζ_r/ε²), where ζ_r=r/tanh(r), for deterministic first-order methods under its dimension-independent hyperbolic optimization model.[7]
- **Assumptions and setting:** Hyperbolic space of curvature −1; globally geodesically convex, M-Lipschitz functions with a global minimizer in a radius-r ball; the oracle returns exact function values and subgradients, and the lower-bound model allows dimension to grow with the query horizon.[6][7]
- **Caveat:** A concurrent arXiv paper posted about a week earlier is reported by this paper to obtain the same tight asymptotic complexity; the present work’s contribution is a distinct construction handling arbitrary deterministic queries.[7]
- **Announcement type:** new submission, 20 October 2026 batch
- **Themes:** Optimization & control; Geometry & topology; Numerical analysis & computation

### 4. [Uniform convergence of canonical discrete uniformization on equilateral approximations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.26798)
- **Abstract:** The authors prove uniform convergence of canonical discrete uniformization maps on increasingly fine equilateral polygonal-disk approximations to the normalized Riemann map, including convergence on the closed reference triangle.[8]
- **Authors:** Aijin Lin, Chunlei Liu
- **arXiv:** `2610.26798v1` (published 2026-10-19 17:44:20 UTC; categories: `math.GT`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** A concrete convergence theorem that the authors say settles a named conjecture for the original Delaunay-flip construction; selected for geometric analysis significance rather than attention metrics.[8]
- **Claimed result:** The paper proves Conjecture 7.3 of Luo, Sun, and Wu for the original construction, using compactness, marked lattice limits, estimates at logarithmic cusp scales, an envelope comparison, and a local affine-energy argument.[8]
- **Assumptions and setting:** Any sequence of equilateral polygonal disks with mesh tending to zero and marked Jordan boundaries converging uniformly; the limiting map is the normalized Riemann map.[8]
- **Caveat:** The theorem is stated for this specific equilateral approximation and boundary-convergence setting; broader discretizations are not covered by the abstract.[8]
- **Announcement type:** new submission, 20 October 2026 batch
- **Themes:** Geometry & topology

### 5. [Small subgraphs of large minimum degree](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.26763)
- **Abstract:** The authors resolve a 1991 extremal-graph problem up to polylogarithmic factors for every even minimum-degree target s, and give a related tight-order extremal-family result that also covers odd s>3.[9]
- **Authors:** Zach Hunter, Barnabás Janzer, Oliver Janzer, Benny Sudakov
- **arXiv:** `2610.26763v1` (published 2026-10-19 17:21:53 UTC; categories: `math.CO`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** A stated resolution of a longstanding problem with explicit bounds and a companion result extending to odd parameters; placement reflects the claimed advance, not observed readership.[9]
- **Claimed result:** For even s≥4 and n-vertex graphs with minimum degree d≥s, every such graph contains a subgraph with minimum degree at least s on O_s(max{n d^{-s/(s-2)}(log n)^180, log n}) vertices; a second theorem gives ex(n,H)=O(n^{2-2/s+ε}) for an appropriate finite family H for each positive integer s and ε>0.[9]
- **Assumptions and setting:** Finite simple-graph extremal setting; the first bound requires even s≥4 and d≥s, while the second applies to all positive integers s and any ε>0.[9]
- **Caveat:** The first theorem’s polylogarithmic factor has exponent 180 and covers even s only; the paper describes the first problem as resolved up to polylogarithmic factors, not with an exact bound.[9]
- **Announcement type:** new submission, 20 October 2026 batch
- **Themes:** Combinatorics & discrete mathematics

### 6. [Convergence of the critical probabilities for stopping near the top of a random walk](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.26670)
- **Abstract:** The author proves Allaart’s conjecture that the critical probabilities in a semi-best stopping problem for a Bernoulli random walk converge to one half.[10]
- **Authors:** José Ángel Islas
- **arXiv:** `2610.26670v1` (published 2026-10-19 16:21:42 UTC; categories: `math.PR`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** The abstract explicitly identifies a resolved conjecture and gives the limiting value; its compact, self-contained probability result is included as a notable result, not as a measured attention leader.[10]
- **Claimed result:** For the stated semi-best selection problem, the sequence of critical probabilities p_n tends to 1/2; for each fixed p<1/2, the stopping and continuation values converge to the expressions in the abstract, which imply eventual optimality of stopping in the critical state.[10]
- **Assumptions and setting:** Bernoulli random walk with up-step probability p and down-step probability 1-p; the stopping time is chosen by horizon N to maximize the probability of stopping within one unit of the walk’s running maximum.[10]
- **Caveat:** The result addresses this specific stopping objective and its critical state; the abstract does not provide a rate of convergence for p_n.[10]
- **Announcement type:** new submission, 20 October 2026 batch
- **Themes:** Probability & statistics

### 7. [Gradient descent reaches arbitrary accuracy in a fixed number of large steps on multiclass logistic regression](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.26748)
- **Abstract:** For linearly separable data, the authors show that gradient descent can reach any prescribed optimality gap in a number of iterations independent of that gap, provided the step size is chosen sufficiently large as a function of the target accuracy and data.[11]
- **Authors:** Andreea-Alexandra Musat, Nicolas Boumal
- **arXiv:** `2610.26748v1` (published 2026-10-19 17:14:12 UTC; categories: `math.OC`, `cs.LG`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** The result connects optimization theory to multiclass learning and makes a nonstandard iteration-complexity claim; the explicit data and step-size dependence is why it is notable, rather than any measured popularity.[11]
- **Claimed result:** The authors establish an iteration count T independent of ε for attaining ε optimality gap, with T depending on data properties such as sample count and margin, while the required step size η depends on ε and, in dimension at least three, another data quantity.[11]
- **Assumptions and setting:** Multiclass logistic-regression loss on a linearly separable dataset; sufficiently large, target-dependent step size and the paper’s specified data conditions.[11]
- **Caveat:** “Fixed number” does not mean a fixed step size independent of accuracy: η must grow or otherwise be selected as a function of ε and dataset properties.[11]
- **Announcement type:** new submission, 20 October 2026 batch
- **Themes:** Optimization & control; Machine learning theory

- **Authors:** Prakash Belkale, Najmuddin Fakhruddin
- **arXiv:** `2610.26774v1` (published 2026-10-19 17:29:27 UTC; categories: `math.AG`, `math.CO`, `math.QA`, `math.RT`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** Its four-way mathematical cross-listing and an explicit conjectural bridge across motives, Hodge theory, and quantum-group representations make it a notable research-direction paper; it is ranked below papers claiming completed theorems.[12]
- **Claimed result:** The paper proposes relationships between KZ-motive weight filtrations and tilting modules plus the Jantzen and Andersen filtrations, and gives evidence rather than claiming a complete proof of those conjectures.[12]
- **Assumptions and setting:** KZ connections associated with finite-dimensional irreducible representations of a simple Lie algebra at rational parameter, where the resulting local systems admit the stated motivic realizations.[12]
- **Caveat:** The central relationships are explicitly conjectural; the abstract reports evidence, not a proved identification of the filtrations.[12]
- **Announcement type:** new submission, 20 October 2026 batch
- **Themes:** Algebra & number theory; Geometry & topology

## Open Problems and Research Directions

- **Sunflower bounds:** The paper gives a universal upper bound below 2^n, while its full text discusses lower bounds with a different exponential base. A natural follow-up is to narrow that gap and determine the best dependence on k.[3]
- **Zeta zeros:** The paper obtains explicit positive proportions for isolated zeros and certain strings of moderate gaps. Possible extensions include improving constants or establishing positive proportions for longer strings; these are proposed directions, not claims made by the abstract.[4][5]
- **Hyperbolic optimization:** The stated matching lower and upper rates use a deterministic first-order model. Test whether comparable curvature dependence holds for randomized algorithms or under additional smoothness, while accounting for the concurrent result noted by the author.[7]
- **KZ motives:** The authors’ conjectures invite precise comparisons between motivic weights and the tilting-module, Jantzen, and Andersen filtrations in additional Lie types or parameter regimes.[12]
- **Other boundaries:** The uniformization theorem suggests testing weaker boundary convergence or other mesh geometries; the graph bounds suggest reducing the polylogarithmic loss and extending the sharp small-subgraph estimate beyond even s; and the stopping result leaves the rate p_n→1/2 open in the abstract.[8][9][10]
