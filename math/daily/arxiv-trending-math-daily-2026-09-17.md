### 1. [An Exact Strip Observable and the Half-Plane One-Arm Probability for Critical Bond Percolation on the Square Lattice](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18421)
- **Abstract:** The paper proves the half-plane one-arm exponent for critical Bernoulli bond percolation on the square lattice by deriving an exact strip observable and combining it with RSW, a finite local modification, and quasi-multiplicativity.
- **Authors:** Wang Zhou
- **arXiv:** `2609.18421v1` (published 2026-09-16 10:13 UTC; categories: `math.PR`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** Concrete exponent result in a central probability model; the abstract states an explicit asymptotic $(r/R)^{1/3}$; no verified Semantic Scholar citation or influential-citation count was available at snapshot time, so the ranking reflects mathematical specificity and likely cross-interest rather than measured readership.
- **Claimed result:** The authors claim, uniformly for $1\le r<R$, $\mathbf P_{1/2}(A_1^+(r,R))\asymp (r/R)^{1/3}$, after establishing the exact strip formula from deletion relations.
- **Assumptions and setting:** Critical Bernoulli bond percolation on the square lattice; half-plane one-arm event; the proof uses the qKZ expression at the homogeneous point, RSW, local modification, and one-arm quasi-multiplicativity.
- **Caveat:** This is a v1 arXiv preprint; the abstract reports the theorem and proof ingredients, but not a referee-verified proof assessment.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, 2026-09-16 batch
- **Themes:** Probability & statistics; Mathematical physics

### 2. [Local Matrix Muckenhoupt Weights and Quantitative Weighted Inequalities Achieving Global Best Known Exponents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19597)
- **Abstract:** The paper develops real-variable properties of local matrix Muckenhoupt weights and proves quantitative bounds for local maximal, fractional-integral, Haar-square-function, and Calderón–Zygmund operators, including a Schrödinger-operator Riesz-transform application.
- **Authors:** Tuomas Hytönen, Dachun Yang, Wen Yuan, Mingdong Zhang
- **arXiv:** `2609.19597v1` (published 2026-09-17 02:22 UTC; categories: `math.FA`, `math.AP`, `math.CA`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** Three mathematics categories, sharpness/global-best-exponent language, and several operator applications make this unusually broad within the batch; no verified Semantic Scholar citation or influential-citation count was available, so measured momentum is unavailable.
- **Claimed result:** The authors claim sharp quantitative bounds for local maximal operators when $p\in(1,2]$ and bounds for local fractional integrals matching the global best-known exponents; they also obtain a local $L^p(W)$ bound for a Schrödinger Riesz transform, equal at $p=2$ to $[W]_{\mathscr A^{\mathrm{loc}}_2(r)}^{3/2}$.
- **Assumptions and setting:** Local matrix weights and local matrix-weighted Lebesgue spaces; operator classes include local fractional integrals, local Haar square functions, Calderón–Zygmund operators with exponential decay, and Schrödinger operators $-\Delta+m^2I$ for sufficiently large $m$.
- **Caveat:** The sharpness statement is explicitly limited by operator and parameter regime; the abstract does not establish that every displayed bound is globally sharp.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, 2026-09-17 batch
- **Themes:** Analysis & PDE; Numerical analysis & computation

### 3. [Ordered matchings versus triangles via pseudorandom triangle-free graphs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19632)
- **Abstract:** For ordered multicolor Ramsey numbers, the paper proves that a uniformly random ordered matching with interval chromatic number two almost surely has an improved lower bound against triangles, using pseudorandom triangle-free graphs and permutation-avoidance estimates.
- **Authors:** Wen Chen, Qizhong Lin, Chunlin You
- **arXiv:** `2609.19632v1` (published 2026-09-17 03:25 UTC; categories: `math.CO`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** The abstract reports a concrete asymptotic improvement over two named previous bounds; Semantic Scholar reported 0 citations and 0 influential citations at snapshot time, as expected for a new submission, so placement is based on the explicit quantitative advance rather than attention.
- **Claimed result:** Almost surely, $\rt(K_3,M)=\Omega\!\left(n^{4/3}/(\log n)^{1/3}\right)$, improving the earlier $\Omega((n/\log n)^{5/4})$ lower bound and the general existential bound by a logarithmic factor.
- **Assumptions and setting:** Ordered edge-colorings of the naturally ordered complete graph; $M$ is a uniformly random ordered matching on $n$ vertices with interval chromatic number two.
- **Caveat:** The result is asymptotic and probabilistic; it concerns the specified random matching model, not every ordered matching.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, 2026-09-17 batch
- **Themes:** Combinatorics & discrete mathematics

### 4. [Asymptotic infinitesimal freeness of covariance matrices](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18187)
- **Abstract:** The paper derives a first-order $1/n$ expansion for mixed moments of independent covariance matrices with independent centered, variance-one complex entries and uses it to prove asymptotic and infinitesimal freeness under a fourth-moment condition.
- **Authors:** Daniel Munoz George, Pei-Lun Tseng
- **arXiv:** `2609.18187v1` (published 2026-09-16 06:25 UTC; categories: `math.PR`, `math.CO`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** Cross-listed probability/combinatorics, an explicit finite-size expansion, and a generalization beyond the complex Gaussian Wishart setting; no verified Semantic Scholar citation or influential-citation count was available at snapshot time.
- **Claimed result:** The authors claim explicit formulas for ordinary and infinitesimal moments of several possibly repeated covariance matrices and asymptotic/infinitesimal freeness of independent covariance matrices under a fourth-moment assumption.
- **Assumptions and setting:** $M=n^{-1}XX^*$ with independent complex entries satisfying mean zero and unit second moment; the freeness application assumes a fourth moment condition.
- **Caveat:** The expansion and freeness conclusions depend on the stated moment assumptions; the abstract does not claim universality under arbitrary entry distributions.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, 2026-09-16 batch
- **Themes:** Probability & statistics; Combinatorics & discrete mathematics

- **Sharp asymptotics and exponent improvement:** Zhou proves the $1/3$ half-plane one-arm exponent, while Hytönen–Yang–Yuan–Zhang target sharp or globally best-known weighted-operator exponents. These are isolated but strong quantitative signals rather than evidence of a field-wide shift.

- **Genericity and rigidity in infinite-dimensional settings:** Ishiki studies $G_\delta$ structure and density of special metrics, connecting metric geometry with descriptive/topological methods.

## Open Problems and Research Directions

- **Percolation:** The selected abstract establishes the half-plane one-arm exponent; a natural author- and field-linked follow-up is to test whether the exact-observable method extends to other boundary geometries, lattices, or multi-arm events. This is a proposed direction, not a claim made in the abstract. [2609.18421]
- **Weighted analysis:** Determine which local matrix-weight bounds are genuinely sharp outside the stated $p\in(1,2]$ and Schrödinger-operator regimes, and whether the extension and scale-lifting properties yield new endpoint estimates. [2609.19597]
- **Ordered Ramsey theory:** The improved random-matching lower bound leaves the corresponding upper bound and the gap to the best possible order open; refining the pseudorandom construction or the permutation-avoidance step is a concrete direction. [2609.19632]
- **Infinitesimal freeness:** Extend the covariance-matrix $1/n$ expansion beyond the fourth-moment condition or quantify the error uniformly for broader repeated-matrix patterns. [2609.18187]
- **Rigid metrics:** Investigate whether the density/$G_\delta$ conclusions persist under weaker topological hypotheses or in non-Polish settings. [2609.19773]
