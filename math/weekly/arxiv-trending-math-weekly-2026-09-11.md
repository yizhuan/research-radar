## Top papers (ranked)

### 1. [A Complete Resolution of Forsythe's Conjecture for Restarted Conjugate Gradients](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04659)
- **Abstract:** The paper classifies exactly when restarted conjugate-gradient residual directions converge in the finite-dimensional symmetric positive-definite setting, proving the conjecture for restart lengths 1–3 and constructing counterexamples for every length at least 4.
- **Authors:** Matthew J. Colbrook, George Stepaniants, Alex Townsend
- **arXiv:** `2609.04659v2` (published 2026-09-04 02:46 UTC; categories: `math.NA`, `math.DS`, `math.OC`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** A nearly six-decade-old conjecture is claimed to be completely classified, with a sharp threshold, explicit counterexamples, and formal Lean verification; the paper was independently surfaced by web search and revised during the window. Semantic Scholar citation data was rate-limited at snapshot time, so no citation momentum is claimed.
- **Claimed result:** For restart lengths s=1,2,3 the universal convergence conclusion holds; for every s≥4, a diagonal positive-definite counterexample exists. The authors also report formal verification of the classification in Lean.
- **Assumptions and setting:** Exact-arithmetic restarted conjugate-gradient iteration on finite-dimensional real symmetric positive-definite problems; the conclusion concerns separate convergence of even and odd normalized residual directions.
- **Caveat:** This is an arXiv preprint; the counterexample and formal-verification claims still require expert scrutiny, and the result does not automatically cover floating-point implementations.
- **Announcement type:** revision, announcement-batch 2026-09-07
- **Themes:** Optimization & control; Numerical analysis & computation; Dynamical systems

### 2. [A unified family of counterexamples to Batyrev's non-negativity conjecture on stringy Hodge numbers](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.06829)
- **Abstract:** The paper places known counterexamples to Batyrev's non-negativity conjecture into a three-parameter family, derives a master formula for their stringy Hodge numbers, and classifies the counterexample behavior in a broad range of parameters.
- **Authors:** Dimitrios I. Dais
- **arXiv:** `2609.06829v2` (published 2026-09-06 20:56 UTC; categories: `math.AG`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** It unifies the sharp dimension-threshold counterexamples reported in recent work and adds a parameterized classification; Semantic Scholar listed 0 citations and 0 influential citations at snapshot time, consistent with the paper's very recent age.
- **Claimed result:** After blowing up the singular linear locus, the author derives a structure formula separating nonnegative contributions from the term responsible for possible negativity, and gives a complete classification for 0≤m≤d−1, d≥3, n≥1 with stated exceptions.
- **Assumptions and setting:** Singular projective hypersurfaces with a linear singular locus, their log resolutions, Hodge–Deligne polynomials, and stringy Hodge numbers; the classification is parameter-dependent.
- **Caveat:** The paper builds on the recently established dimension threshold and its formulas are specialized to the stated family; it does not restore non-negativity outside that family.
- **Announcement type:** revision, announcement-batch 2026-09-09
- **Themes:** Algebra & number theory; Geometry & topology

### 3. [Unimodality shenanigans in Ehrhart theory](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.10513)
- **Abstract:** The paper constructs counterexamples to Stanley's unimodality conjecture for h*-polynomials of IDP polytopes and also disproves several related log-concavity conjectures.
- **Authors:** Luis Ferroni
- **arXiv:** `2609.10513v1` (published 2026-09-09 17:48 UTC; categories: `math.CO`, `math.AC`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** It simultaneously targets a four-decade-old conjecture and multiple related conjectures, with the abstract identifying smooth counterexamples; the arXiv record was independently returned in web search. Semantic Scholar reported 0 citations and 0 influential citations at snapshot time.
- **Claimed result:** A class built from Cayley sums of rectangular prisms yields nonunimodal and non-log-concave examples, including smooth examples for the unimodality conjecture and very ample examples with non-log-concave interior Ehrhart series.
- **Assumptions and setting:** Integer decomposition property (IDP) polytopes, h*-polynomials, Gorenstein and very ample polytopes, Ehrhart series, and regular unimodular flag triangulations.
- **Caveat:** The abstract states existence results; assessing the constructions and their exact combinatorial properties requires reading the examples and proofs.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, announcement-batch 2026-09-09
- **Themes:** Combinatorics & discrete mathematics; Algebra & number theory

### 4. [Sharp Hessian integrability for fully nonlinear elliptic supersolutions in low dimensions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.06817)
- **Abstract:** The paper claims the sharp planar W^{2,ε} integrability exponent for viscosity supersolutions of uniformly elliptic fully nonlinear equations and extends the same mechanism to a substantial three-dimensional regime.
- **Authors:** Thialita M. Nascimento, Eduardo V. Teixeira
- **arXiv:** `2609.06817v1` (published 2026-09-06 20:19 UTC; categories: `math.AP`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** The abstract explicitly claims resolution of the planar Armstrong–Silvestre–Smart conjecture and gives exact exponents; the result connects a long-standing regularity problem to a new contact-geometry mechanism. Citation data was unavailable because Semantic Scholar rate-limited the request.
- **Claimed result:** With ellipticity ratio κ=Λ/λ, the optimal planar exponent is ε₂(κ)=2/(κ+1); in dimension three the paper obtains ε₃(κ)=3/(2κ+1) for 1≤κ≤4 and a lower bound beyond that range.
- **Assumptions and setting:** Viscosity supersolutions of fully nonlinear uniformly elliptic equations; the exact result depends on dimension and ellipticity ratio, with a remaining gap in part of dimension three.
- **Caveat:** The three-dimensional statement is not complete for all κ, and the sharpness claim is a preprint claim pending independent verification.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, announcement-batch 2026-09-07
- **Themes:** Analysis & PDE

### 5. [A sharp covering theorem and Solyanik estimates for Euclidean balls](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.07880)
- **Abstract:** The paper proves a conjectured low-multiplicity covering theorem for dilated Euclidean balls and derives optimal asymptotics for the uncentered Hardy–Littlewood maximal operator.
- **Authors:** Mayukh Mukherjee
- **arXiv:** `2609.07880v1` (published 2026-09-07 18:44 UTC; categories: `math.CA`, `math.MG`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** The abstract reports two sharp conjecture-level consequences, and the arXiv abstract/PDF/HTML records were independently returned by web search. Citation data was unavailable because Semantic Scholar rate-limited the request.
- **Claimed result:** A finite family of balls admits a subfamily whose (1+δ)-dilations cover the union while the undilated multiplicity is O(δ^{-(n−1)/2}); this yields the optimal Solyanik asymptotic and related weak-(1,1), Lp, and Fefferman–Stein bounds.
- **Assumptions and setting:** Euclidean balls in R^n, n≥2, 0<δ<1/2, the uncentered Hardy–Littlewood maximal operator, and modified operators over Radon measures.
- **Caveat:** The constants and asymptotic equivalences are dimension-dependent; applications outside Euclidean-ball geometries are not established by the abstract.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, announcement-batch 2026-09-07
- **Themes:** Analysis & PDE; Geometry & topology

### 6. [A counterexample to Kenig's conjecture for the Laplace double-layer operator](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.07947)
- **Abstract:** The paper constructs a simply connected planar Lipschitz domain whose Laplace double-layer operator has essential spectral radius greater than 1/2, disproving Kenig's conjecture on Neumann-series convergence.
- **Authors:** Matthew J. Colbrook, Siavash Sadeghi
- **arXiv:** `2609.07947v1` (published 2026-09-07 19:59 UTC; categories: `math.AP`, `math-ph`, `math.NA`, `math.SP`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** It is a direct counterexample to a 1994 conjecture, uses a computer-assisted rigorous certificate, and is cross-listed across four mathematical areas; the paper was independently returned by web search. Citation data was unavailable because Semantic Scholar rate-limited the request.
- **Claimed result:** For t>1/2 sufficiently close to 1/2, the construction has ±it in the Fredholm essential spectrum, implying essential spectral radius strictly above 1/2.
- **Assumptions and setting:** Laplace double-layer operators on arclength L2 over bounded simply connected planar Lipschitz domains and periodic Lipschitz graphs.
- **Caveat:** The construction is specialized and computer-assisted; the implications for particular boundary-integral discretizations or smoother domains are not established here.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, announcement-batch 2026-09-07
- **Themes:** Analysis & PDE; Numerical analysis & computation; Mathematical physics

### 7. [A Counterexample to a Conjecture of Liu and Qian and a Refined Inverse Theorem for Restricted Sumsets in Z_p](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.10148)
- **Abstract:** The paper refutes a proposed inverse theorem for critical restricted sumsets in Z_p and proves a refined inverse theorem after imposing the natural condition |A|+|B|≤p−1.
- **Authors:** Jiantao Li, Xinyi Liang
- **arXiv:** `2609.10148v1` (published 2026-09-09 13:27 UTC; categories: `math.NT`, `math.RA`)
- **Evidence for ranking:** It gives both a concrete counterexample and a replacement theorem for a problem open since 1996, with cross-listing between number theory and algebra. Citation data was unavailable because Semantic Scholar rate-limited the request.
- **Claimed result:** The Liu–Qian conjecture fails at the boundary |A|+|B|=p; under |A|+|B|≤p−1, the authors state and prove a refined inverse theorem using prior work plus a new counting argument.
- **Assumptions and setting:** Nonempty subsets A,B of the prime cyclic group Z_p with unequal sizes; restricted sumsets exclude equal summands.
- **Caveat:** The refined theorem deliberately excludes the boundary case that produces the counterexample, so the full inverse problem remains only partially resolved.
- **Announcement type:** new submission, announcement-batch 2026-09-09
- **Themes:** Algebra & number theory; Combinatorics & discrete mathematics

- Counterexamples paired with repaired statements: Ehrhart theory, restricted sumsets, stringy Hodge numbers, and the Morley tetrahedron replace false universal claims with narrower classifications or new conjectures (2609.10513, 2609.10148, 2609.06829, 2609.06553).

## Research opportunities

### Potential research areas

- Extend the restarted-CG classification to finite precision, preconditioning, or nonsymmetric Krylov methods, building on 2609.04659.
- Determine whether the sharp Hessian-integrability mechanism extends through the remaining three-dimensional ellipticity range and to higher dimensions, following 2609.06817.
- Transfer the sharp covering and Solyanik estimates from Euclidean balls to other geometries or bases, motivated by 2609.07880.

### Unsolved problems

- The general Morley tetrahedron conjecture proposed in 2609.06553 remains open.
- The restricted-sumset inverse problem at the boundary |A|+|B|=p is not covered by the refined theorem in 2609.10148.
- The dimension-three Hessian exponent beyond κ=4 remains a gap in the result reported by 2609.06817.

### Potential research directions

- Test whether the counterexample families in 2609.06829 and 2609.10513 admit broader deformation or classification principles beyond their stated parameter families.
- Analyze whether the spectral obstruction in 2609.07947 persists under smoother boundaries, alternative layer-potential norms, or discretization schemes.
